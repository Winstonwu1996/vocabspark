## §1 Pre-1000 知识图景: 古典期 Maya 与跨大洋平行文明

### 1.1 Mesoamerica 古典期 Maya (250-900 CE)

**CN**: 公元 250 年到 900 年, 中美洲低地雨林里有十几座玛雅城邦同时运转。最大的几个 — Tikal (蒂卡尔, 今危地马拉北部)、Palenque (帕伦克, 今墨西哥 Chiapas)、Calakmul (卡拉克穆尔, 今墨西哥 Campeche)、Copán (科潘, 今洪都拉斯西部) — 各自有几万到十几万人口, 中央金字塔神庙超过六十米高。这些城邦不是一个统一帝国, 而是像古希腊城邦一样彼此结盟、通婚、互相打仗。

**EN**: From 250 to 900 CE, the Maya lowlands hosted dozens of city-states at the same time. The biggest ones — Tikal in northern Guatemala, Palenque in Mexican Chiapas, Calakmul in Campeche, and Copán in western Honduras — each held tens of thousands of people. Their pyramid-temples rose over sixty meters. These city-states were not one unified empire. They were more like ancient Greek poleis: allied, intermarried, and at war with each other.

**CN**: 玛雅 古典期 不是技术原始的部落。他们有 logosyllabic 文字 (词符 + 音节合一文字), 一个字符既可以表示一个完整的词, 也可以表示一个音节 — 跟同期的中国汉字 + 日本假名 复合体系结构上类似。他们有数学零的概念 (比印度数学家 Brahmagupta 628 年正式定义还早几个世纪), 一套 Long Count (长纪年) 历法可以精确到一天, 周期跨越 5125 年。

**EN**: Classic Maya was not a primitive tribal culture. They had a logosyllabic script — one sign could be a whole word, or just a syllable, like Chinese characters mixed with Japanese kana. They used the concept of zero in math, centuries before Brahmagupta formally defined it in India in 628 CE. Their Long Count calendar tracked single days across cycles of 5,125 years.

### 1.2 古典期崩 750-900 — careful framing

**CN**: 大约 750 年开始到 900 年, 玛雅低地南部的大城邦一座接一座停止建造新的纪念碑, 王室刻石断绝, 人口大量南迁北移。这就是所谓的「玛雅古典期崩溃」(Classic Maya Collapse)。但这里要 careful framing: 这不是一次神秘消失, 也不是单一原因。

**EN**: Around 750 to 900 CE, the southern lowland Maya cities one by one stopped carving new royal monuments. Population shifted south and north. This is called the Classic Maya Collapse. But careful framing matters here — it was not a mysterious disappearance, and not from one single cause.

**CN**: 学界目前的综合解释 (Marina Frangos《The Maya Collapse》2010 综述) 是多因素叠加: (1) 8 世纪到 9 世纪的多次长期干旱, 湖泊沉积层化学分析能直接读出来; (2) 城邦之间长期高强度战争 — Tikal 跟 Calakmul 几代人打了上百年; (3) 内部权力斗争, 王室无法继续维系超大规模公共工程的合法性; (4) 农业承载力到顶 — 雨林土壤经几百年密集耕作后流失。这 4 个因素哪一个单拎出来都不致命, 但叠在一起就压垮了南部低地的城邦体制。

**EN**: The current academic synthesis (Marina Frangos, *The Maya Collapse*, 2010) is multi-causal: (1) repeated severe droughts in the 8th and 9th centuries, readable directly from lake sediment chemistry; (2) long high-intensity warfare between city-states — Tikal and Calakmul fought for over a century; (3) internal political struggle, where royal lineages could no longer justify giant public projects; (4) agricultural carrying capacity peaking — rainforest soils degraded after centuries of intensive use. No single factor alone was fatal. Together they broke the southern lowland system.

**CN**: 重要的是: 北部低地 (Yucatán 半岛) 的玛雅 没有崩溃。Chichen Itzá 这时候才刚开始崛起。所以 玛雅文明 没有「灭亡」, 只是政治重心从南部低地转移到北部 — 这是 Postclassic Maya 的开端, 也是 Lens 3 主角 Ix Mutal 几百年后出生的世界。

**EN**: One key point: the northern lowlands of the Yucatán peninsula did not collapse. Chichen Itzá was just rising at this time. So Maya civilization did not end. The political center simply moved from the southern lowlands to the north. This was the beginning of Postclassic Maya, the world that Lens 3's Ix Mutal would be born into centuries later.

### 1.3 跨大洋平行: 800 年的世界

**CN**: 800 年是个有意思的年份, 三个相距上万公里的文明同时在做大动作。中国 唐朝 长安 — 玄宗已经死了 38 年, 但长安城还是世界上最大的国际都市, 100 多万人口, 西市的胡商 (波斯 + 粟特) 用银币买卖丝绸。欧洲 — 查理曼 (Charlemagne) 这年圣诞节在罗马被教皇 利奥三世 加冕为「罗马人的皇帝」(Imperator Romanorum), 这是西罗马帝国 476 年灭亡后欧洲第一次有统一帝国号召力。中美洲 — Tikal 跟 Calakmul 的最后几代国王还在刻最后的纪念碑, 几十年后这两个超级城邦就会一起塌掉。

**EN**: 800 CE is an interesting year. Three civilizations, tens of thousands of kilometers apart, were all making big moves at once. In China, Tang dynasty Chang'an was the world's largest international city, with over a million people. Persian and Sogdian traders in the western market bought silk with silver coins. In Europe, on Christmas Day 800, Pope Leo III crowned Charlemagne as Emperor of the Romans in Rome — the first unified imperial claim in Europe since the fall of the Western Roman Empire in 476. In Mesoamerica, the last kings of Tikal and Calakmul were still carving their final monuments. Within decades, both superpower city-states would collapse together.

**CN**: 这种平行对照 不是巧合, 也不是说三个文明在「比赛」, 而是想让 Grade 7 的同学看到: 1500 年欧洲人到达美洲之前, 美洲并不是一个停滞、等待被「发现」的空白大陆。它有自己的兴衰节律, 跟旧大陆的兴衰节律差不多复杂。

**EN**: This parallel framing is not a coincidence and not a competition. It is meant to show Grade 7 readers one thing: before Europeans reached the Americas in 1500, the Americas were not a frozen, empty land waiting to be "discovered." They had their own rhythms of rise and fall, just as complex as those of the Old World.

### 1.4 South America 同期: Tiwanaku + Wari

**CN**: 大约 600 到 1000 年, 南美洲安第斯山区 (今玻利维亚 + 秘鲁) 有两个大型政体: Tiwanaku (蒂瓦纳科, 在的的喀喀湖南岸) 和 Wari (瓦里, 在今秘鲁中部高地)。这两个文明 没有文字, 但已经有 quipu (绳结) 雏形 — 用不同颜色 + 不同打结方式记录数字 + 行政信息。这套系统会一直延续到几百年后 Inca 帝国, 成为人类历史上罕见的、不靠文字而靠绳结管理千万级人口的行政体系。

**EN**: From around 600 to 1000 CE, the Andes mountains in modern Bolivia and Peru hosted two large polities: Tiwanaku, on the southern shore of Lake Titicaca, and Wari, in the central Peruvian highlands. Neither had writing. But early forms of quipu (knotted cord, 绳结) were already in use — different colors and knot types recorded numbers and administrative data. This system would last for centuries and become, in the later Inca empire, one of history's rare cases of running a state of millions of people through cord knots instead of script.

## §2 Postclassic Maya 1000-1500: 分散化, 不是衰落

### 2.1 Chichen Itzá 兴起 1000-1200

**CN**: 古典期南部崩溃后, 北部 Yucatán 半岛的 Chichen Itzá 在 10 世纪到 12 世纪成为整个玛雅地区的政治宗教中心。Chichen Itzá 的建筑风格 跟古典期南部低地 不太一样 — 出现了大量来自中部墨西哥 Toltec (托尔特克) 文明的元素: 蛇柱、武士柱、Chacmool 雕像 (仰卧人形祭石)。最有名的就是金字塔顶上的羽蛇神庙, 玛雅人叫它 Kukulkan (库库尔坎), Toltec 人叫它 Quetzalcoatl (羽蛇神, 魁札尔科亚特尔)。

**EN**: After the southern collapse, Chichen Itzá in the northern Yucatán became the political and religious center of the whole Maya region from the 10th to 12th centuries. Its architecture differed from the southern Classic style. It absorbed many elements from central Mexican Toltec culture: serpent columns, warrior pillars, and Chacmool statues (reclining stone figures). The most famous is the feathered-serpent temple on top of the great pyramid. The Maya called it Kukulkan; the Toltecs called it Quetzalcoatl.

**CN**: 这种跨文化神祇共享 在 Mesoamerica 是常态。Quetzalcoatl / Kukulkan 不是一个民族独占的神, 而是从中部高原墨西哥 Teotihuacan 时代 (~150-650 CE) 就跨越多个文明传播的 pan-Mesoamerican 神。这种共享 跟同时期欧洲基督教在地中海跨语言传播 + 伊斯兰教在中东到北非到伊比利亚跨语言传播, 在文化机制上是同一类现象。

**EN**: This kind of cross-cultural deity sharing was normal in Mesoamerica. Quetzalcoatl / Kukulkan was not the property of one ethnic group. The feathered serpent god had spread across many civilizations since the Teotihuacan era (~150-650 CE). This sharing parallels how Christianity spread across the Mediterranean in many languages, or how Islam spread from the Middle East through North Africa to Iberia. The cultural mechanism is the same kind of pattern.

### 2.2 Mayapan 1200-1450 + Chilam Balam 文本

**CN**: 12 世纪末 13 世纪初, Chichen Itzá 的政治中心地位开始下降, 接替它的是 Mayapan (玛雅潘, 也在 Yucatán 半岛)。Mayapan 跟 Chichen 不一样: 它是一座被城墙包围的紧凑城市, 大约 1.5 万到 2 万人口, 由 Cocom (科科姆) 家族联合多个贵族世家共同统治。Mayapan 的政治模式 比较像意大利文艺复兴时期的城邦寡头制 — 一个家族领头, 但实际权力由议会分享。Mayapan 在 1450 年代因为内部贵族战争而崩溃。

**EN**: At the end of the 12th century, Chichen Itzá's political dominance faded. Mayapan, also in the Yucatán, took over. Mayapan was different from Chichen — it was a compact walled city of roughly 15,000 to 20,000 people, ruled jointly by the Cocom family and several noble lineages. Its political style resembled Italian Renaissance city-state oligarchy: one family led, but the council shared real power. Mayapan collapsed in the 1450s because of an internal noble war.

**CN**: 我们怎么知道这些事? Postclassic Maya 没有像古典期那样在石头上刻很多王室纪事。但他们有口传 + 后来在 16 世纪用拉丁字母记下来的本地语言文本, 统称 Chilam Balam (奇兰巴兰之书, 「美洲豹祭司之书」)。Munro Edmonson 1986 年在 University of Texas 出版的《Heaven Born Merida and Its Destiny: The Book of Chilam Balam of Chumayel》是这套文本的权威英译之一, 里面保留了 Postclassic 玛雅人对自己几百年历史的口述记忆。

**EN**: How do we know all this? Postclassic Maya did not carve as many royal records in stone as the Classic period did. But they had oral traditions, later written down in Mayan languages using Latin letters in the 16th century. These texts are collectively called Chilam Balam, "The Books of the Jaguar Priest." Munro Edmonson's 1986 *Heaven Born Merida and Its Destiny: The Book of Chilam Balam of Chumayel* (University of Texas Press) is one authoritative English translation. It preserves how Postclassic Maya remembered their own centuries of history.

### 2.3 Yucatán 农业村落 + 妇女 weaving — Lens 3 背景

**CN**: 1450 年 Mayapan 崩溃后, Yucatán 半岛进入了几十个分散的小型城邦时代, 没有任何一个中心城市占主导。这种「分散化」状态 一直持续到 1500 年代西班牙人到达。这里要重要 careful framing: 学界 (Susan Kepecs 2003《Chichen Itza, Tula and the "Toltec" Horizons》) 强调 — Postclassic Maya 不是「衰落」, 而是「分散化 + 区域政体」。这两个词 含义完全不同。

**EN**: After Mayapan's collapse in 1450, the Yucatán entered a period of dozens of small dispersed city-states, with no dominant center. This "decentralized" pattern lasted until the Spanish arrived in the 1500s. Careful framing is critical here. Scholars like Susan Kepecs (2003, "Chichen Itza, Tula and the 'Toltec' Horizons") emphasize that Postclassic Maya was not in "decline." It was "decentralization plus regional polities." The two phrases mean very different things.

**CN**: 「衰落」(decline) 暗示文明走下坡路, 不行了; 「分散化」(decentralization) 是说政治结构从单一霸权转成多中心 — 经济、人口、技术、艺术都还在持续运转, 只是不再有一个超级首都。事实上 Postclassic Maya 时代的纺织、陶瓷、长距离贸易 (从 Yucatán 到中部墨西哥的盐 + 棉布 + 黑曜石贸易) 都很活跃。

**EN**: "Decline" suggests a civilization on a downward slope, no longer functioning. "Decentralization" means political structure shifted from single hegemony to multiple centers — while economy, population, technology, and art kept running, just without one super-capital. In fact, Postclassic Maya textile work, ceramics, and long-distance trade (salt, cotton cloth, and obsidian moving between Yucatán and central Mexico) were all very active.

**CN**: Lens 3 主角 Ix Mutal Ahaw 就是这个分散化时代后期 (~1480 出生) 的一个 Yucatán 内陆村落织女。她的世界 — 几千人的小城邦, 周围是玉米 (maize) + 豆 + 南瓜 (Three Sisters 三姐妹种植法) 农田, 妇女在家用 backstrap loom (背带织机) 织棉布, 男人在玉米田劳作或参加村落间的小规模冲突。这个世界 看起来「平静」, 但 1500 年代西班牙人很快就会到来。

**EN**: Lens 3's Ix Mutal Ahaw was born around 1480, late in this decentralized period, in an inland Yucatán village as a weaver. Her world was a small city-state of a few thousand people, surrounded by fields of maize, beans, and squash (the Three Sisters planting system). Women wove cotton cloth at home on the backstrap loom (背带织机). Men worked the maize fields or took part in small inter-village conflicts. This world looks "quiet" — but the Spanish were soon to arrive in the 1500s.

### 2.4 史料 gap: 为什么 Lens 3 必须是虚构 composite

**CN**: 这里有一个学术诚实的问题必须 careful framing。Postclassic Maya 平民妇女 — 特别是织女 — 几乎没有留下个人史料。原因有三: (1) 平民没有刻石头权力; (2) 16 世纪西班牙殖民后 大部分玛雅本土文本被烧毁 (1562 年 Diego de Landa 主教在 Maní 烧了约 27 部 Maya 抄本——今天只剩 4 部存世——以及数千件圣物), 留下来的几本主要记王室 + 祭司 + 男性贵族; (3) 即使是 Chilam Balam 这种保留口传的文本, 视角也是男性祭司视角。

**EN**: One academic-honesty issue must be carefully framed here. Postclassic Maya commoner women — especially weavers — left almost no individual records. Three reasons: (1) commoners had no right to carve stone monuments; (2) after Spanish conquest in the 16th century, most native Maya texts were burned. In 1562, Bishop Diego de Landa burned around 27 Maya codices at Maní — only 4 survive today — along with thousands of sacred objects. The few that survive mostly record kings, priests, and male nobles. (3) Even Chilam Balam, which preserves oral tradition, comes from a male priestly viewpoint.

**CN**: 所以 Lens 3 的 Ix Mutal Ahaw 是一个 composite 虚构角色 — 基于多份考古证据 (Yucatán 内陆村落墓葬、织机残片、骨骼营养分析、洗骨仪式记录) 和 Chilam Balam 文本里关于平民妇女的零散记述, 综合出来的一个「典型」生命轨迹。这跟 Lens 1 真实历史人物 Pachacuti 和 Lens 2 真实历史人物 Malintzin 性质不同 — 学生需要清楚知道这一点。

**EN**: So Lens 3's Ix Mutal Ahaw is a composite fictional character — built from many archaeological sources (Yucatán inland village burials, loom fragments, skeletal nutrition analysis, secondary burial ritual records) plus scattered references to commoner women in Chilam Balam texts. These sources together give us a "typical" life trajectory. This is different from Lens 1's real historical Pachacuti and Lens 2's real historical Malintzin. Students need to know this clearly.

## §3 North Mesoamerica → Aztec 1325 founding

### 3.1 Toltec 1000-1150 Tula

**CN**: 在 Aztec 之前, 中部墨西哥高原的霸主是 Toltec (托尔特克) 文明, 首都 Tula (图拉, 今墨西哥州 Hidalgo 区), 大约 1000 到 1150 年间 鼎盛。Toltec 不是 Aztec 直接祖先, 但 Aztec 后来大量自我建构成「Toltec 文化继承人」— 就像后来欧洲许多王朝都自称「罗马继承人」一样。Aztec 的贵族学校 calmecac (卡尔梅卡克) 教年轻贵族 Toltec 起源神话, 把自己定位成 Toltec 文明的延续者。

**EN**: Before the Aztecs, the dominant power in the central Mexican highlands was the Toltec civilization, with its capital at Tula (in modern Hidalgo state), peaking around 1000 to 1150 CE. The Toltecs were not the direct ancestors of the Aztecs. But the Aztecs later built themselves up as "heirs of Toltec culture" — much like many later European dynasties claimed to be "heirs of Rome." The Aztec elite school called calmecac (贵族学校) taught young nobles Toltec origin myths, positioning themselves as the continuation of Toltec civilization.

### 3.2 Chichimec 移民 1100-1300 — Mexica 起源

**CN**: 1150 年 Tula 崩溃后, 中部墨西哥高原进入了一个移民时代。从北方半干旱地带 (今墨西哥北部 + 美国西南部) 来的多波 Chichimec (奇奇梅克, 「狗族」, 中部墨西哥人对北方游牧民的统称) 部落南下, 进入肥沃的 Lake Texcoco (特斯科科湖) 河谷。Aztec / Mexica (墨西加, 阿兹特克的自称) 是这些 Chichimec 移民里最后到达的一支, 大约 1200 到 1300 年到达河谷。

**EN**: After Tula's collapse in 1150, the central Mexican highlands entered a migration era. From the semi-arid north (modern northern Mexico and the U.S. Southwest), waves of Chichimec ("dog people," the central Mexican label for northern nomads) tribes moved south into the fertile Lake Texcoco valley. The Aztecs, or Mexica (their own name for themselves), were the last of these Chichimec migrant groups to arrive, around 1200 to 1300 CE.

**CN**: 早期 Mexica 在河谷里 处境很差: 周围已经有 Tepanec (特帕内克)、Acolhua (阿科尔瓦)、Culhua (库尔瓦) 等已经定居几代人的城邦, 它们把好地都占了。Mexica 只能给这些城邦做雇佣兵, 或被迫迁居荒岛。

**EN**: The early Mexica had a tough time in the valley. The Tepanecs, Acolhuas, Culhuas, and other city-states had already settled for generations and taken the good land. The Mexica could only serve as mercenaries for these city-states, or get pushed onto wasteland islands.

### 3.3 1325 Tenochtitlán founding — 鹰啄蛇仙人掌

**CN**: 根据后来 Mexica 自己的起源神话 (16 世纪 Mendoza Codex 抄本记载), 1325 年, 流浪中的 Mexica 部落看到一个神兆: 在 Lake Texcoco 中央一个小岛上, 一只老鹰停在仙人掌上叼着一条蛇。他们的祖神 Huitzilopochtli (维齐洛波奇特利, 战神 + 太阳神) 早就预言过这个景象 — 看到这个景象的地方就是他们建城的地方。于是他们在这个沼泽小岛上 建立了 Tenochtitlán (特诺奇蒂特兰, Mexica 都城)。

**EN**: According to the Mexica's own later origin myth (recorded in the 16th-century Mendoza Codex), in 1325 the wandering Mexica saw an omen: on a small island in the middle of Lake Texcoco, an eagle perched on a cactus, holding a snake in its beak. Their patron god Huitzilopochtli (war god and sun god) had long ago prophesied this exact image. Where they saw it would be where they built their city. So they founded Tenochtitlán on this swampy little island.

**CN**: 这个鹰啄蛇仙人掌的图像 至今还在墨西哥国旗中央 — Mexica 起源神话直接成了现代墨西哥国家认同的核心符号。但要 careful framing: Mendoza Codex 是 1540 年代由墨西哥总督 Antonio de Mendoza 委托制作的, 写作时 Tenochtitlán 已经被西班牙人征服 20 年了。所以 1325 年的「神兆」 究竟是历史事件还是后人追溯神话, 学界 (Inga Clendinnen 1991《Aztecs: An Interpretation》Cambridge) 倾向于认为它是 Mexica 帝国期 (15 世纪后半) 的政治神话建构, 用来给后来的统治合法性背书。

**EN**: This eagle-snake-cactus image is still at the center of Mexico's flag today. The Mexica origin myth became the core symbol of modern Mexican national identity. But careful framing: the Mendoza Codex was commissioned in the 1540s by Viceroy Antonio de Mendoza, twenty years after the Spanish conquest of Tenochtitlán. So whether the 1325 omen was a real event or a later mythical retelling is debated. Scholars like Inga Clendinnen (*Aztecs: An Interpretation*, Cambridge 1991) lean toward seeing it as a political myth constructed during the imperial era (late 15th century) to legitimize later Mexica rule.

### 3.4 Aztec 联盟早期 + Triple Alliance 1428

**CN**: 14 世纪整个 100 年, Mexica 是 Tepanec 帝国的附庸, 给 Tepanec 国王 打仗、纳贡。1426 年 Tepanec 王 Tezozomoc 死, Mexica 抓住继承危机的机会。新一代 Mexica 王 Itzcoatl (伊兹科阿特尔, 在位 1427-1440) 联合 Texcoco 城邦的贵族 Nezahualcoyotl (内萨瓦尔科约特尔) 和 Tlacopan 城邦, 在 1428 年共同推翻 Tepanec, 建立 Triple Alliance (三方同盟): Tenochtitlán + Texcoco + Tlacopan。

**EN**: Through the 14th century, the Mexica were vassals of the Tepanec empire. They fought wars and paid tribute to the Tepanec king. In 1426, the Tepanec king Tezozomoc died. The Mexica seized the succession crisis. Their new king Itzcoatl (reigned 1427-1440) allied with the Texcoco prince Nezahualcoyotl and the Tlacopan city-state. Together in 1428 they overthrew the Tepanecs and formed the Triple Alliance: Tenochtitlán + Texcoco + Tlacopan.

**CN**: Triple Alliance 是 Aztec 帝国的真正起点。接下来的 90 年 (1428-1521), 这个三城同盟从中部高原扩张到墨西哥湾岸 + 太平洋岸, 控制人口超过 500 万。Itzcoatl 之后的 Moctezuma I (蒙特苏马一世, 在位 1440-1469) 是真正把同盟变成帝国的人 — 他建立了正式的纳贡体系、扩展了 Tenochtitlán 的水利工程 (堤坝 + 渠道), 让这座岛城能容纳 20 万以上人口。

**EN**: The Triple Alliance was the true beginning of the Aztec empire. Over the next 90 years (1428-1521), this three-city alliance expanded from the central highlands to both the Gulf and Pacific coasts, controlling over 5 million people. After Itzcoatl, Moctezuma I (reigned 1440-1469) turned the alliance into a true empire. He built a formal tribute system and extended Tenochtitlán's water infrastructure (dikes and canals), letting this island city hold over 200,000 people.

### 3.5 Aztec 社会结构

**CN**: Aztec 社会 不是简单的「王 + 平民」二元结构, 而是分四层:
- **Pipiltin** (皮尔廷, 贵族阶层): 世袭贵族, 上 calmecac 学校, 担任官员、祭司、高级军官。
- **Macehualtin** (马塞瓦尔廷, 平民阶层): 自由农民 + 工匠, 大部分人口属于这层, 上 telpochcalli (普通学校), 服徭役, 服兵役, 但也能通过战功上升。
- **Pochteca** (波奇特卡, 商人阶层): 一个特殊的世袭商人群体, 从事远距离贸易 (从中部墨西哥到 Yucatán 到中美洲交界), 兼任帝国情报员。
- **Tlatlacotin** (特拉特拉科廷, 奴隶阶层): 大部分是因债务、罪行、战俘而临时为奴, 不是种族奴隶制, 子女通常自由。

**EN**: Aztec society was not a simple "king and commoners" two-layer structure. It had four layers:
- **Pipiltin** (贵族, hereditary nobles): attended the calmecac elite school, served as officials, priests, and senior officers.
- **Macehualtin** (平民, commoners): free farmers and craftsmen, the largest group. Attended the telpochcalli (regular school), did labor service and military service, but could rise through war achievements.
- **Pochteca** (商人, merchants): a special hereditary merchant class doing long-distance trade from central Mexico to the Yucatán to Central America, also serving as imperial intelligence agents.
- **Tlatlacotin** (奴隶, slaves): mostly temporary slaves due to debt, crime, or war capture. Not race-based slavery. Their children were usually born free.

**CN**: 这种四层结构 在前现代世界其实很常见 — 跟同期欧洲的贵族 + 自由民 + 商人行会 + 农奴 结构, 跟同期明朝的士 + 农 + 工 + 商, 在功能上类似。Aztec 不是一个停滞、封闭的「原始社会」, 而是一个高度分化的、有阶层流动通道的复杂城邦帝国。

**EN**: This four-layer structure was actually common in the premodern world. It functioned like contemporary European nobles + freemen + merchant guilds + serfs, or contemporary Ming dynasty's scholars + farmers + artisans + merchants. Aztec society was not a frozen, closed "primitive" society. It was a highly differentiated complex city-state empire with channels for social mobility.

### 3.6 Aztec 数学、历法、文字

**CN**: Aztec 数字系统 是 vigesimal (二十进制, 基于 20), 跟玛雅一样 — 不是因为他们抄袭玛雅, 而是因为整个 Mesoamerica 文化圈共用这套数学。20 进制的来源 据信是「手指 + 脚趾合计 20」的身体计数。

**EN**: Aztec numbers used a vigesimal system (base-20, 二十进制), like the Maya. This was not because they copied each other. The entire Mesoamerican cultural sphere shared this math. The base-20 origin is thought to come from counting fingers and toes together, totaling 20.

**CN**: Aztec 历法 是双历法叠加: (1) 一个 260 日的仪式历 (tonalpohualli), 由 20 个日名 × 13 个数字组成; (2) 一个 365 日的太阳历 (xiuhpohualli), 18 个月 × 20 日 + 5 日「无名日」。两个历法每 52 年重合一次, 这个 52 年周期叫 calendar round (历法轮). 每次 calendar round 结束都要举行 New Fire Ceremony (新火礼), 全帝国熄火重燃, 象征宇宙重启。

**EN**: The Aztec calendar was a double-calendar overlap: (1) a 260-day ritual calendar (tonalpohualli), made of 20 day-names × 13 numbers; (2) a 365-day solar calendar (xiuhpohualli), 18 months × 20 days + 5 "nameless days." The two calendars realigned every 52 years. This 52-year cycle is called calendar round (历法轮). At the end of each calendar round, a New Fire Ceremony was held — fires were extinguished across the empire and relit, symbolizing cosmic renewal.

**CN**: Aztec 文字 是 Nahuatl (纳瓦特尔语, Aztec 自己的语言) 的图画文字系统, 比玛雅文字简化, 主要记账、纳贡清单、王室谱系、地图。Aztec 文字 不能像玛雅文字那样记录完整的诗歌或长篇叙事 — 但 Aztec 有非常发达的口传诗歌传统, 16 世纪西班牙修士 Bernardino de Sahagún 用拉丁字母把大量 Nahuatl 诗歌记下来, 留下了像 Cantares Mexicanos 这样的诗集。

**EN**: Aztec writing was a Nahuatl (the Aztec language) pictographic system. It was simpler than Maya script and mainly recorded accounts, tribute lists, royal genealogies, and maps. Aztec writing could not record full poems or long narratives the way Maya script could. But the Aztecs had a very developed oral poetic tradition. In the 16th century, Spanish friar Bernardino de Sahagún used Latin letters to record large amounts of Nahuatl poetry, leaving collections like the Cantares Mexicanos.

### 3.7 careful framing: 阿兹特克 不是「单纯血祭文明」

**CN**: 流行媒体 (好莱坞电影、19 世纪欧洲历史书) 经常把 Aztec 描述成「除了血祭什么都没有的恐怖文明」。这是非常严重的扭曲。Inga Clendinnen 1991 年在 Cambridge 出版的《Aztecs: An Interpretation》是这方面的权威 careful framing: Aztec 确实有人祭, 但 (1) 数字被严重夸大 — popular 文献常说一年 8 万人, 学界 estimate 1-2 万 / 年 (Clendinnen 1991), 仍然多, 但是城邦帝国规模相当; (2) 人祭在 Aztec 宇宙观里 不是「残忍」, 而是宇宙能量循环的必要部分 — 太阳每天升起需要血来「喂养」; (3) 即使按学界数字, 1 万 / 年 在一个 500 万人口的帝国里, 比例相当于同期欧洲宗教裁判所 + 战争 + 公开处刑的合计致死率, 不是异常高。

**EN**: Popular media (Hollywood films, 19th-century European history books) often describes the Aztecs as "a horror civilization with nothing but blood sacrifice." This is a serious distortion. Inga Clendinnen's 1991 Cambridge book *Aztecs: An Interpretation* is the authoritative careful framing on this. The Aztecs did practice human sacrifice, but: (1) the numbers are heavily exaggerated — popular sources often claim 80,000 per year, while academic estimates are 10,000-20,000 per year (Clendinnen 1991), still many, but on the scale of a city-state empire; (2) human sacrifice in Aztec cosmology was not "cruelty" but a necessary part of cosmic energy cycling — the sun required blood to rise each day; (3) even at the academic figure of ~10,000 per year, in an empire of 5 million, the rate was comparable to contemporary European deaths from inquisitions, wars, and public executions combined. It was not abnormally high.

**CN**: 更重要的是: Aztec 文明 还有非常发达的非血祭面向 — chinampa (奇南帕, 漂浮园) 农业系统在 Lake Texcoco 上养活 Tenochtitlán 20 万人口; Pochteca 商人体系把帝国经济连成一片; Nahuatl 诗歌哲学体系达到很高水平; Tenochtitlán 的城市规划 (双重水道、棋盘街道) 让 16 世纪初到达的西班牙人 Hernán Cortés 在他的信件里写「这是世界上最美丽的城市, 比威尼斯还壮观」。

**EN**: More importantly, Aztec civilization had highly developed non-sacrifice dimensions. The chinampa (漂浮园, floating garden) farming system on Lake Texcoco fed Tenochtitlán's 200,000 people. The Pochteca merchant system tied the empire's economy together. Nahuatl poetic and philosophical traditions reached high levels. Tenochtitlán's urban planning — double aqueducts, grid streets — led Hernán Cortés, when he arrived in the early 16th century, to write in his letters: "This is the most beautiful city in the world, more magnificent than Venice."

## §4 Inca pre-Pachacuti 1200-1438

### 4.1 Manco Cápac 神话 founder ~1200

**CN**: Inca (印加) 起源神话有几个版本, 最常见的是: 太阳神 Inti (因蒂) 派他的儿子 Manco Cápac (曼科卡帕克) 和女儿 / 妻子 Mama Ocllo (玛玛奥克略) 从 Lake Titicaca 出发, 拿着一根金杖, 走到金杖能轻易插入土地的地方就建城。他们走到 Cusco (库斯科, 今秘鲁南部高地) 河谷, 金杖陷入土中, 于是建立 Cusco 城, 时间大约 1200 年左右。

**EN**: The Inca origin myth has several versions. The most common: the sun god Inti sent his son Manco Cápac and his daughter / wife Mama Ocllo out from Lake Titicaca, carrying a golden staff. Wherever the staff sank easily into the ground, they would build their city. They walked to the Cusco valley in the southern Peruvian highlands. The staff sank in. They founded Cusco there, around 1200 CE.

**CN**: 这个起源神话 跟 Aztec 鹰啄蛇仙人掌神兆一样, 是后来帝国期 (15 世纪后半) 的政治建构, 不能当作字面历史。但里面有一个真实的考古信号: Cusco 河谷确实在 12 世纪到 13 世纪之间出现了一个新的政治中心, 跟北方 Wari 文明 (~600-1000) 的衰落 + 南方 Tiwanaku 文明的衰落 时间衔接得上。

**EN**: This origin myth, like the Aztec eagle-snake-cactus omen, was a political construction of the later imperial period (late 15th century) and cannot be taken as literal history. But it does contain a real archaeological signal: the Cusco valley did indeed see a new political center emerge between the 12th and 13th centuries. The timing aligns with the decline of the northern Wari civilization (~600-1000) and the southern Tiwanaku civilization.

### 4.2 早期 Inca 部落 — 不是帝国

**CN**: 1200 年到 1438 年这两个多世纪的 Inca 不是帝国, 是 Cusco 河谷一个相对小的酋邦, 跟周围几十个安第斯山区部落 (Chanca / Colla / Lupaca / Quechua 等) 经常打仗、结盟、通婚。Inca 的领导人叫 Sapa Inca (萨帕印加, 「独一首领」), 但这个时期的 Sapa Inca 实际权力 跟周围酋邦首领 差不多, 不是后来那种神化的帝国君主。

**EN**: From 1200 to 1438, for over two centuries, the Inca were not an empire. They were a relatively small chiefdom in the Cusco valley, often at war, in alliance, and intermarrying with dozens of surrounding Andean tribes (Chancas, Collas, Lupacas, Quechuas, and others). The Inca ruler was called Sapa Inca (萨帕印加, "Sole Leader"). But the actual power of the Sapa Inca during this period was roughly equal to that of nearby chiefdom heads — not the deified imperial monarch of later times.

### 4.3 7 任 pre-Pachacuti 大酋长

**CN**: 后来的 Inca 帝国官方记载列出了 7 任 pre-Pachacuti Sapa Inca:
1. Manco Cápac (神话 founder, ~1200)
2. Sinchi Roca (~1230)
3. Lloque Yupanqui
4. Mayta Cápac
5. Capac Yupanqui
6. Inca Roca
7. Yahuar Huacac
8. Viracocha Inca (在位 ~1410-1438)

第 8 任 Viracocha Inca 是 Pachacuti 的父亲, 1438 年时是在位的 Sapa Inca。

**EN**: Later Inca imperial records list 8 pre-Pachacuti Sapa Incas:
1. Manco Cápac (mythical founder, ~1200)
2. Sinchi Roca (~1230)
3. Lloque Yupanqui
4. Mayta Cápac
5. Capac Yupanqui
6. Inca Roca
7. Yahuar Huacac
8. Viracocha Inca (reigned ~1410-1438)

The 8th, Viracocha Inca, was Pachacuti's father, the reigning Sapa Inca in 1438.

### 4.4 1438 节点: Chanca 攻 Cusco + Cusi Yupanqui 反败为胜

**CN**: 1438 年 (一些史料 1437 — 殖民期史料日期不太精确) 发生了一件改变安第斯山区历史的大事: Cusco 北方的 Chanca (查恩卡) 部落联军大举进攻 Cusco。在位 Sapa Inca 的 Viracocha 看局势不妙, 带着大儿子 + 一部分贵族 出逃到山区避难。但 Viracocha 的次子 Cusi Yupanqui (库西尤潘基) 留在 Cusco 城里, 召集愿意死守的战士反抗。

**EN**: In 1438 (some sources say 1437; colonial dates are not always precise), a turning point hit Andean history. The Chanca tribal coalition north of Cusco launched a major attack on Cusco. The reigning Sapa Inca Viracocha saw things going badly. He took his eldest son and part of the nobility into the mountains for refuge. But Viracocha's second son, Cusi Yupanqui, stayed inside Cusco and rallied warriors willing to fight to the death.

**CN**: 据后来的 Inca 神话 (16 世纪殖民期史料 Sarmiento de Gamboa 1572 + Garcilaso de la Vega《Comentarios Reales》1609 记载), Cusi Yupanqui 在战斗中得到了创世神 Viracocha (跟他父亲同名的神, 不是父亲本人) 的显圣, 神告诉他石头会变成战士帮他打仗。结果 Cusi Yupanqui 不仅守住了 Cusco, 还反攻把 Chanca 联军彻底击溃。

**EN**: According to later Inca mythology (recorded in the 16th-century colonial sources Sarmiento de Gamboa 1572 and Garcilaso de la Vega's *Comentarios Reales* 1609), Cusi Yupanqui received a divine vision during the battle from the creator god Viracocha (the deity sharing his father's name, not his father himself). The god told him stones would turn into warriors to fight for him. Cusi Yupanqui not only held Cusco but counterattacked and shattered the Chanca coalition.

### 4.5 改名 Pachacuti — 「时间转折者」

**CN**: 反败为胜后, Cusi Yupanqui 不再承认逃跑的父亲 Viracocha 是 Sapa Inca, 自立为 Sapa Inca, 并改名 Pachacuti (帕查库蒂) — Quechua 语 (盖丘亚语) 意思是「时间转折者」(pacha = 时间 / 世界, kuti = 翻转)。这个名字 自我宣告: 他不仅是新国王, 而且开启了一个新时代。

**EN**: After the victory, Cusi Yupanqui no longer recognized his fled father Viracocha as Sapa Inca. He took the throne for himself and renamed himself Pachacuti — in Quechua (the Inca language), "Time Turner" (pacha = time / world, kuti = to turn over). The name was a self-proclamation: he was not just a new king, but the start of a new era.

**CN**: Pachacuti 接下来 30 年 (1438-1471 在位) 把 Cusco 河谷酋邦 扩张成 Tawantinsuyu (塔万廷苏尤, 「四区合一」) — Inca 帝国正式名称, 控制范围从今哥伦比亚南部到智利北部, 沿安第斯山脉 4000 多公里。这个 30 年扩张 节奏跟 Genghis Khan (成吉思汗) 1206 年统一蒙古后 30 年扩张到中亚、伊朗、东欧的 节奏几乎一致 — 都是 perpetrator-actor 类型的 30 年帝国期。

**EN**: Over the next 30 years (reigning 1438-1471), Pachacuti expanded the Cusco valley chiefdom into Tawantinsuyu (塔万廷苏尤, "The Four Regions United") — the official name of the Inca empire. It stretched from today's southern Colombia to northern Chile, over 4,000 km along the Andes. This 30-year expansion rhythm matches almost exactly Genghis Khan's 30-year expansion after unifying Mongolia in 1206, reaching Central Asia, Iran, and Eastern Europe. Both were perpetrator-actor-type 30-year imperial bursts.

### 4.6 careful framing: pre-Pachacuti 史料 90% 是 1438 后追溯

**CN**: 这里要非常 careful framing。pre-Pachacuti Inca (1200-1438) 这两个多世纪的「历史」 学界 (Catherine Julien 2000 在 University of Iowa 出版的《Reading Inca History》) 估计 90% 是 1438 年 Pachacuti 即位之后由 Inca 帝国宫廷史官重新建构出来的「合法性叙事」。原因有 3:
- (1) Inca 没有文字。quipu (绳结) 能记录数字 + 行政信息, 但学界目前一致认为 quipu 不能记录完整的叙事性历史 — 只能记账。
- (2) 我们今天能看到的 pre-Pachacuti 叙事 全部来自 16 世纪殖民期史料: Sarmiento de Gamboa《Historia de los Incas》(1572), Garcilaso de la Vega《Comentarios Reales》(1609), Pedro Cieza de León《Crónica del Perú》(1553)。这些都是西班牙征服后 30-80 年, 由西班牙人 + 西班牙化 Inca 贵族 编写的。
- (3) Pachacuti 即位后做了一件帝国创始人很常见的事: 系统重写自己家族的历史, 把自己的王朝追溯成神圣起源。

**EN**: Very careful framing required here. For the pre-Pachacuti Inca period (1200-1438), scholars such as Catherine Julien (*Reading Inca History*, University of Iowa Press 2000) estimate that about 90% of this "history" was reconstructed by Inca court historians after Pachacuti took power in 1438, as a legitimacy narrative. Three reasons:
- (1) The Inca had no writing. Quipu (knotted cord, 绳结) could record numbers and administrative data, but scholars today agree quipu could not record full narrative history. Only accounts.
- (2) All the pre-Pachacuti narratives we have today come from 16th-century colonial sources: Sarmiento de Gamboa's *Historia de los Incas* (1572), Garcilaso de la Vega's *Comentarios Reales* (1609), and Pedro Cieza de León's *Crónica del Perú* (1553). All written 30-80 years after the Spanish conquest, by Spaniards or Hispanized Inca nobles.
- (3) After taking power, Pachacuti did what imperial founders commonly do — he systematically rewrote his family history, tracing his dynasty back to a sacred origin.

**CN**: 所以当我们说「Manco Cápac 1200 年建立 Cusco」「7 任 Sapa Inca 之间发生了什么」时, 实际是说「这是 Pachacuti 帝国宫廷在 1450 年代左右编出来的官方历史」, 不一定是字面发生过的事。这跟同期欧洲 Charlemagne 帝国之后法兰克王朝重写自己家族追溯到罗马的做法, 在性质上一样。

**EN**: So when we say "Manco Cápac founded Cusco in 1200" or "what happened among the 8 Sapa Incas," we are really saying "this is the official history compiled by Pachacuti's imperial court around the 1450s." It is not necessarily what literally happened. This parallels what the Frankish dynasty after Charlemagne's empire did in Europe, tracing their family back to Rome. It is the same kind of legitimizing rewrite.

### 4.7 跟 Mali Aisha 1413 的对比: 非文字行政

**CN**: 安第斯山区 quipu 跟同期非洲 Mali 帝国 (Aisha 商人 1413 年那个 Topic) 的口传 + 阿拉伯化记账系统, 是个有趣的对比。Mali 是有文字 (阿拉伯文) 的, 但只有少数受过教育的商人 + 学者用; 大部分行政靠口传 + 数字记账。Inca 完全没有文字, 但 quipu 能管理一个跨 4000 公里的帝国。这两个例子 都说明: 「文字是文明唯一标准」的旧观点 已经被 20 世纪后半的人类学全面推翻 — 复杂行政可以建立在多种媒介上。

**EN**: The Andean quipu offers an interesting comparison with the same-era African Mali Empire (the Topic about the trader Aisha in 1413). Mali had writing (Arabic), but only a small group of educated traders and scholars used it. Most administration ran on oral tradition and numeric accounts. The Inca had no writing at all, yet quipu managed an empire stretching 4,000 km. Both examples show that the old view "writing is the only standard of civilization" has been thoroughly overturned by late-20th-century anthropology. Complex administration can be built on many media.

## §5 1500 时空切片: 三 lens 主角站在世界什么位置

### 5.1 1500 年三 lens 主角

**CN**: 把镜头定格在 1500 年, 三个未来 Lens 主角分别站在生命的不同位置:
- **Lens 1 Pachacuti** 已经死了 28 年 (1472 死)。他建立的 Tawantinsuyu 帝国 现在是孙子 Huayna Capac (瓦伊纳卡帕克, 在位 1493-1527) 的全盛期。
- **Lens 2 Malintzin** 刚出生 (~1500), 在墨西哥湾岸 Coatzacoalcos (科阿察夸尔科斯) 区域一个 Nahuatl 语贵族家庭, 还是个婴儿。
- **Lens 3 Ix Mutal Ahaw** 已经 20 岁 (~1480 出生), 是一个 Yucatán 内陆村落的成年织女, 可能已经结婚, 育有一两个孩子。

**EN**: If we freeze the frame on 1500, the three future Lens protagonists are each at a different point in life:
- **Lens 1 Pachacuti** has been dead for 28 years (died 1472). The Tawantinsuyu empire he built is now in its full peak under his grandson Huayna Capac (reigned 1493-1527).
- **Lens 2 Malintzin** has just been born (~1500), an infant in a Nahuatl-speaking noble family in the Coatzacoalcos area on the Mexican Gulf coast.
- **Lens 3 Ix Mutal Ahaw** is already 20 years old (born ~1480), an adult weaver in an inland Yucatán village, probably already married with one or two children.

### 5.2 美洲三大文明 1500 状态

**CN**: 1500 年时, 美洲三大文明各自的状态:
- **Aztec Triple Alliance** 全盛期。Moctezuma II (蒙特苏马二世) 即将 1502 年即位, 帝国控制人口 500-600 万, 首都 Tenochtitlán 20 万以上居民, 是当时世界上最大的城市之一 (跟同期巴黎 + 君士坦丁堡 + 北京同级)。
- **Inca Tawantinsuyu** 全盛期。Huayna Capac 在位, 帝国控制人口约 1000 万, 跨 4000 公里安第斯山脉, 首都 Cusco 是宗教中心, 但帝国第二都市 Quito (基多, 今厄瓜多尔) 也在快速发展。
- **Postclassic Maya** 处于分散政体时代。Mayapan 1450 年代崩溃后, Yucatán 半岛是几十个小型城邦, 没有统一政治中心。但人口、贸易、纺织业、农业都还活跃。Lens 3 Ix Mutal 就在这个分散世界里。

**EN**: In 1500, the three great American civilizations stood as follows:
- **Aztec Triple Alliance** at full peak. Moctezuma II would take the throne in 1502. The empire ruled 5-6 million people. The capital Tenochtitlán held over 200,000 — one of the largest cities in the world at that time, comparable to contemporary Paris, Constantinople, and Beijing.
- **Inca Tawantinsuyu** at full peak. Huayna Capac on the throne. The empire ruled around 10 million people across 4,000 km of Andes. Cusco was the religious capital, while the second imperial city Quito (in modern Ecuador) was growing fast.
- **Postclassic Maya** in the era of dispersed polities. After Mayapan's 1450s collapse, the Yucatán was dozens of small city-states with no unified political center. But population, trade, textile work, and agriculture were all still active. Lens 3's Ix Mutal lived in this dispersed world.

### 5.3 同期 1500 欧洲: Grade 7 平行 anchor

**CN**: 给 Grade 7 同学一个平行参照: 1500 年的欧洲在做什么?
- **意大利文艺复兴** 全盛。Lorenzo de' Medici (洛伦佐·美第奇) 已经死了 8 年 (1492 死)。Leonardo da Vinci 48 岁, 在画 Mona Lisa (《蒙娜丽莎》, 1503-1519 创作)。Michelangelo 25 岁, 即将开始 David 雕像 (1501-1504)。
- **Columbus** 1492 年才第一次到达 Hispaniola (伊斯帕尼奥拉岛, 今海地 + 多米尼加共和国), 还没有任何欧洲人到达过墨西哥本土或秘鲁本土。1500 年的西班牙人对美洲大陆 完全无知。
- **Cortés** (后来 1519 征服 Aztec 那个) 这年才 19 岁, 还在西班牙萨拉曼卡大学 (Universidad de Salamanca) 读法律, 后来辍学。
- **Pizarro** (后来 1532 征服 Inca 那个) 这年 22 岁, 在西班牙南部还是个穷养猪人, 没读过书。

**EN**: Here is a parallel anchor for Grade 7 readers: what was Europe doing in 1500?
- **Italian Renaissance** at full peak. Lorenzo de' Medici had been dead for 8 years (died 1492). Leonardo da Vinci was 48, about to paint the Mona Lisa (1503-1519). Michelangelo was 25, about to begin the David statue (1501-1504).
- **Columbus** had only first reached Hispaniola (modern Haiti and Dominican Republic) in 1492. No European had yet reached mainland Mexico or mainland Peru. The Spanish in 1500 were completely unaware of the American mainland.
- **Cortés** (who would conquer the Aztecs in 1519) was just 19 years old, studying law at the University of Salamanca in Spain. He would drop out.
- **Pizarro** (who would conquer the Inca in 1532) was 22 years old, still a poor pig farmer in southern Spain, illiterate.

**CN**: 这里要让学生看到的反 Whig 关键: 1500 年时, Aztec 和 Inca 帝国 各自比同期任何一个欧洲国家 (西班牙 + 法国 + 英格兰 + 葡萄牙) 都更大, 更复杂, 更人口密集。后来 1519-1533 年的「征服」 不是因为美洲文明「落后」或「等待被发现」, 而是疾病 (天花 smallpox 是头号杀手, 杀掉 50-90% 美洲原住民人口) + 恰好的政治时机 (Aztec 帝国统治不稳, 被征服盟友反抗; Inca 帝国正好陷入 Huascar / Atahualpa 内战) + 西班牙的钢铁 + 火药 + 马 + 远洋航海技术 共同造就的。

**EN**: The anti-Whig point students should see: in 1500, the Aztec and Inca empires were each larger, more complex, and more densely populated than any contemporary European state — Spain, France, England, or Portugal. The later "conquest" of 1519-1533 was not because American civilizations were "backward" or "waiting to be discovered." It was a combination of disease (smallpox was the number-one killer, wiping out 50-90% of Native American populations), perfect political timing (the Aztec empire was unstable and conquered allies revolted; the Inca empire happened to be in a Huascar / Atahualpa civil war), and Spanish steel, gunpowder, horses, and ocean-going ship technology working together.

**CN**: 1500 年这一年 是接下来 32 年 (1500-1532) 整个美洲世界即将面对的 大转折点的「前夕」。Lens 1 Pachacuti 已经入土, 但他建立的帝国还要再 33 年才被征服; Lens 2 Malintzin 还在襁褓里, 19 年后她将成为 Cortés 征服 Aztec 的关键翻译; Lens 3 Ix Mutal Ahaw 已经 20 岁, 接下来 40 年她和她的家族将要见证 Yucatán 玛雅世界缓慢被殖民的全过程 (Yucatán 真正被西班牙完全控制要到 1546 年, 比 Aztec + Inca 都晚)。

**EN**: 1500 was the "eve" of a 32-year turning point (1500-1532) for the entire American world. Lens 1 Pachacuti was already in the grave, but the empire he built still had 33 years left before conquest. Lens 2 Malintzin was still an infant in swaddling clothes; 19 years later she would become the key translator for Cortés's conquest of the Aztecs. Lens 3 Ix Mutal Ahaw was already 20 years old; the next 40 years she and her family would witness the slow colonization of the Yucatán Maya world. (The Yucatán was not fully under Spanish control until 1546, later than either the Aztecs or the Inca.)

**CN**: Part 2 将进入 Pachacuti 真正的 perpetrator-actor 30 年 (1438-1471) 详细叙事 + Tawantinsuyu 的行政创新 (mit'a 劳役 + 道路网 + quipu 行政) + 1500 年 Aztec / Inca / Maya 三个世界 各自的内部动力学。Part 3 将转到 1500-1532 三 lens 视角的征服故事。

**EN**: Part 2 will enter the detailed narrative of Pachacuti's true perpetrator-actor 30 years (1438-1471), the administrative innovations of Tawantinsuyu (mit'a labor service, road networks, quipu administration), and the internal dynamics of the Aztec, Inca, and Maya worlds in 1500. Part 3 will shift to the conquest stories from the three Lens perspectives during 1500-1532.

### 6.1 跟同期世界的 5 个对照锚点

**CN**: Part 1 涉及大约 500 年时间 (1000-1500 CE), 这段时间在旧大陆同期发生的事 同样精彩。给学生 5 个跨 Topic 缝合锚点, 帮助他们把美洲 + 旧大陆放在同一个时间轴上理解:

**EN**: Part 1 covers roughly 500 years (1000-1500 CE). The Old World during the same period was equally rich. Here are 5 cross-Topic anchor points to help students put the Americas and the Old World on the same timeline.

**CN**: **锚点 1 — 800 年三大文明同步**: 唐朝长安 100 万人口 + Charlemagne 圣诞节加冕 + Maya 古典期 Tikal/Calakmul 最后纪念碑刻立, 三件事都发生在同一个世纪。这告诉我们: 复杂文明从来不是一处独有的现象, 而是多点同步演化的全球现象。

**EN**: **Anchor 1 — Three civilizations synchronized at 800 CE**: Tang Chang'an's million-person city, Charlemagne's Christmas coronation, and the last monuments of Maya Tikal and Calakmul — all in the same century. Complex civilization is never a single-place phenomenon. It is a global pattern of multi-point synchronous evolution.

**CN**: **锚点 2 — 1206 Genghis 跟 1438 Pachacuti 30 年帝国期 pattern 共享**: 草原文明的成吉思汗 1206 年统一蒙古, 30 年内打到中亚 + 伊朗 + 东欧。安第斯山区的 Pachacuti 1438 年改名即位, 30 年内把 Cusco 河谷酋邦扩张成跨 4000 公里的 Tawantinsuyu。两个完全不知道对方存在的 perpetrator-actor 类型创始者, 在不同大陆使用同一种节奏 — 这是世界史最有意思的 parallel pattern 之一。

**EN**: **Anchor 2 — The 1206 Genghis and 1438 Pachacuti 30-year empire-burst pattern**: Steppe civilization's Genghis Khan unified Mongolia in 1206 and within 30 years reached Central Asia, Iran, and Eastern Europe. Andean Pachacuti renamed himself and took the throne in 1438 and within 30 years expanded the Cusco chiefdom into a 4,000-km Tawantinsuyu. Two perpetrator-actor founders, completely unaware of each other's existence, used the same rhythm on different continents — one of world history's most interesting parallel patterns.

**CN**: **锚点 3 — Mali Aisha 1413 跟 Inca quipu 非文字行政对照**: 同期非洲 Mali 帝国的商人 Aisha 时代 (1413), 用阿拉伯文字 + 口传混合记账; Inca 完全没有文字, 用 quipu 绳结管理千万级人口帝国。这两个例子合起来 拆掉了「文字 = 文明」这个 19 世纪欧洲中心主义观点 — 复杂行政的载体可以多元。

**EN**: **Anchor 3 — Mali's Aisha 1413 and the Inca quipu, contrasting non-script administration**: The contemporary Mali Empire merchant Aisha era (1413) used a hybrid of Arabic script and oral tradition for accounting. The Inca had no script at all, yet managed an empire of millions through knotted quipu. Together, these examples dismantle the 19th-century Eurocentric view that "writing equals civilization." Complex administration can ride on multiple media.

**CN**: **锚点 4 — Postclassic Maya「分散化 ≠ 衰落」跟欧洲中世纪后期分散化对比**: 1300 年后欧洲也进入了一个相对分散化时代 (神圣罗马帝国名存实亡 + 意大利城邦林立 + 法国百年战争分裂)。我们没有把欧洲中世纪后期叫做「文明衰落」, 那也不应该把 Postclassic Maya 叫做「衰落」。两边都是「单一霸权解体, 多中心分散化, 经济文化继续活跃」的同一类历史阶段。

**EN**: **Anchor 4 — Postclassic Maya's "decentralization ≠ decline" parallel with late medieval Europe**: After 1300, Europe also entered a relatively decentralized phase (the Holy Roman Empire became hollow, Italian city-states multiplied, France splintered during the Hundred Years' War). We do not call late medieval Europe a "civilizational decline." We should not call Postclassic Maya a "decline" either. Both are the same kind of historical phase: single-hegemony dissolution, multi-center decentralization, with economy and culture still active.

**CN**: **锚点 5 — 1500 年三大美洲文明 vs 同期欧洲: 反 Whig 关键**: 1500 年时, Aztec 帝国 (500-600 万人口) + Inca 帝国 (1000 万人口) 各自比同期任何一个欧洲国家都大。Tenochtitlán (20 万 +) 跟同期巴黎 (~20 万) + 君士坦丁堡 (~20 万) + 北京 (~70 万) 同级。所以 1519-1533 的征服不是「先进文明征服落后文明」, 而是疾病 + 政治时机 + 钢铁火药航海技术 三因素的偶然叠加。

**EN**: **Anchor 5 — The three American civilizations in 1500 vs. contemporary Europe: the anti-Whig key**: In 1500, the Aztec empire (5-6 million people) and Inca empire (10 million) were each larger than any contemporary European state. Tenochtitlán (200,000+) was on the same scale as contemporary Paris (~200,000), Constantinople (~200,000), and Beijing (~700,000). The 1519-1533 conquest was not "advanced civilization conquering backward civilization." It was a contingent stacking of three factors: disease, political timing, and steel-gunpowder-ocean-navigation technology.

### 6.2 反 Whig 总结: Part 1 想让学生破除的 4 个流行误解

**CN**:
- **误解 1**: 玛雅古典期崩溃是「神秘消失」 → **真相**: 多因素 (干旱 + 战争 + 内部权力 + 农业承载) 综合, 而且只是南部低地, 北部 Yucatán 没崩。
- **误解 2**: Postclassic Maya 是「衰落文明」 → **真相**: 是「分散化 + 区域政体」, 经济文化继续活跃, 跟欧洲中世纪后期同类型。
- **误解 3**: Aztec 是「单纯血祭文明」 → **真相**: 复杂城邦帝国 + 商业 + chinampa 农业 + 诗歌哲学; 人祭存在但数字被严重夸大 (popular 8 万 / 年 vs 学界 1-2 万 / 年, Clendinnen 1991)。
- **误解 4**: Inca pre-Pachacuti 历史是「真实的祖先记忆」 → **真相**: 90% 是 1438 年 Pachacuti 即位后宫廷史官重新建构的合法性叙事 (Catherine Julien 2000), 跟欧洲许多王朝追溯到罗马的做法一样。

**EN**:
- **Myth 1**: The Maya Classic collapse was a "mysterious disappearance." → **Truth**: It was a combination of factors (drought + war + internal power struggles + agricultural carrying capacity), and only the southern lowlands collapsed; the northern Yucatán did not.
- **Myth 2**: Postclassic Maya was a "civilization in decline." → **Truth**: It was "decentralization + regional polities," with active economy and culture, the same kind of phase as late medieval Europe.
- **Myth 3**: The Aztecs were a "pure blood-sacrifice civilization." → **Truth**: A complex city-state empire with trade, chinampa agriculture, poetry, and philosophy. Human sacrifice existed but the numbers are heavily exaggerated (popular sources say 80,000/year vs. academic estimates of 10,000-20,000/year, Clendinnen 1991).
- **Myth 4**: Pre-Pachacuti Inca history is "authentic ancestral memory." → **Truth**: 90% is a legitimacy narrative reconstructed by court historians after Pachacuti's 1438 accession (Catherine Julien 2000), the same kind of practice as European dynasties tracing themselves back to Rome.

## §6 Pachacuti 1438-1472 — 时间转折者 ⭐⭐⭐

### §6.1 1438 Chanca 攻 Cusco · 反败为胜

1438 年春, Cusco 河谷里飘着烟。Chanca 部落 (Chanca people, 来自西边 Apurímac 河上游) 集结了几千战士, 朝 Inca 城邦 Cusco 进发。当时的 Inca 还不是「帝国」— 只是 Andes 高原上几十个城邦之一, 控制范围不超过 50 公里。

In spring 1438, smoke drifted over the Cusco valley. The Chanca, a federation from the upper Apurímac River to the west, marched several thousand warriors toward the Inca city-state of Cusco. The Inca at this point were not an "empire" — just one of dozens of Andean polities, with a domain barely 50 km across.

老国王 Viracocha Inca (第 8 任 Sapa Inca, 「Sapa Inca」= 唯一的王 / unique king) 看局势不妙, 带着嫡子 Inca Urco 和大部分王室出逃, 退到东北山里的 Caquia Xaquixaguana 要塞躲。留在 Cusco 的, 是 Viracocha 的另一个儿子 — Cusi Yupanqui, 当时 ~20 岁, 不是嫡子, 不被看好。

The aging king Viracocha Inca (8th Sapa Inca, "Sapa Inca" = unique king) judged the situation hopeless. He fled with his designated heir Inca Urco and most of the royal household to the fortress of Caquia Xaquixaguana in the hills northeast. Left behind in Cusco was another son — Cusi Yupanqui, ~20 years old, not the chosen heir, not favored.

Cusi Yupanqui 没跑。他召集 Cusco 周边城邦 — Quechua 部 (西南) + Cana 部 (南) — 凑了大约 等量的战士。1438 在 Cusco 城外 Yahuarpampa 平原 (Yahuar = 血, pampa = 平原, 「血原」), 双方决战。

Cusi Yupanqui did not flee. He rallied the surrounding city-states — the Quechua (southwest) and the Cana (south) — assembling roughly matched numbers. In 1438, on the Yahuarpampa plain outside Cusco (Yahuar = blood, pampa = plain, "Plain of Blood"), the two armies met.

后来的 Inca 王朝叙事说: 战斗到一半, Cusi Yupanqui 祈祷, 平原上的石头变成了战士加入战斗 — 「pururaucas」, 石头战士。Chanca 大败, 撤退。Cusi Yupanqui 自立为第 9 任 Sapa Inca, 改名 **Pachacuti** — Quechua 语「pacha」= 时间 / 大地, 「kuti」= 翻转, 合起来「时间转折者 / 大地震荡者」。

Later Inca dynastic narrative said: mid-battle, Cusi Yupanqui prayed, and stones on the plain turned into warriors who joined the fight — *pururaucas*, stone warriors. The Chanca broke and retreated. Cusi Yupanqui declared himself 9th Sapa Inca, taking the name **Pachacuti** — Quechua *pacha* (time / earth) + *kuti* (turn / overturn), "Time-Turner" or "Earth-Shaker."

**careful framing**: 这一段 1438 大战, 史料是 16 世纪西班牙人到了之后才写下的 — Pedro Sarmiento de Gamboa 1572《Historia índica》, Inca Garcilaso de la Vega 1609《Comentarios reales》。学者 Catherine Julien 2000《Reading Inca History》认为, 1438 反败为胜 + Pachacuti 改朝换代 是历史核心事实, 但「石头变战士」这种细节是后世王朝把开国神话化了。读这段时, 把握「框架真, 细节疑」: Pachacuti 是真人, 1438 节点是真节点, 但戏剧化叙事是 Inca 王朝 PR。

**careful framing**: This 1438 episode comes from sources written after the Spanish arrival — Pedro Sarmiento de Gamboa's 1572 *Historia índica*, Inca Garcilaso de la Vega's 1609 *Comentarios reales*. Scholar Catherine Julien (*Reading Inca History*, 2000) argues the historical core — 1438 reversal + Pachacuti dynasty change — is solid, but the "stones turning to warriors" is later dynastic mythologizing. Reading rule: frame is real, details are PR.

### §6.2 1438-1450 帝国扩张 · 系统重建

胜了 Chanca 之后, Pachacuti 没歇。1438-1450 这 12 年, 他做了三件事: 重建 Cusco 城, 创立帝国结构, 立国教。

After the Chanca victory, Pachacuti did not rest. In the 12 years from 1438 to 1450 he did three things: rebuilt Cusco, founded an imperial structure, and instituted a state religion.

**Cusco 重建**: Pachacuti 把 Cusco 重新规划成美洲豹形 (puma) — 城心是头, Saqsaywaman 要塞是头顶。Saqsaywaman 的 zigzag 巨石城墙 (现在还在) 用的是 100-200 吨重的石块, 拼接精度到「插不进刀片」级别 — 没有砂浆, 全靠精准切割。Coricancha (Quri Kancha, 「黄金围院」) 太阳神殿建在城心, 内墙镀金板, 庭院有金制玉米杆 (西班牙人 1533 来时融了拿走)。

**Cusco rebuild**: Pachacuti redesigned Cusco in the shape of a puma — the city center as the head, Saqsaywaman fortress as the crown. Saqsaywaman's zigzag megalithic walls (still standing) use 100-200 ton stones fitted to "razor-blade" precision — no mortar, just exact cutting. Coricancha (Quri Kancha, "Golden Enclosure"), the Sun Temple, sat at city center, walls plated in gold, courtyards holding life-sized gold maize stalks (the Spanish melted them down in 1533).

**Tawantinsuyu**: Pachacuti 给帝国起名 **Tawantinsuyu** — Quechua「tawa」= 四, 「-ntin」= 在一起, 「suyu」= 区域, 合起来「四区合一」。四个区从 Cusco 放射出去:
- **Chinchaysuyu** 北 (今厄瓜多尔 / 哥伦比亚南)
- **Antisuyu** 东 (Amazon 林缘)
- **Cuntisuyu** 西 (太平洋海岸)
- **Collasuyu** 南 (今玻利维亚 / 阿根廷 / 智利北)

**Tawantinsuyu**: Pachacuti named the empire **Tawantinsuyu** — Quechua *tawa* (four) + *-ntin* (together) + *suyu* (region), "Four Regions Together." Four quarters radiating from Cusco: Chinchaysuyu (north, today's Ecuador / southern Colombia), Antisuyu (east, Amazon edge), Cuntisuyu (west, Pacific coast), Collasuyu (south, today's Bolivia / Argentina / northern Chile).

**mit'a 劳役制**: 帝国基层制度。每个成年男性臣民每年欠帝国 ~90 天劳役 — 不交税 (没货币), 而是出工。修路, 种国家粮田, 矿里挖银, 当兵戍边。**mit'a** Quechua 词, 「轮班」的意思 — 强调是「轮」, 大家都摊。

**mit'a labor levy**: The empire's base institution. Every adult male subject owed ~90 days of labor per year — not taxes (no currency), but work. Building roads, farming state fields, mining silver, garrisoning frontiers. **mit'a** is Quechua for "turn / shift" — emphasizing rotation, everyone takes turns.

**ayllu 亲属社区**: mit'a 是怎么组织的? 通过 **ayllu** — 扩展亲属社区, 几十到几百户共享土地 + 共同祖先 + 互助义务。ayllu 是 Andean 社会的基础单位, Pachacuti 没发明它 (Andean 社会几千年都在), 但他把 ayllu 嵌进了帝国行政结构: 10 户 → 50 户 → 100 户 → 500 户 → 1000 户 → 1 万户, 每级有 kuraka (头人) 管。

**ayllu kin community**: How was mit'a organized? Through **ayllu** — extended kin communities of dozens to hundreds of households, sharing land + common ancestors + mutual obligations. The ayllu was the bedrock of Andean society for millennia (Pachacuti did not invent it), but he embedded ayllu into imperial administration: 10 households → 50 → 100 → 500 → 1,000 → 10,000, each tier headed by a *kuraka* (chief).

**太阳崇拜国教化**: Pachacuti 自封「Inti 太阳之子」(Intip Churin) — 太阳神 Inti 是 Inca 王室祖先, Sapa Inca 是 Inti 在地上的代理。这一招 是国家工程的核心: 你反 Sapa Inca = 反神。

**Sun cult as state religion**: Pachacuti styled himself "Son of the Sun" (*Intip Churin*) — the sun god Inti as royal ancestor, the Sapa Inca as Inti's earthly viceroy. This was core state engineering: opposing the Sapa Inca = opposing god.

**careful framing 跨 Topic**: Pachacuti 1438 立国 + Yasa 式法律 + 国教 — 跟 Mongol Genghis Khan 1206 立 Yasa 法 + 萨满 Tengri 天命, 是同种「游牧/小邦 → 大帝国」的国家工程。两个都不是孤例 — 13-15 世纪欧亚美三大洲都有这种类型的快速扩张体。Catherine Julien 2000 + Mary Doyle 1988《The Ancestor Cult and Burial Ritual》指出, Pachacuti 的国教化在他死后 3 代 (Topa Inca + Huayna Capac) 才完成全帝国渗透。

**Cross-topic careful framing**: Pachacuti's 1438 founding + Yasa-like law + state religion parallels Mongol Genghis Khan's 1206 Yasa law + shaman-Tengri mandate — both are the "small polity → vast empire" engineering pattern of the 13-15c. Two of three continents (Eurasia, Americas) ran this play. Julien 2000 + Mary Doyle 1988 (*The Ancestor Cult and Burial Ritual at Inka Cuzco*) argue Pachacuti's religious centralization took 3 generations (through Topa Inca + Huayna Capac) to fully permeate the empire.

### §6.3 1450-1472 quipu 绳结 · Qhapaq Ñan 路网

帝国铺开了, Pachacuti 面对一个问题: 怎么管? Inca 没有文字 — 没字母, 没字符, 没象形。但他们有 **quipu** (Quechua「khipu」= 结)。

Empire spread, Pachacuti faced a problem: how to administer? The Inca had no writing — no alphabet, no glyphs, no pictographs. But they had **quipu** (Quechua *khipu* = knot).

**quipu 绳结记账**: 一根主绳, 下面挂几十到几百根支绳, 每根支绳上打不同位置 + 不同种类的结。位置编码十进位数字 (个位 / 十位 / 百位 / 千位), 颜色和结型编码类别 (玉米 / 土豆 / 战士 / 户)。一个 quipu 能记: 某村今年产玉米 4,532 蒲式耳, 户数 87, 适龄男 23, 欠 mit'a 工 18 人。

**quipu knot record**: A primary cord with dozens to hundreds of pendant cords, each knotted at varying positions + types. Position encoded base-10 digits (ones / tens / hundreds / thousands); color and knot-type encoded category (maize / potato / warrior / household). A single quipu could record: village X this year — 4,532 bushels maize, 87 households, 23 adult males, 18 mit'a laborers owed.

**careful framing**: quipu 不是「文字的替代」, 是不同范式 — 它擅长记数字 + 行政记录, 但能不能记叙事 (故事 / 历史) 是学术争论。Gary Urton 2003《Signs of the Inka Khipu》论证某些 quipu 可能有叙事编码, 但没破译。

**careful framing**: quipu is not a "writing substitute" — it is a different paradigm. Excellent at numbers + administrative records; whether it could encode narrative (stories / history) is debated. Gary Urton (*Signs of the Inka Khipu*, 2003) argues some quipus may carry narrative coding, but it remains undeciphered.

**跨 Topic cross-ref**: 同期 Mali Empire 商人女儿 Aisha (Topic: West African Empires 600-1591) 在 Timbuktu 用阿拉伯字母 + 商业账簿记交易 — 用的是字母系统。同时间, Andean 帝国用绳结管理 — 不同信息技术路径, 都管理了百万级人口。这不是「谁高级」, 是「问题不同, 工具不同」。

**Cross-topic**: Contemporary in Mali Empire (Topic: West African Empires 600-1591), the merchant's daughter Aisha kept ledgers in Arabic script in Timbuktu — alphabetic system. Simultaneously, the Andean empire administered millions via knotted cords — different information technologies, both managing million-scale populations. Not "who is more advanced" — different problems, different tools.

**Qhapaq Ñan 路网**: Pachacuti + Topa Inca 修了 ~40,000 公里的 Inca 路网 — Quechua 「Qhapaq Ñan」= 王道。从厄瓜多尔 / 哥伦比亚边界, 一路到阿根廷 / 智利北部 — 跨过 Andes 山脉 (海拔 4,000-5,000 米), 跨过沙漠 (Atacama, 世界最干), 跨过雨林边缘 (Amazon 西缘)。

**Qhapaq Ñan road network**: Pachacuti + Topa Inca built ~40,000 km of Inca roads — Quechua *Qhapaq Ñan* = Royal Road. From the Ecuador / Colombia border south to northern Argentina / Chile — across the Andes (4,000-5,000 m elevation), across the Atacama desert (driest on Earth), along the Amazon's western edge.

**chasqui 跑信使**: 路网上每 1.5-3 公里设一个 tambo (驿站), 站着 chasqui — 训练有素的跑步信使, 接力传消息。一个 chasqui 跑 ~2 公里, 把 quipu 或口信交给下一个。接力下来, 消息一天传 250-400 公里。

**chasqui runners**: Every 1.5-3 km along the road, a *tambo* (way station) housed *chasqui* — trained running messengers, relaying messages. One chasqui ran ~2 km, passed the quipu or verbal message to the next. Relay total: 250-400 km per day.

**跨 Topic cross-ref Mongol Yam**: Mongol 帝国 (Topic: Mongol Empire 1206-1368) 的 Yam 驿站 25-50 公里一站, 骑马接力, 一天可传 ~300 公里。两个体系到达类似速度 — 但 Inca 全靠人腿 + 海拔 4,000 米山路 + 没马。这一对照让你看到: 美洲没有马 / 牛 / 驴 (大型驯化哺乳动物) — 这不是「Inca 落后」, 是大陆生物地理决定的 (Diamond 1997《Guns, Germs, and Steel》框架, 但谨慎: Diamond 框架是大尺度叙事, 具体决策仍是人做的)。

**Mongol Yam cross-ref**: The Mongol Empire's (Topic: Mongol Empire 1206-1368) Yam relay stations sat 25-50 km apart, horse-relay, ~300 km/day. Both systems hit similar speeds — but the Inca did it on human legs, on 4,000-m mountain trails, with no horses. This contrast highlights: the Americas had no horses / cattle / donkeys (large domesticable mammals) — not "Inca were behind," but continental biogeography (Diamond 1997 *Guns, Germs, and Steel* framework, used carefully: Diamond is a macro-narrative, specific decisions still belong to humans).

**careful framing**: Qhapaq Ñan 在 21 世纪被 UNESCO 列为世界文化遗产 (2014, 涉及 6 国 — 阿根廷 / 玻利维亚 / 智利 / 哥伦比亚 / 厄瓜多尔 / 秘鲁)。现在很多段你还能走 — Cusco 到 Machu Picchu 的「Inca Trail」是其中最有名的小段。

**careful framing**: Qhapaq Ñan was inscribed as UNESCO World Heritage in 2014 (spanning 6 countries — Argentina, Bolivia, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru). Many segments are still walkable today — the Cusco-to-Machu Picchu "Inca Trail" is the most famous short segment.

### §6.4 mit'a 系统 · 受影响的人

帝国机器转起来了, 但机器不是空气做的 — 是人做的。Pachacuti 的 mit'a + mitmaq + yanakuna 三个系统, 让普通 Andean 人的生活完全改了。

The imperial machine turned, but machines are not made of air — they are made of people. Pachacuti's three systems — mit'a + mitmaq + yanakuna — completely reshaped ordinary Andean life.

**mit'a 90 天**: 名义上「轮班」, 实际上对受指派的人很沉重。如果你是矿工 mit'a (Potosí 银矿之后的事, 西班牙时代延续, 但 Inca 时也有矿 mit'a), 一年里 90 天在地下挖矿, 死亡率高。如果你是远征军 mit'a, 跟着 Topa Inca 北征厄瓜多尔, 走半年。一些人不见了 — 死矿里, 死战场, 死路上。

**mit'a 90 days**: Nominally "rotation," in practice heavy on those assigned to the worst tasks. Mine *mit'a* (silver mining; the system continued and worsened under the Spanish at Potosí, but it existed under the Inca too) meant 90 days underground per year, with high mortality. Military *mit'a* meant marching with Topa Inca to Ecuador, away half a year. Some did not return — dying in mines, on battlefields, on the road.

**mitmaq 移民**: 整族迁徙制度。Pachacuti 征服一个区域之后, 把那里 不老实 的部落整族搬迁到帝国另一头, 同时把 老实的 ayllu 搬过去填空。这做政治稳定: 反叛部落被打散, 新迁来的 ayllu 没有当地根基, 只能靠帝国。

**mitmaq forced migration**: Whole-community relocation. After conquest, Pachacuti shifted unruly populations to the empire's other end, replacing them with loyal ayllu from elsewhere. Politically stabilizing: rebel communities were dispersed; transplanted ayllu had no local roots, dependent on the empire.

但被迁徙的 ayllu — 离开了祖坟 (Andean 文化里祖先 huaca / 圣物 在土地里), 离开了熟悉的山, 到了不会种当地作物的新地。Mary Doyle 1988 + Carolyn Dean 2010《Inka Bodies and the Body of Christ》: 这种地理 + 精神双重切断 是 mitmaq 的真实代价。

But the relocated ayllu — cut from ancestral graves (Andean *huaca* / sacred sites in the land), cut from familiar mountains, dropped into terrain whose crops they did not know. Mary Doyle 1988 + Carolyn Dean 2010 (*Inka Bodies and the Body of Christ*): the dual geographic + spiritual severance was the real cost of mitmaq.

**yanakuna 永久仆**: 战俘 + 边缘部落 → 永久 Inca 王室 + 贵族家仆。脱离 ayllu, 不再有亲属社区保护, 终身服务一个王室家族 (panaca)。儿子继承父辈 yanakuna 身份。

**yanakuna permanent servants**: Captives + marginal peoples → lifelong servants of the Inca royal family + nobles. Severed from ayllu, no kin community to protect them, bound to one royal household (*panaca*) for life. Sons inherited yanakuna status.

**反 Whig 警告**: popular media (David Macaulay 类儿童书 / Disney 类) 把 Inca 浪漫化成「福利国家」— 国家管理粮仓, 没人挨饿, 没人无家可归。学术看法 (D'Altroy 2002《The Incas》/ Catherine Julien 2000): Inca 帝国确实有粮仓 (qollqa) 救灾 + 老弱病残保障 — 这部分是真。但 mit'a 死矿 / mitmaq 切根 / yanakuna 永奴 也是真。两面都是真, 不是「Inca 好 vs Inca 坏」的二选一。

**Anti-Whig caution**: Popular media (David Macaulay-style children's books / Disney) romanticizes the Inca as a "welfare state" — state-managed granaries, nobody starving, nobody homeless. Scholarship (D'Altroy 2002 *The Incas*; Julien 2000): the Inca did maintain *qollqa* (granaries) for famine relief + provisions for the elderly / sick / disabled — this part is real. But mit'a mining deaths / mitmaq uprooting / yanakuna lifelong servitude are also real. Both are true; not a "good Inca vs bad Inca" binary.

**Lens 3 setup anchor**: Lens 3 主角 Ix Mutal 是 Yucatán Postclassic Maya 平民, 不是 Inca 子民 — 但 「平民被国家工程的 receiving end 是什么感觉」这个问题, 在 Andean mit'a 系统里跟在 Aztec flower-war 边境 + Maya 农民 cargo 里, 是同一类问题。Part 3 在 Ix Mutal 视角的时候, 这一段 setup 可调用。

**Lens 3 setup anchor**: Lens 3 protagonist Ix Mutal is a Yucatán Postclassic Maya commoner, not an Inca subject — but the question "what does it feel like to be on the receiving end of state engineering" is the same class of question in Andean mit'a, Aztec flower-war frontier, and Maya peasant cargo systems. Part 3, viewing through Ix Mutal, will draw on this setup.

### §6.5 1472 Pachacuti 死 · 木乃伊还参政

1472 年, Pachacuti 死了, 54 岁。在位 34 年。

In 1472, Pachacuti died at 54. Reign: 34 years.

继位的是他选定的儿子 **Topa Inca Yupanqui** (1471-1493 在位, 父亲死前已共治) — 不是嫡长子, 是 Pachacuti 看中的能扩张帝国的儿子。Topa Inca 已经在父亲在位时北征到了今厄瓜多尔基多 (Quito) 一带, 父亲死后继续南征到智利中部 Maule 河 (那里碰到 Mapuche 部落 — Mapuche 没被征服, Inca 帝国南界停在 Maule 河)。

His chosen successor was son **Topa Inca Yupanqui** (reigned 1471-1493; co-ruled with his father before death) — not the eldest legitimate son, but the one Pachacuti judged capable of empire-expansion. Topa Inca had already pushed north to today's Quito, Ecuador during his father's reign; after his father's death he pushed south to the Maule River in central Chile (where the Mapuche stopped them — Mapuche were never conquered; the empire's southern border held at the Maule).

**panaca 木乃伊王室**: 这里有个 反 Whig 必讲的细节。Pachacuti 死后, 他的尸体被 木乃伊化 — 不是埋进墓里, 是 保存在 Cusco 城里, 由他生前的家族 (panaca) 持续供奉。木乃伊有自己的房子, 自己的田地 (生前的封地继续是「他」的), 自己的仆人 (yanakuna 继续服务)。重大节日, 木乃伊被抬出来参加仪式, 「跟」其他活着的王坐一起。

**panaca royal mummy**: Here is an anti-Whig essential. After death, Pachacuti's body was *mummified* — not buried, but kept in Cusco, perpetually venerated by his lineage household (*panaca*). The mummy had its own house, its own estates (his living-era lands remained "his"), its own servants (yanakuna continued service). On major festivals, the mummy was carried out to participate, "sitting" alongside living kings.

这不是 superstition / 迷信 — 是宪政结构。每一任 Sapa Inca 死后木乃伊化 + 自己的 panaca 永久占有自己生前的财产 → 新任 Sapa Inca 上台时, 前任的财产他不能动 (前任「还在」) → 新任只能 重新征服新土地 凑自己的财产。这是 Inca 帝国为什么 不停扩张 的制度动因 (Maria Rostworowski 1999《History of the Inca Realm》指出这一点)。

This was not superstition — it was constitutional structure. Each Sapa Inca, on death, was mummified + their panaca permanently retained their living-era estates → the new Sapa Inca could not touch the predecessor's wealth (predecessor was "still there") → the new ruler had to *conquer fresh territory* to build his own estate. This was a structural driver of Inca *perpetual expansion* (Maria Rostworowski 1999 *History of the Inca Realm* makes this point).

careful framing: 这个制度 1532 西班牙人到 Cusco 时 还在运作 — 11 任 Sapa Inca 的木乃伊都还在 (Pizarro 后来下令毁掉 1559)。Catherine Julien 2000 + Carolyn Dean 2010 都强调: Inca 帝国对欧洲人来说 不是「死的过去」, 而是「死人还在治国的现在」— 这是认知冲击。

careful framing: This system was still operating when the Spanish reached Cusco in 1532 — 11 Sapa Inca mummies still present (Pizarro ordered them destroyed in 1559). Julien 2000 + Dean 2010 emphasize: the Inca Empire, to Europeans, was not "the dead past" but "the dead still ruling the present" — a cognitive shock.

## §7 Aztec Triple Alliance 1428-1500 — 全盛

### §7.1 1428 Triple Alliance 立盟

跟 Inca Pachacuti 1438 几乎同时, 中美洲 Mexico Valley 也在搞国家工程。

Almost simultaneously with Inca Pachacuti's 1438 founding, central Mexico ran its own state-engineering project.

1428 年, Mexica 部落 (Tenochtitlán 城邦的居民, 「Aztec」是后世名, 当时他们叫自己 Mexica) 联合 Texcoco + Tlacopan 两个城邦, 推翻了 Tepanec 帝国 (首都 Azcapotzalco)。三方立 **Triple Alliance**「三边联盟」, Tenochtitlán 是大头, Texcoco 第二, Tlacopan 小弟。

In 1428, the Mexica (residents of Tenochtitlán; "Aztec" is a later term, they called themselves Mexica) allied with Texcoco + Tlacopan to overthrow the Tepanec Empire (capital Azcapotzalco). The three founded the **Triple Alliance**, with Tenochtitlán dominant, Texcoco second, Tlacopan junior.

第一任联盟统治者是 **Itzcoatl** (1427-1440) — Tenochtitlán 第 4 任 tlatoani (「说话者」, 城邦统治者称号)。Itzcoatl 做了一件影响深远的事: 烧了旧历史书, 重写 Mexica 起源神话 — 把 Mexica 从「Tepanec 附庸」改写成「太阳神 Huitzilopochtli 选民, 注定征服世界」。

The first alliance ruler was **Itzcoatl** (1427-1440) — Tenochtitlán's 4th *tlatoani* ("speaker," ruler title). Itzcoatl did something with long shadow: he burned the old history books and rewrote Mexica origins — recasting the Mexica from "Tepanec vassals" to "chosen people of the sun god Huitzilopochtli, destined to conquer the world."

**careful framing 跨 Inca cross-ref**: Itzcoatl 1428 烧史 + Pachacuti 1438 自封 Inti 之子 — 都是新兴帝国的「合法性工程」, 同种范式不同大陆。Inga Clendinnen 1991《Aztecs: An Interpretation》: 这种「新创建的古老」是国家工程典型动作。

**Cross-Inca framing**: Itzcoatl's 1428 history-burn + Pachacuti's 1438 Son-of-the-Sun claim — both are emerging-empire "legitimacy engineering," same paradigm, different continents. Inga Clendinnen 1991 (*Aztecs: An Interpretation*): this "newly invented antiquity" is a hallmark state move.

跟 Inca 不同点: Aztec Triple Alliance 不是单一帝国 — 是城邦联盟, 三个 tlatoani 并存 (虽然 Tenochtitlán 越来越独大)。Inca 是 单一 Sapa Inca。

Difference from Inca: the Aztec Triple Alliance was *not* a unitary empire — it was a city-state coalition, three tlatoani coexisting (though Tenochtitlán grew increasingly dominant). The Inca had a single Sapa Inca.

### §7.2 Moctezuma I 1440-1469 · 帝国扩张

Itzcoatl 1440 死, 侄子 **Moctezuma I** (Motecuhzoma Ilhuicamina, 「天空射箭者」) 接任。Moctezuma I 在位 29 年 (1440-1469), 把 Triple Alliance 从 Mexico Valley 一个区域势力, 扩张到了从 Veracruz 海岸 (东) 到 Oaxaca 高原 (南) 到 Pacific 海岸 (西) 的中美洲大半。

Itzcoatl died in 1440; his nephew **Moctezuma I** (Motecuhzoma Ilhuicamina, "He Shoots Arrows at the Sky") took the throne. Moctezuma I reigned 29 years (1440-1469), expanding the Triple Alliance from a Mexico-Valley regional power to a Mesoamerican span — Veracruz coast (east) to Oaxaca highlands (south) to Pacific coast (west).

但有一个区域他征服不了: 东边山地的 Tlaxcala 联盟 (4 城邦联盟)。Tlaxcala 跟 Aztec 同语 (Nahuatl), 同文化, 但拒绝臣服。Moctezuma I 多次进攻, 都失败。

But one area resisted: the Tlaxcala confederation (4 city-states) in the eastern highlands. Tlaxcala spoke the same language as the Aztec (Nahuatl), shared the culture, but refused submission. Moctezuma I attacked repeatedly, failed.

**Xochiyaoyotl 花战 / flower wars**: 征服不了, 怎么办? Moctezuma I 跟 Tlaxcala 立了一个奇怪的协议 — 仪式化战争。约定时间 + 约定地点, 双方军队在中间空地打, 主要目的不是占领土地, 是 抓俘虏 — 双方都需要俘虏作血祭。Nahuatl 称这种为 **Xochiyaoyotl**, 「花战」(xochitl = 花, yaoyotl = 战) — 「花」隐喻战士的鲜血与生命。

**Xochiyaoyotl flower wars**: Cannot conquer? Moctezuma I and Tlaxcala struck an odd compact — *ritualized warfare*. Set time, set place, armies met on neutral ground; primary goal was not territory but *capturing prisoners* — both sides needed captives for blood sacrifice. Nahuatl called it **Xochiyaoyotl**, "flower war" (*xochitl* = flower, *yaoyotl* = war) — "flower" a metaphor for the blood and life of warriors.

**careful framing**: popular media 把 flower wars 简化成「Aztec 嗜血」。学术 (Ross Hassig 1988《Aztec Warfare: Imperial Expansion and Political Control》) 看法是 flower wars 是 多功能制度:
1. 政治威慑: Tlaxcala 包在 Aztec 领土里, 长期消耗 Tlaxcala 人口
2. 军事训练: Aztec 年轻战士在 flower wars 里实战训练
3. 仪式经济: 持续供应俘虏维持血祭体系
4. 外交平衡: 不全灭 Tlaxcala (省得边界失控), 也不放弃压力

**careful framing**: popular media reduces flower wars to "bloodthirsty Aztecs." Scholarship (Ross Hassig 1988 *Aztec Warfare: Imperial Expansion and Political Control*) reads flower wars as a *multipurpose institution*: (1) political attrition — Tlaxcala enclosed in Aztec territory, long-term population drain; (2) military training — young Aztec warriors gained combat experience in flower wars; (3) ritual economy — sustained captive supply for sacrifice; (4) diplomatic balance — not annihilating Tlaxcala (avoid frontier collapse) while maintaining pressure.

这个 flower-war 协议在 1519 Cortés 来到 Veracruz 时还在运作 — Tlaxcala 80 年屈居 Aztec 包围中, 这是为什么 Tlaxcala 1519 年立刻跟 Cortés 结盟反 Aztec 的 真正原因 (后续 Lens 2 Malintzin 视角 Part 3 会详写)。

This flower-war compact was *still operating* in 1519 when Cortés arrived at Veracruz — Tlaxcala spent 80 years encircled by Aztecs, which is the *real reason* Tlaxcala allied with Cortés against the Aztec in 1519 (Part 3, Lens 2 Malintzin will detail this).

### §7.3 Aztec 血祭 careful framing

这一段必须 careful framing — 因为 popular knowledge 在这里扭曲最大。

This section requires careful framing — popular knowledge is most distorted here.

**popular media 数字**: 1487 年, Tenochtitlán **Templo Mayor** (大神殿) 落成扩建仪式, 据说 4 天里屠 80,000 战俘。这个数字来自 16 世纪西班牙人记录 — Bernal Díaz del Castillo 1568《Historia verdadera de la conquista de la Nueva España》和 Diego Durán 1581《Historia de las Indias de Nueva España》。

**Popular media figure**: In 1487, Tenochtitlán's **Templo Mayor** dedication allegedly involved sacrificing 80,000 captives over 4 days. This number comes from 16c Spanish accounts — Bernal Díaz del Castillo 1568 (*Historia verdadera de la conquista de la Nueva España*) and Diego Durán 1581 (*Historia de las Indias de Nueva España*).

**学术估计**: Inga Clendinnen 1991《Aztecs》+ Ross Hassig 1988 + Michael Harner 1977 之间的学术辩论得出的合理范围是: Aztec 帝国全境 一年总血祭量 ~10,000-20,000 人, 不是单一仪式 4 天 80,000。1487 Templo Mayor 仪式可能有几千人 (大事件), 但「80,000」是 16c 西班牙人为了证明征服正当性 而放大的数字 (类比: 任何征服文献都倾向放大被征服文化的「野蛮」)。

**Scholarly estimate**: The debate among Inga Clendinnen 1991 (*Aztecs*), Ross Hassig 1988, and Michael Harner 1977 yields a reasonable range: empire-wide total ~10,000-20,000 sacrifices per year, not 80,000 in a single 4-day rite. The 1487 Templo Mayor event likely involved a few thousand (a major occasion), but "80,000" is a 16c Spanish figure inflated to justify conquest (as conquest literature universally inflates the conquered's "savagery").

**careful framing 不是「不血祭」**: 是「数字 popular vs 学术 差 4-5 倍」+ 「血祭是国家工程的一部分, 不是单纯的宗教狂热」。

**careful framing is NOT "no sacrifice"**: it is "numbers differ 4-5x between popular and scholarly" + "sacrifice was part of state engineering, not mere religious frenzy."

血祭的国家逻辑 (Clendinnen 1991): Aztec 宇宙观认为太阳每天升起需要血供养; 不血祭 → 太阳不升 → 世界毁灭。Sapa Inca 是「Inti 之子」, Mexica tlatoani 是「Huitzilopochtli 的代理」— 都把国家权力嵌进宇宙运转。打仗 → 抓俘虏 → 血祭 → 太阳升 → 帝国合法。

The state logic of sacrifice (Clendinnen 1991): Aztec cosmology held the sun rose daily because of blood sustenance; without sacrifice → sun fails → world ends. The Sapa Inca was "Son of Inti"; the Mexica tlatoani was "agent of Huitzilopochtli" — both wove state power into cosmic operation. War → captives → sacrifice → sun rises → empire legitimate.

**反 Whig 跨 Topic cross-ref Spanish Inquisition**: 同期 (Topic: Renaissance and Reformation 1300-1648), 西班牙 Tomás de Torquemada 1483 年成立宗教裁判所, 1483-1498 烧死 ~2,000 异端 (历史学家 Henry Kamen 1997《The Spanish Inquisition》估计), 加上 1492 年驱赶 ~80,000 犹太人 (Sephardim)。Iberian 国家工程也以大量人命为代价。这不是 「whataboutism / 你也是」 — 是 让 1480-1500 年代每个国家工程都被同等审视 — 反 Whig 的核心要求。

**Anti-Whig cross-Topic Spanish Inquisition**: Contemporaneously (Topic: Renaissance and Reformation 1300-1648), Spain's Tomás de Torquemada founded the Inquisition in 1483; 1483-1498 ~2,000 heretics burned (Henry Kamen 1997 *The Spanish Inquisition* estimate), plus the 1492 expulsion of ~80,000 Sephardic Jews. Iberian state engineering also extracted massive human cost. This is *not* whataboutism — it is *equal scrutiny of every 1480-1500 state project*, which is the core anti-Whig demand.

### §7.4 chinampa · Pochteca · 商业体系

血祭是 Tenochtitlán 的一面, 商业 + 农业是另一面。1500 时 Tenochtitlán 人口 ~25 万, 是当时世界最大城市之一 (跟巴黎 ~22 万 / 伊斯坦布尔 ~30 万 同级)。

Sacrifice is one face of Tenochtitlán; commerce + agriculture is the other. By 1500, Tenochtitlán's population was ~250,000 — among the world's largest cities (alongside Paris ~220,000, Istanbul ~300,000).

25 万人在湖中岛上, 怎么吃饱? 答案是 **chinampa** — 漂浮园。

How feed 250,000 on a lake island? Answer: **chinampa** — floating gardens.

**chinampa 漂浮园**: 不是真的漂浮, 是 lake-bottom 沉积建造的人工岛 — 用 wattle (木栅) 围出长方形, 填湖底淤泥 + 水生植物根, 上面种玉米 / 豆 / 南瓜 / 番茄 / 辣椒 / 苋菜。湖水从四面渗进园里, 永远湿润, 一年三季收成。Chinampa 网在 Tenochtitlán 周围 Lake Texcoco 南部 + Lake Xochimilco (今墨西哥城南) 覆盖 ~9,000 公顷。

**chinampa floating garden**: Not actually floating — lake-bottom-anchored artificial islands. Wattle frames define rectangles, filled with lake mud + aquatic plant roots; planted with maize / beans / squash / tomato / chili / amaranth. Lake water seeps in from all sides, perpetual moisture, three harvests per year. The chinampa network covered ~9,000 hectares around southern Lake Texcoco + Lake Xochimilco (today's southern Mexico City).

**Pochteca 商人阶层**: Aztec 跟 Inca 不同 — 有 跨 Mesoamerica 长程商人。**Pochteca** Nahuatl 词, 来自「pochtli」(一种树) — 商人公会自封名称。Pochteca 不是普通商人, 是 半官方 — 既做生意 (运 obsidian 黑曜石 / jade 翡翠 / quetzal 鸟羽毛 / cacao 可可豆), 也兼任间谍 (帝国扩张前先派 pochteca 探察)。

**Pochteca merchant class**: Unlike the Inca, the Aztec had *long-distance merchants spanning Mesoamerica*. **Pochteca**, a Nahuatl word from *pochtli* (a tree), was the merchants' guild name. Pochteca were not ordinary traders — they were semi-official, doing business (obsidian / jade / quetzal feathers / cacao beans) and doubling as intelligence agents (sent ahead to scout before imperial expansion).

**货币**: Aztec 有商品货币 — cacao 豆 (做巧克力的可可) 是小额货币 ("一只兔子 = 100 cacao 豆"), cotton mantle (cotton 毛布大件) 是大额货币 ("一个奴隶 = 100 mantles")。Inca 没有货币 — 一切是国家分配 + ayllu 互惠。这是两个文明的根本经济结构差。

**Currency**: Aztec used commodity currency — cacao beans (the chocolate cacao) for small purchases ("one rabbit = 100 cacao beans"), cotton mantles (large cloth) for large ("one slave = 100 mantles"). The Inca had *no* currency — everything was state distribution + ayllu reciprocity. A fundamental structural divergence between the two civilizations.

**careful framing 跨 Topic Mali**: Aztec Pochteca + cacao-bean 货币 跟 Mali 帝国 (Topic: West African Empires) Mansa Musa 时代 trans-Saharan 商人 + cowrie-shell 货币是同期 (15c) 同类型经济。三大洲都跑「长程商人 + 商品货币」体系。

**Cross-topic Mali**: Aztec Pochteca + cacao currency parallels Mali Empire (Topic: West African Empires) Mansa Musa-era trans-Saharan merchants + cowrie-shell currency, contemporaneously (15c), same type of economy. Three continents ran "long-distance trader + commodity currency" systems.

## §8 Postclassic Maya 1450 — Mayapan 崩 · 区域分散

### §8.1 Mayapan 崩

Yucatán 半岛 (今墨西哥东南 + 危地马拉 / 伯利兹) 的 Maya 文明经历过两次大转折:

The Yucatán Peninsula's (today's southeast Mexico + Guatemala / Belize) Maya civilization went through two major shifts:

1. **Classic Maya 崩 ~900 年** — 南部 Petén 雨林大城 (Tikal / Palenque / Calakmul) 在 8-9 世纪相继被废弃, 人口南迁北迁 + 政体分散。原因争论中: 旱灾 (Richardson Gill 2000) + 战争 + 生态 + 贵族体系崩 (David Webster 2002) 综合作用 (Part 1 §3 详写)。

1. **Classic Maya collapse ~900** — southern Petén rainforest cities (Tikal, Palenque, Calakmul) abandoned across the 8th-9th centuries, populations migrating + polities fragmenting. Causes debated: drought (Richardson Gill 2000) + warfare + ecological strain + elite-system breakdown (David Webster 2002) in combination (Part 1 §3 details).

2. **Postclassic 重整 ~1000-1450** — 政治中心北移到 Yucatán, Chichén Itzá (10-13c) → Mayapán (13-15c) 相继成为区域霸主。

2. **Postclassic regrouping ~1000-1450** — political center shifted north to Yucatán; Chichén Itzá (10-13c) → Mayapán (13-15c) successively dominated the region.

1450 年, Mayapán 内部权力斗争爆发。两大家族 — **Cocom** (mayor 派) 和 **Xiu** (副派) — 撕破脸。Xiu 家族突袭 Cocom 主城, 杀光 Cocom 男系成员 (一个 Cocom 出差去洪都拉斯做买卖, 逃过, 回来后建了新家)。

In 1450, Mayapán erupted in internal power struggle. Two great houses — **Cocom** (dominant) and **Xiu** (junior) — broke. The Xiu raided the Cocom precinct, slaughtering male Cocom (one Cocom, away on a trade trip to Honduras, survived and returned to rebuild).

Mayapán 被废弃。Yucatán 没有出现下一个统一中心 — 而是分裂为 ~16 个区域城邦 (kuchkabal), 每个由本地 batab (城邦头) 治理。这个分散状态一直持续到 1517 西班牙人 Hernández de Córdoba 第一次到 Yucatán 海岸。

Mayapán was abandoned. No successor unified Yucatán — instead, it fragmented into ~16 regional polities (*kuchkabal*), each ruled by a local *batab* (city-state head). This dispersed configuration persisted until 1517, when the Spanish Hernández de Córdoba first reached the Yucatán coast.

### §8.2 Postclassic 平民日常 — Lens 3 setup

**反 Whig 必讲**: popular media 把 Postclassic Maya 描绘为「衰落」— 不再有 Classic 时代的金字塔大城, 不再有长字符碑文, 所以「文明在等待终结」。学术看法 (Susan Kepecs 2003《Postclassic Maya Economy and Society》/ Marilyn Masson 2002《In the Realm of Nachan Kan》): Postclassic Maya 是 不同形态的稳定 — 政治分散 + 农业稳定 + 区域贸易网仍密集 + 文化连续 (Maya 语言 / 历法 / 神话 / 织布技术全部传承到 16c 西班牙人来时, 一直传到 21c 现存 600 万 Maya 后裔)。

**Anti-Whig essential**: popular media depicts Postclassic Maya as "in decline" — no more Classic-era pyramid metropolises, no more long-form glyph stelae, so "civilization awaiting its end." Scholarship (Susan Kepecs 2003 *Postclassic Maya Economy and Society*; Marilyn Masson 2002 *In the Realm of Nachan Kan*): Postclassic Maya was a *different stable form* — politically dispersed + agriculturally stable + regional trade networks dense + culturally continuous (Maya language / calendar / mythology / weaving all transmitted to 16c Spanish arrival, and on to today's 6 million Maya descendants).

**Yucatán 农村日常** (Lens 3 主角 Ix Mutal 1500 年 ~20 岁的世界):

**Yucatán village daily life** (the world of Lens 3 protagonist Ix Mutal at ~20 in 1500):

**milpa 三姐妹**: Maya 平民种 milpa — 「milli」(田) + 「-pan」(在...上) Nahuatl + Maya 混词。一块 milpa 种 三姐妹: 玉米 + 豆 + 南瓜。玉米杆给豆藤攀, 豆固氮养玉米, 南瓜叶覆盖地面保湿压草。一块 milpa 种 2-3 年, 然后 swidden (休耕) 8-10 年, 让地恢复。

**milpa three sisters**: Maya commoners farmed *milpa* — Nahuatl-Maya hybrid term, *milli* (field) + *-pan* (on). One milpa held the *three sisters*: maize + beans + squash. Maize stalks supported bean vines; beans fixed nitrogen feeding maize; squash leaves blanketed soil retaining moisture and suppressing weeds. A milpa was cultivated 2-3 years, then *swiddened* (fallowed) 8-10 years for recovery.

**huipil 妇女织布**: 妇女从童年学 back-strap loom (背带织机) — 一头绑在树, 一头绑在腰, 用身体张力织布。织 **huipil** (上衣, 长方形布对折开领) + skirt + manta (披巾)。每件 huipil 的 神话纹样 — 玉米神 / 雨神 Chaac / 双头蛇 — 编进图案, 不是装饰是 史册。

**huipil women's weaving**: Women learned the *back-strap loom* from childhood — one end tied to a tree, the other to the waist, using body tension to weave. They wove the **huipil** (tunic, rectangular cloth folded with neck slit) + skirt + *manta* (shawl). Each huipil's *mythological motifs* — maize god, rain god Chaac, two-headed serpent — were woven in as pattern, not decoration but *chronicle*.

**careful framing**: huipil 织布技术从 Classic Maya (1c-9c) 一直传到今天 21c — Yucatán 农村 abuela (祖母) 还在教 nieta (孙女) 织 huipil。这是 Lens 3 Ix Mutal anchor 之一: 她织的 huipil 跟 21c 学生看到的 huipil 是 直接同根的同一项技艺。这给 Grade 7 「Maya 文明没消失, 在你身边」的 anchor。

**careful framing**: The huipil weaving technique transmitted from Classic Maya (1c-9c) to today's 21c — Yucatán village *abuelas* still teach *nietas* huipil weaving. This is a Lens 3 Ix Mutal anchor: the huipil she weaves is *the same continuous craft* a 21c student sees. Anchor for Grade 7 "Maya civilization did not vanish, it is next to you."

## §9 1500 时空切片 — 三大文明 + 跨大洋

1500 年 1 月 1 日, 美洲三大文明 都不知道 一个东西即将到来。

January 1, 1500. The Americas' three great civilizations did *not know* something was coming.

### §9.1 三大文明 1500 状态

**Aztec Triple Alliance**:
- 在位 tlatoani: Ahuitzotl (1486-1502, 第 8 任 Tenochtitlán tlatoani)
- 即将即位: Moctezuma II (1502-1520, 第 9 任, 1519 接见 Cortés 那位)
- 帝国范围: ~600 万人, Mexico Valley + Veracruz + Oaxaca + Pacific 海岸
- 城市: Tenochtitlán ~25 万人

**Aztec Triple Alliance**: reigning tlatoani Ahuitzotl (1486-1502, 8th Tenochtitlán tlatoani); soon to take throne Moctezuma II (1502-1520, the one who would receive Cortés in 1519); empire ~6 million people, Mexico Valley + Veracruz + Oaxaca + Pacific coast; capital Tenochtitlán ~250,000.

**Inca Tawantinsuyu**:
- 在位 Sapa Inca: **Huayna Capac** (1493-1527, 第 11 任 Sapa Inca, Pachacuti 孙)
- 帝国范围: ~1,200 万人, 厄瓜多尔 → 智利中部, 长 4,000 公里
- 首都: Cusco ~10 万人 + 第二都 Quito (Huayna Capac 偏爱)

**Inca Tawantinsuyu**: reigning Sapa Inca **Huayna Capac** (1493-1527, 11th Sapa Inca, Pachacuti's grandson); empire ~12 million people, Ecuador → central Chile, 4,000 km long; capital Cusco ~100,000 + secondary capital Quito (favored by Huayna Capac).

**Postclassic Maya**:
- 政治: ~16 个 kuchkabal 城邦, 无统一中心
- 总人口: ~80 万 (Yucatán) + ~40 万 (Guatemala 高原) ≈ 120 万 Maya 语者
- 状态: 农业稳定, 区域贸易, 政治分散

**Postclassic Maya**: politically ~16 kuchkabal city-states, no unifying center; total population ~800,000 (Yucatán) + ~400,000 (Guatemala highlands) ≈ 1.2 million Maya speakers; condition: agriculturally stable, regional trade active, politically dispersed.

### §9.2 三个 lens 主角 1500 状态

**Lens 1 Pachacuti**: 1472 已死 28 年。木乃伊在 Cusco 自己的 panaca 之家, 每月被请出来参加王室仪式, 坐在 Huayna Capac (孙) 旁边「商议国事」。

**Lens 1 Pachacuti**: dead 28 years. His mummy resided in his Cusco panaca household; monthly, he was carried out to royal ceremonies, "seated" beside grandson Huayna Capac, "consulting on matters of state."

**Lens 2 Malintzin**: 1500 年 0 岁, 即将出生 (Veracruz 边境 Coatzacoalcos 区, Aztec 帝国南端 + Maya 影响北端的 中间地带, 双语 Nahuatl + Yucatec Maya 边界)。1500-1505 之间出生, 历史学家 Camilla Townsend 2006《Malintzin's Choices》估计 1500-1502 出生概率最高。

**Lens 2 Malintzin**: in 1500, age 0, about to be born (Veracruz border, Coatzacoalcos region — between Aztec southern frontier and Maya northern reach, bilingual zone of Nahuatl + Yucatec Maya). Born 1500-1505; historian Camilla Townsend (*Malintzin's Choices*, 2006) estimates 1500-1502 most likely.

**Lens 3 Ix Mutal**: 1500 年 ~20 岁, Yucatán 西部一个 kuchkabal 农村。已结婚, 有一个孩子, 织 huipil, 种 milpa, 拜 Chaac 雨神。她不知道有「Aztec」存在 (太远), 不知道有「Inca」存在 (再远), 当然不知道「Spaniards」存在。

**Lens 3 Ix Mutal**: in 1500, age ~20, in a kuchkabal village in western Yucatán. Married, one child, weaving huipils, farming milpa, propitiating the rain god Chaac. She did not know "Aztecs" existed (too far away), did not know "Inca" existed (farther still), certainly did not know "Spaniards" existed.

### §9.3 同期欧洲 — 跨大洋 cross-ref

1500 年欧洲, 几个跟 Part 3 主角直接相关的人物:

In 1500 Europe, several figures directly bearing on Part 3 protagonists:

- **Francisco Pizarro** in 1500 was 22, soldiering in Extremadura, Spain (sailed for Hispaniola in 1502).
- **Bartolomé de las Casas** in 1500 was 16; his father had returned from Columbus's second voyage (1493-1496); Bartolomé himself went to Hispaniola in 1502 — later in 1542 he wrote *Brevísima relación de la destrucción de las Indias* (the humanitarian indictment of Spanish conquest, a core AP World primary source).

### §9.4 Grade 7 anchor — 「无 warning」

1500 年, 美洲三大文明全盛, 总人口估计 ~5,000-7,000 万 (Henry Dobyns 1966 高估 1 亿; William Denevan 1992《The Native Population of the Americas in 1492》中位数 ~5,400 万 — 比同期欧洲 ~7,800 万, 同级)。

In 1500, the Americas' three civilizations were at peak; total population estimated ~50-70 million (Henry Dobyns 1966 high estimate 100 million; William Denevan 1992 *The Native Population of the Americas in 1492* median ~54 million — comparable to contemporary Europe ~78 million).

Pachacuti 木乃伊在 Cusco「办公」, Moctezuma II 即将即位 Tenochtitlán, Ix Mutal 织 huipil, Malintzin 即将出生。没人知道, 32 年内 Aztec 都城被夷平 (1521 Tenochtitlán 陷), 53 年内 Inca 帝国被劫 (1533 Atahualpa 死), 75 年内 Yucatán 大部 Maya 沦陷 (1546 chichimeca 起义被压, 但 Itzá Maya 在 Lake Petén 一直坚持到 1697)。

Pachacuti's mummy "presided" in Cusco; Moctezuma II was about to take Tenochtitlán's throne; Ix Mutal was weaving huipils; Malintzin was about to be born. None knew that within 32 years the Aztec capital would be razed (Tenochtitlán fell 1521); within 53 years the Inca empire would be looted (Atahualpa killed 1533); within 75 years most Yucatán Maya would fall (chichimeca uprising suppressed 1546, though Itzá Maya at Lake Petén held until 1697).

**Grade 7 anchor**: 「文明 + 终结」之间没有 warning。1500 年的 Pachacuti 木乃伊不知道孙子的孙子 (Atahualpa) 33 年后会被陌生白人勒死。1500 年的 Ix Mutal 不知道她会活到见证西班牙船第一次出现 (1517 Hernández de Córdoba 到 Yucatán 海岸时她 ~37 岁)。

**Grade 7 anchor**: between "civilization peak" and "ending" there is *no warning*. In 1500, Pachacuti's mummy did not know that his grandson's grandson (Atahualpa) would be garroted by strange white men 33 years later. In 1500, Ix Mutal did not know she would live to see Spanish ships first appear (in 1517, when Hernández de Córdoba reached the Yucatán coast, she would be ~37).

历史不是「在它发生前可以预料的」— 是从结果倒推才看到 trajectory。读这段时, 把 1500 年的 三大文明 当作真实当下 — 不是「快被殖民了的过去」, 是「百万人口活在自己的中年」。

History is not "foreseeable before it happens" — trajectories are visible only working backward from outcomes. Read this section with the three civilizations of 1500 as a *real present* — not "the soon-to-be-colonized past," but "millions living their own middle age."

## §10 Malintzin 出生到 1519 (~70 行)
## §10 Malintzin's birth to 1519

### 10.1 Coatzacoalcos 边境女孩 / A girl on the Coatzacoalcos frontier

**~1500 出生 Coatzacoalcos.** Malintzin 出生在 Coatzacoalcos — 今 Veracruz 与 Tabasco 接壤的湿热低地, Aztec 帝国的东南边境。
**~1500, born in Coatzacoalcos.** Malintzin was born in Coatzacoalcos — today's wet hot lowland where Veracruz meets Tabasco, the southeastern frontier of the Aztec empire.

这块地是 Nahuatl (阿兹特克语) 与 Maya (玛雅语) 的语言交界。北面村子讲 Nahuatl, 南面讲 Yucatec Maya 或 Chontal Maya。Malintzin 母语是 Nahuatl, 但很小就听 Maya。
This land was the language border between Nahuatl (Aztec) and Maya. Villages to the north spoke Nahuatl, those to the south spoke Yucatec Maya or Chontal Maya. Malintzin's mother tongue was Nahuatl, but she heard Maya from a young age.

**父亲 cacique, 早死.** 据 Bernal Díaz del Castillo《Historia verdadera de la conquista de la Nueva España》(1568), 她父亲是当地 cacique (酋长) — 也就是说她出生贵族, 不是奴。
**Father a cacique, died early.** According to Bernal Díaz del Castillo's *Historia verdadera de la conquista de la Nueva España* (1568), her father was a local cacique (chief) — meaning she was born noble, not enslaved.

但父亲早死。母亲改嫁, 与新夫又生了一个儿子。Bernal Díaz 写: 母亲为了让新生儿继承家产, 把 Malintzin 卖给了过路商人, 对外宣称女儿死了。
But her father died early. Her mother remarried and had a son with the new husband. Bernal Díaz wrote: to make the newborn boy inherit the family estate, the mother sold Malintzin to passing merchants, telling everyone outside that the daughter had died.

**Careful framing — 不一定是「卖」.** Camilla Townsend《Malintzin's Choices》(University of New Mexico Press 2006) 提醒: Bernal Díaz 1568 年回忆这件事时离事件 49 年, 而且他写的是给西班牙读者看的「悲惨女主角」叙事 — 也可能 Malintzin 是被战争 captured 走的, 不是被母亲卖的。Townsend 倾向 captured 说。
**Careful framing — not necessarily "sold".** Camilla Townsend's *Malintzin's Choices* (University of New Mexico Press 2006) reminds us: Bernal Díaz wrote 49 years after the event, and he was crafting a "tragic heroine" narrative for Spanish readers — Malintzin may instead have been captured in war, not sold by her mother. Townsend leans toward captured.

总之, 8-10 岁的某一天, Malintzin 离开了 Coatzacoalcos, 进入奴隶网络。
Either way, on some day around age 8-10, Malintzin left Coatzacoalcos and entered the slave network.

### 10.2 转手数次, 1519 在 Tabasco / Passed through several hands, by 1519 in Tabasco

从 1510 左右到 1519, Malintzin 在 Mesoamerica 奴隶贸易里转手数次。从 Nahuatl 区域被卖到 Maya 区域 — 这就是为什么 1519 年她已经精通双语。
From around 1510 to 1519, Malintzin was traded several times through Mesoamerica's slave network. She was sold from a Nahuatl-speaking region into a Maya-speaking region — which is why by 1519 she was fluent in both languages.

到 1519 年初, 她在 Tabasco 一个 Chontal Maya 部落, 大约 19 岁。
By early 1519, she was with a Chontal Maya group in Tabasco, around 19 years old.

**1519.3 Tabasco 战.** 1519 年 3 月, Hernán Cortés 率船队从 Cuba 出发, 第一站是 Tabasco 沿海。Tabasco Maya 与西班牙人短战, Maya 败 — 西班牙人有铁剑、马、火枪、钢甲, Maya 是黑曜石矛和棉甲。
**March 1519, the battle of Tabasco.** In March 1519, Hernán Cortés led a fleet from Cuba, his first stop the Tabasco coast. The Tabasco Maya fought the Spaniards briefly and lost — the Spanish had iron swords, horses, muskets, steel armor; the Maya had obsidian-tipped spears and cotton armor.

战败后 Tabasco cacique 送了 20 个奴隶妇女给 Cortés 作和解礼物。Malintzin 是其中之一。
After the defeat, the Tabasco cacique gave Cortés 20 enslaved women as a peace offering. Malintzin was one.

### 10.3 1519.4 受洗 Marina / April 1519, baptized Marina

1519 年 4 月, Cortés 把这 20 个女人受洗成 Christian, 分给手下西班牙军官。Malintzin 受洗名是「Marina」 — 西班牙女人常用名。
In April 1519, Cortés had these 20 women baptized Christian and distributed among his Spanish officers. Malintzin's baptismal name was "Marina" — a common Spanish woman's name.

最初她被分给 Alonso Hernández Portocarrero。但 Cortés 几天内就发现 Malintzin 会说 Nahuatl + Maya, 而且学得快, 立刻把她调到自己身边作翻译。
At first she was given to Alonso Hernández Portocarrero. But within days Cortés realized Malintzin spoke Nahuatl + Maya and learned fast, and pulled her to his own side as interpreter.

**名字三次变.** 一生中她的名字变了三次:
- ~1500-1519 出生原名 (失传 — 没有任何史料记录)
- 1519 后 Nahuatl 人称她 Malintzin (「Marina」+ Nahuatl 尊称 -tzin)
- 1820s Mexico 独立后被叫 La Malinche, 含义从中性翻译变成「叛徒」(Townsend 2006)
**Three name changes in one life.** Through her life, her name changed three times:
- ~1500-1519 birth name (lost — no source records it)
- After 1519, Nahuatl speakers called her Malintzin ("Marina" + Nahuatl honorific -tzin)
- After 1820s Mexican independence, she was called La Malinche, with the meaning shifted from neutral interpreter to "traitor" (Townsend 2006)

**反 Whig framing.** 她不是「叛徒」 — 1519 年她 19 岁, 已经是奴隶 11 年, 没有「祖国」可背叛。她也不是「英雄」 — 她是被卖被战俘被赠的女奴, 翻译是她唯一能换取生存空间的技能。Townsend 2006 的核心论点: Malintzin 是「在没有选择的处境里做了她能做的选择」(Malintzin's choices)。
**Anti-Whig framing.** She wasn't a "traitor" — at 19 in 1519, she had been enslaved for 11 years and had no "homeland" to betray. She wasn't a "hero" either — she was a sold-captured-gifted slave girl, and interpreting was the one skill that bought her survival space. Townsend 2006's central argument: Malintzin "made the choices she could in a situation with no real choice."

## §11 Ix Mutal 1500-1517 — Lens 3 setup (~80 行) ⭐
## §11 Ix Mutal 1500-1517 — Lens 3 setup

### 11.1 Yucatán 后 Mayapan 时代 / Post-Mayapan Yucatán

**~1480 出生.** Ix Mutal 出生在 Yucatán 半岛北部一个小村, 距 Mayapan 旧城遗址几日路。
**Born ~1480.** Ix Mutal was born in a small village in northern Yucatán, a few days' walk from the abandoned ruin of Mayapan.

(回忆 Part 2 §6: Mayapan 1441-1461 内乱崩塌, 之后 Yucatán 是「碎片化政治」状态 — 16-18 个独立 cuchcabal/小邦, 没有一个统一中心。)
(Recall Part 2 §6: Mayapan collapsed in civil war 1441-1461, after which Yucatán entered "fragmented politics" — 16-18 independent cuchcabal/petty states, no single center.)

她父亲是 milpa 农夫, 种玉米/豆/南瓜。母亲织 huipil (Maya 女性传统长袍)。Ix Mutal 7 岁开始学织 huipil, 12 岁会独立织一件完整的婚礼 huipil。
Her father farmed milpa — maize, beans, squash. Her mother wove huipil (Maya women's traditional long tunic). Ix Mutal began learning at 7, and by 12 could weave a complete wedding huipil on her own.

### 11.2 Chilam Balam 口传 / The Chilam Balam oral tradition

她村子有一位老 chilam (祭司) — 记得 Mayapan 没崩之前的事, 也记得族里世代口传的预言。
Her village had an old chilam (priest) — who remembered the time before Mayapan fell, and who carried generations of oral prophecy.

Maya《Chilam Balam》文本 (16-17 世纪 Yucatán Maya 用拉丁字母写下来的口传记录, 不是 Pre-Columbian 文物本身, 是被 Spanish 征服后 Maya 自己用新字母记录下的旧口传) 里有大量预言式段落: 哪一个 katun (20 年周期) 会有饥荒, 哪一个会有外来人, 哪一个会有疾病。
The *Chilam Balam* texts (16-17 century records written by Yucatec Maya in Latin script, not pre-Columbian artifacts but old oral material recorded with the new alphabet after Spanish conquest) contain extensive prophetic passages: which katun (20-year cycle) brings famine, which brings foreigners, which brings disease.

Ix Mutal 小时候听老 chilam 念过这些。她不全懂, 但她记得「外来 bearded 人」(foreign bearded ones) 这个意象。
Ix Mutal heard the old chilam recite these as a child. She didn't understand it all, but she remembered the image of "foreign bearded ones."

**Careful framing.** Restall《Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest》(Oxford 2003) 警告: 不要把 Maya 预言读成「他们预知西班牙人来」 — 预言文本是 1540-1600 间写的, 含有 retrospective rationalization (事后合理化)。但 Maya 确实有 katun 周期论传统, 这是真的。
**Careful framing.** Restall's *Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest* (Oxford 2003) warns: don't read Maya prophecy as "they foresaw the Spanish coming" — the prophetic texts were written 1540-1600 and contain retrospective rationalization. But the Maya did have a real katun-cycle tradition.

### 11.3 1517 第一批船 / 1517, the first ships

**1517 年 2 月**, Francisco Hernández de Córdoba 率三艘小船从 Cuba 出发, 第一次接触 Yucatán 北岸 — Cape Catoche 附近。
**February 1517**, Francisco Hernández de Córdoba led three small ships from Cuba and made first European contact with northern Yucatán at Cape Catoche.

Maya 与他们短战, Maya 用箭和投石把 Hernández 重伤, 西班牙人撤回 Cuba — Hernández 几周后因伤死。第一次接触, Maya 算「赢」。
The Maya fought them briefly. Hernández was gravely wounded by arrows and slingstones; the Spaniards retreated to Cuba and Hernández died of his wounds weeks later. In this first contact, the Maya "won."

但村里的人开始互相讲: 看到了从未见过的船, 看到了 bearded 人。Ix Mutal 那时 ~37 岁, 她听了, 她记住了。
But village people began to tell each other: ships never seen before, bearded men. Ix Mutal was about 37, she heard, she remembered.

**1518.4-6 Grijalva 第二批.** Juan de Grijalva 率四艘船第二次来, 沿 Yucatán 海岸南下到 Tabasco — 这就是 1519 Cortés 走的同一路线侦察。Grijalva 这次没大战, 但接触更广。
**1518.4-6, Grijalva's second expedition.** Juan de Grijalva led four ships, sailing south along Yucatán to Tabasco — the route Cortés would follow in 1519 as scouting. Grijalva didn't have major battles but made wider contact.

### 11.4 1518-1520 第一波天花 / 1518-1520, the first smallpox wave

接触后疾病开始扩散。1518 年起 Caribbean (Hispaniola/Cuba) 已经天花流行, 沿船带到 Yucatán 海岸。
After contact, disease began to spread. From 1518 smallpox was already raging in the Caribbean (Hispaniola/Cuba), and was carried along the ships to the Yucatán coast.

**Ix Mutal 见证.** ~1519-1520, 她 39-40 岁, 看到家里两个孩子病死, 看到邻村空了一半。她不懂细菌不懂免疫, 但她看出: 这病只杀我们, 不杀 bearded 人。
**Ix Mutal witnessed.** Around 1519-1520, age 39-40, she saw two of her own children die, saw a neighboring village half-emptied. She didn't understand germs or immunity, but she saw: this sickness only kills us, not the bearded ones.

**死亡数字 careful framing.** Noble David Cook《Born to Die: Disease and New World Conquest, 1492-1650》(Cambridge 1998) 估计: 1518-1600 期间美洲原住民因天花/麻疹/流感/伤寒等欧亚病原体死亡 80-95%。这是 estimate range, 因为 Pre-Columbian 总人口本身就有 800 万到 1 亿不等的估计 (Cook 偏中高), 但 80-95% 死亡率是学界中位共识。
**Mortality figures, careful framing.** Noble David Cook's *Born to Die: Disease and New World Conquest, 1492-1650* (Cambridge 1998) estimates 1518-1600 indigenous American mortality from smallpox/measles/influenza/typhus at 80-95%. This is an estimate range — Pre-Columbian total population is itself estimated from 8 million to 100 million (Cook leans middle-high) — but the 80-95% mortality rate is the scholarly median consensus.

**跨 Topic cross-ref ⭐.** William McNeill《Plagues and Peoples》(1976) 比较了 1346-1353 Black Death 与 1518-1600 New World epidemic — 结论: 同样 pattern (新病原 + 无免疫人群 + 高接触), 同样 25-50% (Black Death) 到 80-95% (New World) 死亡率。差异是 Black Death 后欧洲人活下来的人有部分免疫, 而美洲原住民有几十种新病原同时来, 没有时间形成免疫。
**Cross-Topic cross-ref ⭐.** William McNeill's *Plagues and Peoples* (1976) compared 1346-1353 Black Death and 1518-1600 New World epidemic — same pattern (new pathogen + non-immune population + high contact), same 25-50% (Black Death) to 80-95% (New World) mortality. Difference: Black Death survivors had partial immunity left in Europe; the Americas saw dozens of new pathogens arrive at once, with no time to build immunity. (See Black Death Topic Lens 3 for the European receiving-end side.)

### 11.5 Lens 3 voice setup / Lens 3 voice anchor

Ix Mutal 这里站定: 「我不是 Malintzin 那种 broker, 我也不是 Cortés, 我也不是 Moctezuma, 我也不是写历史的人。我是看见的、活下来的、记得的人。我女儿死了, 我哥哥死了, 我村里 9/10 的人死了。我的故事不在 Cortés 的信里, 不在 Bernal Díaz 的回忆里, 但它存在。」
Ix Mutal stands here: "I am not a broker like Malintzin, not Cortés, not Moctezuma, not the one who writes history. I am the one who saw, who survived, who remembers. My daughter died, my brother died, nine out of ten in my village died. My story is not in Cortés's letters, not in Bernal Díaz's memoir, but it exists."

**defaultLens 选她的理由.** Grade 7 学生最容易投射的不是征服者也不是 broker, 而是「在事件里活着但没有发言权」的人 — Ix Mutal 是那个 receiving-end 视角。
**Why she's the defaultLens.** Grade 7 students project most easily not onto conqueror or broker, but onto "someone who lives through events without a voice" — Ix Mutal is that receiving-end perspective.

## §12 Cortés 1519-1521 + Malintzin 角色 — Lens 2 主角 (~250 行) ⭐⭐⭐
## §12 Cortés 1519-1521 + Malintzin's role — Lens 2 protagonist

### §12.1 1519.3-7 第一批 expedition (~60 行)
### §12.1 March-July 1519, the first expedition

#### 12.1.1 Tabasco 登陆 + 接收 Malintzin / Tabasco landing + receiving Malintzin

**1519.3.4** Cortés 11 艘船 + ~500 西班牙人 + 16 匹马 + ~200 Cuban 原住民 + 数门小炮, 从 Cuba 出发。第一站 Cozumel 岛 (Yucatán 东海岸), 然后 1519.3 中旬到 Tabasco 河口 (今 Mexico 东南 Tabasco 州)。
**1519.3.4** Cortés set sail from Cuba with 11 ships, ~500 Spaniards, 16 horses, ~200 Cuban auxiliaries, and several small cannons. First stop Cozumel island (east coast of Yucatán), then mid-March 1519 to the Tabasco river mouth (today's Tabasco state, southeast Mexico).

Tabasco 战. 西班牙人 vs Maya, Maya 败 (黑曜石不破钢甲, 没见过马) → 1519.3 末 Tabasco cacique 送和解礼包括 20 个奴隶妇 → 1519.4 初 Cortés 把她们受洗给 Marina 等名字 → Cortés 发现 Malintzin 双语, 调到身边。
Battle of Tabasco. Spaniards vs Maya, Maya lost (obsidian couldn't break steel, never seen horses) → late March 1519, Tabasco cacique gave peace gift including 20 enslaved women → early April 1519, Cortés had them baptized as Marina etc. → Cortés discovered Malintzin's bilingualism and pulled her to his side.

#### 12.1.2 三角通信链 / The three-way translation chain

1519 年 4-7 月, Cortés 团队的通信链是这样的:
April-July 1519, the Cortés group's translation chain worked like this:

1. Cortés 说西班牙语 →
2. **Jerónimo de Aguilar** 听 (Aguilar 是 1511 年 Yucatán 沉船幸存者, 在 Maya 部落作奴 8 年, 1519.3 Cortés 在 Cozumel 接他出来, 他会西班牙语 + Yucatec Maya) → Aguilar 翻成 Yucatec Maya →
3. **Malintzin** 听 Maya, 翻成 Nahuatl →
4. Aztec 人或 Tlaxcala 人听 Nahuatl 回答 →
5. Malintzin 听 Nahuatl, 回 Maya →
6. Aguilar 听 Maya, 回西班牙语 →
7. Cortés 听。

1. Cortés spoke Spanish →
2. **Jerónimo de Aguilar** listened (Aguilar was a 1511 Yucatán shipwreck survivor, enslaved among the Maya for 8 years, picked up by Cortés at Cozumel in March 1519; he spoke Spanish + Yucatec Maya) → Aguilar translated to Yucatec Maya →
3. **Malintzin** heard Maya, translated to Nahuatl →
4. Aztec or Tlaxcala listener responded in Nahuatl →
5. Malintzin heard Nahuatl, translated back to Maya →
6. Aguilar heard Maya, returned to Spanish →
7. Cortés heard.

到 1519 年底, Malintzin 学会了西班牙语, 这条链缩成: Cortés ↔ Malintzin ↔ Nahuatl 对方。Aguilar 仍在但作用减小。
By late 1519, Malintzin had learned Spanish, and the chain shortened to: Cortés ↔ Malintzin ↔ Nahuatl speaker. Aguilar remained but his role shrank.

**Lens 2 lonely-mediator anchor.** Malintzin 是这条链的中间结点。每一次 Cortés 跟 Aztec 谈判, 每一次西班牙人警告或威胁或承诺, 都是从她嘴里出去。Aztec 一方记下她的脸她的声音 — Aztec 1540-1560 年代 Florentine Codex 里的图画, Malintzin 几乎总是站在 Cortés 旁边, 跟 Cortés 一样大, 而西班牙士兵被画得更小。
**Lens 2 lonely-mediator anchor.** Malintzin was the middle node of this chain. Every time Cortés negotiated with the Aztec, every time the Spaniards warned or threatened or promised, it came out of her mouth. The Aztec side remembered her face and voice — in Aztec 1540s-1560s Florentine Codex drawings, Malintzin almost always stands next to Cortés, drawn the same size as him, while Spanish soldiers are drawn smaller.

#### 12.1.3 1519.4-5 Veracruz 立基 + 烧船 / April-May 1519, founding Veracruz + scuttling the ships

**1519.4.22 Cortés 立 Villa Rica de la Vera Cruz** (今 Veracruz 港附近)。这是法律手段 — Cortés 出 Cuba 时是 Cuba 总督 Diego Velázquez 的 expedition leader, 法律上他不能擅自征服。但他立了一个新「市」, 让市政会 (cabildo) 选他为 captain general, 这就绕过 Velázquez, 直接对 Charles V 国王效忠。
**1519.4.22 Cortés founded Villa Rica de la Vera Cruz** (near today's Veracruz harbor). This was a legal maneuver — when Cortés left Cuba, he was expedition leader under Cuba governor Diego Velázquez and legally could not conquer on his own. By founding a new "town" and having the cabildo elect him captain general, he bypassed Velázquez and reported directly to King Charles V.

**1519.7-8 烧船.** Cortés 自己第二封信 (Carta de relación, 1520.10.30 给 Charles V) 里写明: 他下令把船弄沉/拆毁 — 不是「英雄烧船绝退路」myth, 是政治实操 (让手下不能溜回 Cuba 投 Velázquez)。
**1519.7-8, the ships scuttled.** Cortés's own second letter (Carta de relación, sent to Charles V on 1520.10.30) states clearly: he ordered the ships sunk or dismantled — not the "heroic burn the ships, no retreat" myth, but a practical political move (so his men couldn't sneak back to Cuba and side with Velázquez).

**反 Whig.** 这件事在浪漫历史叙事里被讲成「Cortés 像项羽破釜沉舟, 决心征服墨西哥」。实际是: 防止 desertion + 防止 Velázquez 派人来抓他。Hugh Thomas《Conquest》(Simon &amp; Schuster 1993) 详细论述这是 routine 西班牙征服法律操作, 不是个人英雄。
**Anti-Whig.** Romantic historical narrative tells this as "Cortés like Xiang Yu broke the cooking pots and sank the boats, resolved to conquer Mexico." Reality: prevent desertion + prevent Velázquez agents from arresting him. Hugh Thomas's *Conquest* (Simon & Schuster 1993) details this as routine Spanish conquest legal procedure, not personal heroism.

#### 12.1.4 1519.8-9 Tlaxcala 同盟 / August-September 1519, the Tlaxcala alliance

Cortés 从 Veracruz 内陆走 (~400km 山路) 朝 Tenochtitlán。第一个大障碍是 Tlaxcala — 一个独立的 Nahua 共和国 (4 个 lordships 联合), 长期反 Aztec。
Cortés moved inland from Veracruz toward Tenochtitlán (~400km of mountain roads). The first major obstacle was Tlaxcala — an independent Nahua republic (a confederation of 4 lordships) that had long resisted the Aztec.

**Tlaxcala 反 Aztec 历史.** 回忆 Part 2 §7.2: Aztec 与 Tlaxcala 打 flower wars 几十年 — Aztec 想要俘虏献祭, Tlaxcala 是仅存几个不被 Aztec 征服的 Nahua 邦。Tlaxcala 对 Aztec 仇恨 deep。
**Tlaxcala's anti-Aztec history.** Recall Part 2 §7.2: Aztec and Tlaxcala fought flower wars for decades — Aztec wanted captives for sacrifice, Tlaxcala was one of the few unconquered Nahua states. Tlaxcala's hatred of the Aztec ran deep.

**1519.9 短战 + 转盟.** Cortés 团队先与 Tlaxcala 战 ~3 周, 双方都死人, 但 Cortés 没溃。Tlaxcala 长老开会决议: 与其继续耗, 不如借西班牙人之力反 Aztec — 1519.9 中下旬转盟。
**1519.9, brief war then alliance flip.** Cortés's group fought Tlaxcala for ~3 weeks. Both sides took casualties, but Cortés didn't break. The Tlaxcala elders met and decided: rather than keep fighting, use the Spanish to attack the Aztec — they switched to alliance in mid-late September 1519.

**Malintzin 角色.** 从 Tlaxcala 谈判起, Malintzin 是核心。她是 Nahuatl native speaker, 跟 Tlaxcala 长老直接对话 — Aguilar 退到背后。Townsend 2006 强调: Tlaxcala 文献后来记她是「真正在场的西班牙代言人」(la lengua, 「the tongue」)。
**Malintzin's role.** From the Tlaxcala negotiations on, Malintzin was central. As a native Nahuatl speaker, she talked directly to the Tlaxcala elders — Aguilar receded. Townsend 2006 emphasizes: later Tlaxcala documents remember her as "the real Spanish spokesperson present" (la lengua, "the tongue").

**跨 Topic cross-ref.** Tlaxcala 反 Aztec 与西班牙结盟 — 这是「内部敌对集团联合外来势力打主敌」的 pattern, 跟 1099 First Crusade 时 Christian 与 Eastern Roman 联合反 Seljuk 同 pattern, 跟 1453 Constantinople 围城前 Byzantine 与 Genoa 商人合作同 pattern。内部分裂往往比外来力量更致命。
**Cross-Topic cross-ref.** Tlaxcala allying with the Spanish against the Aztec — this is the "internal hostile faction joins outside power against main enemy" pattern, same as 1099 First Crusade Christian-Byzantine alliance against the Seljuks, same as the Genoese-Byzantine cooperation in the 1453 Constantinople siege. Internal division is often more lethal than the outside force itself.

#### 12.1.5 1519.10 Cholula 大屠杀 / October 1519, the Cholula massacre

**1519.10 Cholula.** Tlaxcala 同盟之后, Cortés + Tlaxcala 联军继续走, 下一站 Cholula (今 Puebla 附近) — Cholula 是 Aztec 盟邦, 大宗教中心, 拥 Quetzalcoatl 大金字塔。
**October 1519, Cholula.** After the Tlaxcala alliance, Cortés + Tlaxcala forces continued. The next stop was Cholula (near today's Puebla) — an Aztec ally and major religious center with a great pyramid to Quetzalcoatl.

Cortés 进 Cholula 后 ~2 周, 怀疑 Cholula 准备 ambush — 据说 Malintzin 从一个 Cholula 老妇那儿得到情报: Cholula 与 Aztec 商定围杀西班牙人。
After ~2 weeks in Cholula, Cortés suspected an ambush — reportedly Malintzin learned from a Cholula old woman that Cholula and the Aztec had arranged to encircle and kill the Spaniards.

**1519.10 (具体日期史料分歧, 大约 16-18 号)** Cortés 召集 Cholula 贵族进中央广场, 然后命令屠杀 — 5,000-6,000 Cholultec 死, 大部分是手无寸铁的贵族和市民。
**1519.10 (exact date varies in sources, around 16-18)** Cortés summoned Cholula nobility into the central plaza and ordered a massacre — 5,000-6,000 Cholultecs killed, most unarmed nobles and citizens.

**Careful framing — Malintzin 角色争议.** 16 世纪 Spanish 记 (Bernal Díaz, Cortés 信) 把功劳/责任都给 Malintzin (「她从老妇得情报救了我们」)。这暗含两件事:
- 西班牙叙事是用 Malintzin 给屠杀的 self-defense 提供 justification (「我们是被迫先发」)
- Malintzin 实际有多大决定权 — 不清楚。她当时 19 岁, 是奴, 即使她传了情报, 决定屠杀的是 Cortés。

Townsend《Malintzin's Choices》2006 careful framing: 不要把 Malintzin 写成屠杀策划者 — 她可能传了情报, 但屠杀决定是西班牙的。也不要把她写成完全无辜 — 她确实是链上的一环。她的「choice」是: 在那个处境里, 她选择对她的主人忠诚 (因为反抗等于死)。

**Careful framing — controversy over Malintzin's role.** 16th-century Spanish records (Bernal Díaz, Cortés letters) gave the credit/responsibility to Malintzin ("she got intel from an old woman and saved us"). This implies two things:
- The Spanish narrative used Malintzin to justify the massacre as self-defense ("we were forced to strike first")
- How much real decision power Malintzin had is unclear. She was 19, enslaved; even if she passed the intel, the kill order was Cortés's.

Townsend's *Malintzin's Choices* 2006 careful framing: don't write Malintzin as the planner of the massacre — she may have passed intel, but the decision was Spanish. Don't write her as wholly innocent either — she was a real link in the chain. Her "choice" was: in that situation, she chose loyalty to her master (because resistance meant death).

**Cholula 屠杀效果.** Aztec 帝国震动 — 一个西班牙 vs Aztec-allied 城的屠杀, 让其他 Aztec 城邦看到「西班牙不是商人是军队」。Moctezuma II 在 Tenochtitlán 收到消息, 派使者带礼物给 Cortés, 试图劝退 — 没用。
**Effect of the Cholula massacre.** It shook the Aztec empire — a single Spanish-led massacre of an Aztec-allied city showed other Aztec cities that "the Spanish are not merchants but an army." Moctezuma II in Tenochtitlán received the news and sent envoys with gifts to Cortés, trying to persuade him to turn back. It didn't work.

### §12.2 1519.11 Tenochtitlán 入城 (~50 行)
### §12.2 November 1519, entering Tenochtitlán

#### 12.2.1 1519.11.8 入城 / November 8, 1519, entry

**1519.11.8** Cortés + Malintzin + ~300 西班牙残部 + ~1,000 Tlaxcala 同盟 走过 Iztapalapa causeway 进入 Tenochtitlán — Aztec 帝国首都, 当时人口 ~200,000-250,000, 比 1519 年欧洲任何城市都大 (London ~50,000, Paris ~200,000)。
**1519.11.8** Cortés + Malintzin + ~300 remaining Spaniards + ~1,000 Tlaxcala allies walked across the Iztapalapa causeway into Tenochtitlán — the Aztec capital, population at the time ~200,000-250,000, larger than any European city in 1519 (London ~50,000, Paris ~200,000).

Bernal Díaz 1568 写: 「我们看见水中之城, 看见塔楼神庙白色发光, 我们中很多人怀疑这是不是梦境。」
Bernal Díaz wrote in 1568: "We saw the city in the water, we saw the towers and temples shining white, many of us doubted whether this was a dream."

#### 12.2.2 Moctezuma II 接见 / Moctezuma II's reception

**1519.11.8** Moctezuma II 在城南 causeway 边接 Cortés。Moctezuma 当时 ~52 岁, 是 Aztec 第 9 任 huey tlatoani, 1502 年即位, 帝国版图鼎盛。
**1519.11.8** Moctezuma II met Cortés at the southern causeway. Moctezuma was ~52, the 9th Aztec huey tlatoani, having taken the throne in 1502, with the empire at its territorial peak.

Moctezuma 给 Cortés 礼物 (黄金、羽毛披风、玉器), 让他们住进市中心一座大宫殿。
Moctezuma gave Cortés gifts (gold, feathered cloaks, jade), and lodged them in a large central palace.

#### 12.2.3 Quetzalcoatl 神兆 myth — careful framing ⭐
#### 12.2.3 The Quetzalcoatl-omen myth — careful framing ⭐

后世西班牙叙事讲: Moctezuma 把 Cortés 误认为是 Quetzalcoatl (羽蛇神) 回归 — Quetzalcoatl 神话里说他从东方海上来, Cortés 正好从东方海上来, Moctezuma 因此犹豫不杀。
Later Spanish narrative claimed: Moctezuma mistook Cortés for the returning Quetzalcoatl (Feathered Serpent) — myth said Quetzalcoatl would come from the eastern sea, and Cortés came from the eastern sea, so Moctezuma hesitated to kill.

**反 Whig — 这是 post-conquest myth.** Matthew Restall《Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest》(Oxford 2003) 第三章详细论证: Quetzalcoatl-Cortés identification 在 1519 年 Moctezuma 当时的实际想法里没有证据 — 这个故事最早出现在 1540s-1550s 的 Florentine Codex (Sahagún 编), 是被殖民后的 Aztec 长辈用来给 Moctezuma 的失败找一个「神意」解释 (这样 Moctezuma 不是软弱, 是被神话困住)。
**Anti-Whig — this is a post-conquest myth.** Matthew Restall's *Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest* (Oxford 2003) chapter 3 argues in detail: the Quetzalcoatl-Cortés identification has no evidence in Moctezuma's actual 1519 thinking — the story first appears in the 1540s-1550s Florentine Codex (Sahagún), used by post-conquest Aztec elders to give Moctezuma's failure a "divine" explanation (so Moctezuma wasn't weak, just trapped by prophecy).

实际更可能: Moctezuma 是政治家, 他在评估 — Cortés 是敌人但兵力不大, Tlaxcala 同盟是真正的威胁。Moctezuma 选择拖延和谈判, 不是因为神话, 而是因为他在看局势。
More likely actual reason: Moctezuma was a politician, assessing — Cortés was an enemy but not large in force; Tlaxcala alliance was the real threat. Moctezuma chose delay and negotiation not from myth but from reading the situation.

#### 12.2.4 软禁 6 个月 / Six months of house arrest

**1519.11.14 起.** Cortés 入城 ~6 天后, 找了一个借口 (Veracruz 一队西班牙人被 Aztec 边境贵族杀了), 把 Moctezuma 软禁 — Moctezuma 名义上还是皇帝, 实际在西班牙看守下。
**From 1519.11.14.** ~6 days after entry, Cortés used a pretext (some Spaniards had been killed by an Aztec frontier lord near Veracruz) to put Moctezuma under house arrest — nominally still emperor, in practice under Spanish guard.

软禁持续 1519.11.14 - 1520.6.30 ~6.5 个月。期间 Moctezuma 继续发命令但都过 Cortés 审。Aztec 城中贵族越来越不满。
House arrest lasted from 1519.11.14 to 1520.6.30, roughly 6.5 months. Moctezuma continued issuing orders but only through Cortés's filter. The Aztec nobility grew increasingly resentful.

Malintzin 全程在场, 全程翻译 — 每一次 Moctezuma 与 Cortés 之间的话, 是从她嘴里过的。
Malintzin was present and translating throughout — every word between Moctezuma and Cortés passed through her mouth.

### §12.3 1520.5-7 La Noche Triste (~60 行)
### §12.3 May-July 1520, La Noche Triste

#### 12.3.1 1520.5 Cortés 离城去打 Narváez / May 1520, Cortés leaves to fight Narváez

**1520.5 初.** Cuba 总督 Velázquez 派 Pánfilo de Narváez 率 ~900 西班牙人 + 80 马 + 10 几艘船到 Veracruz, 任务: 抓 Cortés 回 Cuba 受审 (Cortés 1519 立 Vera Cruz 绕过 Velázquez 是非法的)。
**Early May 1520.** Cuba governor Velázquez sent Pánfilo de Narváez with ~900 Spaniards + 80 horses + 10+ ships to Veracruz with orders to arrest Cortés and bring him back to Cuba for trial (Cortés's 1519 founding of Vera Cruz had been illegal).

Cortés 决定亲自去打 Narváez。他留 Pedro de Alvarado 守 Tenochtitlán (大约 80 西班牙 + Tlaxcala 几百), 自己带剩下 ~250 西班牙南下。
Cortés decided to fight Narváez personally. He left Pedro de Alvarado holding Tenochtitlán (~80 Spaniards + several hundred Tlaxcala) and led ~250 Spaniards south.

**1520.5.27-28** Cortés 夜袭 Narváez 营 (Cempoala), Narváez 失一眼被俘, 大部分 Narváez 部下转投 Cortés (Cortés 用钱和承诺 Mexico 黄金诱惑) — Cortés 现在有 ~1,300 西班牙人。
**1520.5.27-28** Cortés night-attacked the Narváez camp at Cempoala, Narváez lost an eye and was captured, most of his men switched to Cortés (lured by money and promises of Mexican gold) — Cortés now had ~1,300 Spaniards.

#### 12.3.2 Alvarado Templo Mayor 屠杀 / Alvarado's Templo Mayor massacre

但 Cortés 不在期间, Tenochtitlán 出大事。
But while Cortés was gone, disaster struck Tenochtitlán.

**1520.5 Toxcatl 节.** Aztec 一个大宗教节日, Templo Mayor (大金字塔) 上贵族跳舞唱歌。Alvarado 怀疑 (或者借口) Aztec 准备 uprising, 1520.5.20 左右下令士兵关广场出口, 然后屠杀正在跳舞的 ~300-600 贵族。
**1520.5, the Toxcatl festival.** A great Aztec religious festival; nobility danced and sang on the Templo Mayor. Alvarado suspected (or pretexted) an uprising and ordered soldiers to seal the plaza exits around May 20 1520, then massacred ~300-600 dancing nobles.

**反 Whig.** 与 Cholula 屠杀类似, Alvarado 屠杀的「先发制人」justification 在后世史料里被反复检讨 — Cortés 自己 1520.10 信里都对 Alvarado 决定有保留。Hugh Thomas 1993 评: Alvarado 是 Cortés 团队里最暴的, 他做这事多半是 paranoid + greed (节日上贵族戴金饰品)。
**Anti-Whig.** Like Cholula, the "preemptive strike" justification has been repeatedly questioned — even Cortés in his October 1520 letter showed reservations about Alvarado's decision. Hugh Thomas 1993: Alvarado was the most violent on Cortés's team and did this mostly out of paranoia + greed (the nobles wore gold).

Tenochtitlán 全城起义 — Aztec 围攻西班牙营。Moctezuma (软禁中) 被推上城墙劝百姓退, 被自己人扔石头砸中, 几天后死 (1520.6.29) — careful framing: 死因争议, 西班牙说 Aztec 石头打死, 一些 Aztec 来源说西班牙看 Moctezuma 已无用就杀了他。
The whole city revolted — Aztecs besieged the Spanish quarters. Moctezuma (under house arrest) was pushed onto the walls to plead with the people, was struck by stones from his own subjects, and died days later (1520.6.29) — careful framing: cause of death disputed, the Spanish said Aztec stones killed him, some Aztec sources say the Spaniards killed him after he became useless.

#### 12.3.3 Cortés 回城 + 1520.6.30 La Noche Triste / Cortés returns + the Night of Sorrows

**1520.6.24** Cortés 带 ~1,300 西班牙 + ~2,000 Tlaxcala 急行军回 Tenochtitlán, 进城 — 但发现城已围, 只能跟 Alvarado 部汇合, 继续被围。
**1520.6.24** Cortés led ~1,300 Spaniards + ~2,000 Tlaxcala in a forced march back to Tenochtitlán and entered — but found the city in revolt, could only link up with Alvarado's group, still besieged.

**1520.6.30 夜** Cortés 决定撤退 — 从西边 Tlacopan causeway 出城。但 Aztec 已切断 causeway 桥段, 西班牙人需要带 portable 木桥跨过缺口。
**Night of 1520.6.30** Cortés decided to retreat — out via the western Tlacopan causeway. But Aztec had cut bridge sections; the Spaniards had to carry portable wood bridges to cross gaps.

夜里下雨, Aztec 发现, 从两边独木舟攻击 — 西班牙挤在 causeway 上像鸭子被射。携带的金子 (从 Moctezuma 处掠来的) 拖累, 很多人沉湖死。
It rained that night, the Aztec spotted them, attacked from canoes on both sides — the Spaniards crammed on the causeway like sitting ducks. The gold they carried (looted from Moctezuma's stores) weighed them down, many drowned.

**死亡数字.** 西班牙 ~600+ 死 (~半数 Cortés 出 Cuba 时带的 + Narváez 部), Tlaxcala 几千死, 16 匹马里大部分死 (一些被 Aztec 俘后献祭), 大部分掠的金沉湖。
**Casualties.** Spanish ~600+ killed (about half of those Cortés brought from Cuba + Narváez's men), several thousand Tlaxcala killed, most of the 16 horses killed (some captured and sacrificed by the Aztec), most looted gold sunk in the lake.

**Malintzin 也险死.** 她在出城队伍里, 但活下来 — 怎么活下来史料不详, 可能跟 Cortés 同骑或在受保护中心。
**Malintzin barely survived.** She was in the retreating column and lived — how is unclear, possibly riding with Cortés or in the protected center.

Cortés 据说在出城后一棵 ahuehuete 树下哭了一夜 — 「La Noche Triste」「悲痛之夜」名字由此 (Bernal Díaz 1568 记, careful framing: 这棵树在 Mexico City Tacuba 区现在还有, 但这个细节可能是 1568 浪漫化)。
Cortés reportedly wept all night under an ahuehuete tree — hence the name "La Noche Triste" "Night of Sorrows" (Bernal Díaz 1568, careful framing: the tree still exists in Mexico City's Tacuba district, but the detail may be 1568 romanticization).

#### 12.3.4 1520.7-1521.5 Tlaxcala 重整 / July 1520 - May 1521, regrouping in Tlaxcala

剩下 ~440 西班牙 + Tlaxcala 同盟撤回 Tlaxcala — Tlaxcala 没背叛, 接纳他们。
The remaining ~440 Spaniards + Tlaxcala allies retreated to Tlaxcala — Tlaxcala did not betray them, took them in.

10 个月里 Cortés 重整:
- Veracruz 不断有 Cuba 来的西班牙人补给 (传 Mexico 有金子, 雇佣兵 + 商人不断来)
- Tlaxcala 木匠造 13 艘 brigantine (中型双桅船) — 计划是船陆联攻 Tenochtitlán (Tenochtitlán 是湖中之城, 没船纯陆攻很难)
- Aztec 这边: Moctezuma 1520.6.29 死 → Cuitláhuac 即位 (Moctezuma 弟弟) → 1520.6-12 在位 6 个月 → 1520.12 天花死 → Cuauhtémoc 即位 (Moctezuma 侄子, 22 岁)
Over 10 months Cortés regrouped:
- Constant Spanish reinforcements from Cuba via Veracruz (rumors of Mexican gold pulled mercenaries and merchants)
- Tlaxcala carpenters built 13 brigantines (medium two-masted ships) — plan was a combined ship-land assault on Tenochtitlán (Tenochtitlán was a lake city, pure land assault was very hard)
- On the Aztec side: Moctezuma died 1520.6.29 → Cuitláhuac (Moctezuma's brother) took the throne → reigned June-December 1520 (6 months) → died of smallpox in December 1520 → Cuauhtémoc took the throne (Moctezuma's nephew, age 22)

### §12.4 1521.5-8 Tenochtitlán 围城 + 陷落 (~50 行)
### §12.4 May-August 1521, the siege and fall of Tenochtitlán

#### 12.4.1 1521.5 围城开始 / May 1521, the siege begins

**1521.4-5** Cortés 把 13 艘 brigantine 拆零件运过山, 在 Texcoco (湖东岸) 重组下水。这是工程奇迹 — 几百 Tlaxcala 民工抬着船板木走 ~80km 山路。
**April-May 1521** Cortés had the 13 brigantines disassembled, hauled across mountains, reassembled and launched at Texcoco (eastern lake shore). It was an engineering feat — hundreds of Tlaxcala porters carried planks ~80km of mountain road.

**1521.5.22 围城开始.** Cortés 把军分三路堵三条 causeway (Tacuba 北, Iztapalapa 南, Coyoacan 西南), 13 艘 brigantine 控湖面, 切水切粮。
**1521.5.22 the siege begins.** Cortés divided his force into three columns blocking three causeways (Tacuba north, Iztapalapa south, Coyoacan southwest); the 13 brigantines controlled the lake surface, cutting water and food.

军队规模: ~900 西班牙 (10 个月不断补给) + ~150,000 同盟原住民 (Tlaxcala + 后期更多 Aztec 不满附庸城邦倒戈) vs Tenochtitlán 内 ~80,000 Aztec 战士 + 平民。
Force size: ~900 Spaniards (after 10 months of reinforcement) + ~150,000 allied indigenous (Tlaxcala plus more disaffected Aztec tributary cities defecting later) vs ~80,000 Aztec warriors + civilians inside Tenochtitlán.

**反 Whig.** 注意比例 — 「西班牙征服 Mexico」这个说法误导。实际是 ~150,000 原住民 + 900 西班牙打 ~80,000 原住民。Restall 2003 强调: 这是 Mesoamerican civil war + Spanish 加成, 不是 Spanish solo conquest。
**Anti-Whig.** Note the ratio — "Spanish conquered Mexico" is misleading. Reality: ~150,000 indigenous + 900 Spaniards fought ~80,000 indigenous. Restall 2003 stresses: this was a Mesoamerican civil war + Spanish leverage, not a Spanish solo conquest.

#### 12.4.2 天花第二波 / The second smallpox wave

**1520.10-1521.3 天花扫过 Tenochtitlán.** 这是 La Noche Triste 后, 西班牙撤退给城里留下的更狠的武器。
**October 1520 - March 1521, smallpox swept Tenochtitlán.** This was the more lethal weapon left behind by the Spanish retreat after La Noche Triste.

Aztec 1540s Florentine Codex 描述: 「人不能动、不能吃、不能起、不能转身... 整身充满 pustules... 许多人就这样死在床上...」
Aztec 1540s Florentine Codex describes: "People could not move, eat, rise, turn... bodies were covered with pustules... many died right where they lay..."

Cuitláhuac 死于此 (1520.12)。Tenochtitlán 城内死 ~30-40% 人口 — 死的多是带免疫指挥的中老贵族, 留下的多是年轻没指挥经验的。
Cuitláhuac died of it in December 1520. Tenochtitlán lost ~30-40% of its population — disproportionately older command-experienced nobles, leaving younger inexperienced fighters.

**Ix Mutal 这时在 Yucatán** (~ 41 岁), 也在第二波。Yucatán + Mexico 高原同步在被同一场天花扫。
**Ix Mutal at this time in Yucatán** (~ age 41), also in the second wave. Yucatán + Mexican plateau were swept by the same epidemic in parallel.

#### 12.4.3 1521.5-8 75 天攻城 / 75-day attack May-August 1521

5 月底到 8 月初, 75 天的逐街战。Aztec 在 Cuauhtémoc 指挥下顽抗 — 城已残但战斗到最后。
Late May to early August, 75 days of street fighting. Under Cuauhtémoc the Aztec resisted fiercely — the city was broken but fought to the last.

Cortés 战术: 每天进城拆一片建筑用石头填运河, 让骑兵能跑 — 一周拆几个街区。Aztec 战术: 用独木舟从未拆区出击, 拖落单西班牙人献祭。
Cortés's tactic: each day enter the city, demolish a sector, fill canals with rubble so cavalry could move — clearing a few blocks a week. Aztec tactic: canoes from un-demolished zones sortied out, dragging isolated Spaniards back for sacrifice.

**1521.6-7** 西班牙看到从 Templo Mayor 顶被俘同伴被剥皮献祭。Bernal Díaz 1568 写: 「我们听见 conch 号, 看见我们的人被拖上去... 听见心被切出来献给神...」
**June-July 1521** Spaniards watched captured comrades being skinned and sacrificed atop the Templo Mayor. Bernal Díaz 1568: "We heard the conch trumpet, saw our men dragged up... heard hearts cut out for the gods..."

#### 12.4.4 1521.8.13 陷落 / 1521.8.13 the fall

**1521.8.13** Cuauhtémoc 试图独木舟逃, 被 brigantine 拦截俘虏。Aztec 抵抗结束。
**1521.8.13** Cuauhtémoc tried to escape by canoe, was intercepted and captured by a brigantine. Aztec resistance ended.

**死亡总数 careful framing.** 75 天围城中:
- Aztec 死 ~100,000-240,000 (战死 + 饿死 + 病死, estimate range, Hugh Thomas 1993 偏 240,000)
- 西班牙战死 ~100 + 围城前夕 + 围城中 (Cortés 自己第三封信 1522 给 Charles V 写 ~50, careful framing 西班牙倾向少报)
- Tlaxcala + 同盟死 ~30,000+
- Total Mesoamerica 这三年 (1519-1521) 死 ~30-40% 人口 (高原 Aztec 区 + Yucatán 合算)
**Total casualties, careful framing.** During the 75-day siege:
- Aztec deaths ~100,000-240,000 (combat + starvation + disease, estimate range, Hugh Thomas 1993 leans 240,000)
- Spanish combat deaths ~100 in/near the siege (Cortés's third letter to Charles V 1522 says ~50, careful framing — Spanish tended to under-report)
- Tlaxcala + allied indigenous dead ~30,000+
- Total Mesoamerica three-year (1519-1521) population loss ~30-40% (Aztec plateau + Yucatán combined)

**跨 Topic cross-ref ⭐⭐⭐.** Tenochtitlán 1521.8.13 陷落 与 Constantinople 1453.5.29 陷落 — 两个不同大陆但同 pattern:
- 都是「千年城」(Tenochtitlán 200 年, Constantinople 1100 年, Tenochtitlán 量级小但当时是 Mesoamerica 中心)
- 都是被新军事技术攻破 (Constantinople 被 Mehmed II 巨炮; Tenochtitlán 被 Cortés brigantine + 钢甲 + 同盟)
- 都是文明 epoch 终结 (Constantinople 后 Byzantine 千年帝国结束; Tenochtitlán 后 Aztec 帝国结束 + Mesoamerica entire pre-Columbian 政治体系崩塌)
- 都有内部分裂 critical (Constantinople 时 Eastern + Western Christianity 不合; Tenochtitlán 时 Tlaxcala 等附庸联西班牙)
**Cross-Topic cross-ref ⭐⭐⭐.** The fall of Tenochtitlán 1521.8.13 and the fall of Constantinople 1453.5.29 — different continents, same pattern:
- Both "millennial cities" (Tenochtitlán 200 yrs, Constantinople 1100 yrs; smaller scale for Tenochtitlán but it was Mesoamerica's center then)
- Both broken by new military tech (Constantinople by Mehmed II's giant cannons; Tenochtitlán by Cortés's brigantines + steel + allies)
- Both ended a civilizational epoch (after Constantinople, the thousand-year Byzantine empire; after Tenochtitlán, the Aztec empire and the entire pre-Columbian Mesoamerican political system)
- Both had critical internal division (Constantinople: Eastern + Western Christianity at odds; Tenochtitlán: Tlaxcala and other tributaries siding with the Spanish)

**跨 Topic cross-ref 印刷术.** Cortés 1519-1526 写了 5 封 Cartas de relación 给 Charles V。第二封 (1520) 1522 在 Seville 印刷, 第三封 (1522) 1523 印, 后续 1524-1525 都印 — Mesoamerica 征服叙事在欧洲 1522-1525 就大量传播, 因为 Gutenberg 1455 印刷术 70 年后已经成熟。这是「征服叙事的全球传播」第一次靠印刷术 — 印刷术不只让 Reformation 1517-1521 蔓延, 也让 Conquest 1519-1521 在欧洲变成大众故事。
**Cross-Topic cross-ref, the printing press.** Cortés wrote 5 Cartas de relación to Charles V from 1519-1526. The second (1520) was printed in Seville in 1522, the third (1522) printed 1523, the rest printed 1524-1525 — the Mesoamerica conquest narrative spread widely in Europe between 1522-1525 because Gutenberg's printing press, 70 years on, was now mature. This was the first time a "conquest narrative" went global through print — print didn't just carry the Reformation 1517-1521 across Europe, it also carried the Conquest 1519-1521 into European popular storytelling.

### §12.5 Malintzin 1521-1529 后期 + 死 (~30 行)
### §12.5 Malintzin 1521-1529, late life and death

#### 12.5.1 1522 生子 Martín / 1522, son Martín born

**1522.1 (大约)** Cortés 与 Malintzin 生子 Martín Cortés。这是有史可查最早的 mestizo (西班牙 + 美洲原住民混血) 之一 — 不是「第一个」(其他征服者已经有更早), 但是最有名的, 因为 Cortés 后来正式认子, Martín 上 Spanish 贵族圈。
**~January 1522** Cortés and Malintzin had a son, Martín Cortés. He was one of the earliest historically recorded mestizos (Spanish + indigenous American) — not "the first" (other conquistadors had earlier ones), but the most famous because Cortés later formally legitimized him, and Martín entered Spanish noble circles.

**跨 Topic cross-ref.** Mestizo 第一代 1522 — 跟 Mali Aisha 1413 商人女传承 (West Africa Topic, Aisha 是商人混血女传承家族 trans-Saharan 网络) 同 pattern: 帝国边境上, 「混血 mediator 子女」是新文明的桥梁也是受害者。Martín Cortés 后来 1568 因「conspiracy」被 Spain 流放, 死异乡 — 边境 mediator 后代往往两边都不被完全接受。
**Cross-Topic cross-ref.** First-generation mestizo 1522 — same pattern as Mali Aisha 1413 the merchant's daughter (West Africa Topic, Aisha as mixed-blood female heir of a trans-Saharan trading family): on imperial frontiers, "mixed mediator children" become bridges of a new civilization and also its victims. Martín Cortés was later exiled from Spain in 1568 for "conspiracy" and died abroad — descendants of frontier mediators often belong fully to neither side.

#### 12.5.2 1525-1526 Honduras 远征 / 1525-1526 Honduras expedition

**1524-1526** Cortés 率队下 Honduras (一个反叛西班牙副官)。Malintzin 随行翻译。
**1524-1526** Cortés led an expedition into Honduras (chasing a rebel Spanish lieutenant). Malintzin came along as interpreter.

**1525.2.28** 在 Acalan (今 Tabasco/Campeche), Cortés 处死 Cuauhtémoc — 借口 Cuauhtémoc 谋反, 实际可能是消除 Aztec 复辟可能。Cuauhtémoc 死前据说说: 「我早该死在 Tenochtitlán。」
**1525.2.28** at Acalan (today's Tabasco/Campeche border), Cortés executed Cuauhtémoc — pretext was a Cuauhtémoc plot, real reason likely to eliminate Aztec restoration possibility. Cuauhtémoc reportedly said before death: "I should have died in Tenochtitlán long ago."

Malintzin 在场翻译 Cuauhtémoc 临终之言 — 她从 1519 到 1525 见证 Aztec 帝国从顶盛到完全终结。
Malintzin was there interpreting Cuauhtémoc's last words — she witnessed the Aztec empire's full arc from peak to ending, 1519 to 1525.

#### 12.5.3 1526 Cortés 婚 + Malintzin 嫁 / 1526 Cortés married + Malintzin married off

**1526** Cortés 在 Spain 与 Juana de Zúñiga 结婚 (西班牙贵族小姐, 政治婚姻)。同年 Cortés 安排 Malintzin 嫁给他部下西班牙军官 Juan Jaramillo。
**1526** Cortés married Juana de Zúñiga in Spain (Spanish noblewoman, political match). The same year, Cortés arranged Malintzin's marriage to his Spanish lieutenant Juan Jaramillo.

Malintzin 婚后住在 Mexico City (Tenochtitlán 旧址)。她得到一些土地分封 (encomienda 或 tribute rights), 在物质上有保障。
After marriage, Malintzin lived in Mexico City (the site of old Tenochtitlán). She received land grants (encomienda or tribute rights) and was materially secured.

#### 12.5.4 1529 死 / 1529, death

**1529 (大约)** Malintzin 死, ~29 岁。死因 + 具体日期史料模糊 — Townsend 2006 careful framing: 可能 1528 末 Mexico City 流行病时死, 没有明确墓址。
**~1529** Malintzin died, around age 29. Cause of death and exact date are unclear in the sources — Townsend 2006 careful framing: she may have died in a Mexico City epidemic in late 1528, no clear grave is known.

她生命 29 年里:
- 0-8 noble 女孩
- 8-19 奴隶, 至少 3 次主人易主
- 19-21 Cortés 翻译 + 同居 + 母亲
- 21-26 Cortés 助手 + Honduras 远征
- 26-29 西班牙军官妻 + 土地拥有者
- 死时她是 1521 后 Mexico 最知名的原住民女性, 但是 1820s 之后 (300 年后) 才被叫成「La Malinche 叛徒」 — 她活着时不是这个名声。
Across her 29 years:
- 0-8 noble girl
- 8-19 enslaved, sold at least 3 times
- 19-21 Cortés interpreter + concubine + mother
- 21-26 Cortés aide + Honduras expedition
- 26-29 Spanish officer's wife + landowner
- At her death she was the most famous indigenous woman in post-1521 Mexico, but only after the 1820s (300 years later) did she get called "La Malinche the traitor" — that wasn't her reputation while alive.

**跨 Topic cross-ref ⭐.** Malintzin 1500-1529 + Erasmus 1466-1536 — 两人都是「lonely mediator」: Erasmus 站在 Catholic + Protestant 之间用拉丁语和希腊语翻译《新约》(1516), 想避免 Reformation 分裂; Malintzin 站在 Spanish + Aztec 之间用 Nahuatl + Maya + Spanish 翻译, 在征服里活下来。两人都同代 (Erasmus 1517 印希腊语《新约》正是 Malintzin 17 岁还在奴隶网络转手时), 都不属于任何一方, 都被后世两边各取一面骂或捧。
**Cross-Topic cross-ref ⭐.** Malintzin 1500-1529 and Erasmus 1466-1536 — both "lonely mediators": Erasmus stood between Catholic and Protestant, using Latin and Greek to translate the New Testament (1516), trying to prevent Reformation rupture; Malintzin stood between Spanish and Aztec, using Nahuatl + Maya + Spanish, surviving inside conquest. They were contemporaries (Erasmus printed his Greek New Testament in 1517 just as Malintzin, age 17, was still being passed through the slave network), neither belonged to either side, and both were taken up afterwards from one side or another to praise or condemn.

## §13 Inca 1493–1532 后期 — 从扩张顶点到内战崩溃 / Late Inca: from peak to civil war

### 13.1 Huayna Capac 1493–1527 — 帝国的最后一次扩张 / The empire's last expansion

CN: 1493 年 Topa Inca Yupanqui 死, 他的儿子 Huayna Capac (「年轻的国王」, c. 1468–1527) 即位。这一年, 在大西洋彼岸, Columbus 刚结束第一次航行回到西班牙——但 Cusco 没人知道。Huayna Capac 把 Tawantinsuyu (「四方之地」, 即 Inca 帝国) 的疆域往北推到 Quito (今厄瓜多尔首都), 把行政中心一分为二: Cusco (南) + Quito (北)。这个决定, 30 多年后会撕裂帝国。

EN: In 1493, Topa Inca Yupanqui died and his son Huayna Capac ("the young king", c. 1468–1527) took the fringed headband. That same year, across the Atlantic, Columbus was returning from his first voyage—but no one in Cusco knew. Huayna Capac pushed Tawantinsuyu ("the four parts together"—the Inca name for their empire) north to Quito (today the capital of Ecuador), and split the administrative core in two: Cusco (south) + Quito (north). Thirty years later, that decision would tear the empire apart.

### 13.2 1525–1527 Quito 流行病 / The Quito epidemic

CN: 1525 年前后, Huayna Capac 在 Quito 北部前线遇到一场陌生的瘟疫。学界 (Noble David Cook《Born to Die: Disease and New World Conquest, 1492–1650》Cambridge 1998) careful framing: 这很可能是天花——从 Mexico 1520 Tenochtitlán 流行后, 沿巴拿马陆桥 + 沿海贸易网南传, 比 Pizarro 本人早到 Inca 6 年。注意: Cook 自己强调「probable, not certain」——因为 Inca 没有文字病例记录, 我们靠的是西班牙人后来 1550s 的口述采访 (Cieza de León, Sarmiento de Gamboa)。

EN: Around 1525, Huayna Capac was on the northern frontier in Quito when a strange plague hit. The careful scholarly framing (Noble David Cook, *Born to Die: Disease and New World Conquest, 1492–1650*, Cambridge 1998) is: probably smallpox, traveling south from the 1520 Tenochtitlán outbreak through the Panama land-bridge and coastal trade networks—arriving in the Inca heartland six years before Pizarro himself. Note: Cook stresses "probable, not certain"—because the Inca had no writing for medical records, we rely on Spanish oral interviews from the 1550s (Cieza de León, Sarmiento de Gamboa).

### 13.3 1527 Huayna Capac 突死 + 继承危机 / The succession crisis

CN: 1527 年 Huayna Capac 在 Quito 突然死于这场瘟疫。同一波瘟疫, 还杀死了他的嫡子 Ninan Cuyochi——本来已经被指定继位的人。两个法定继承人同时死亡, 帝国瞬间陷入空位。

EN: In 1527, Huayna Capac died suddenly in Quito from this plague. The same wave killed his designated heir, Ninan Cuyochi. Two legitimate successors gone at once—the empire fell into a vacuum.

### 13.4 1527–1532 Atahualpa vs Huáscar 内战 / Civil war between half-brothers

CN: Huayna Capac 还有两个儿子, 同父异母:
- **Huáscar** (南, Cusco 系, 嫡母) — Cusco 贵族支持
- **Atahualpa** (北, Quito 系, 母亲是 Quito 当地公主) — Quito 军队支持 (因为 Huayna Capac 最后 10 年都在 Quito, 军队跟 Atahualpa 走)

1527–1532 五年内战。1532 年 4 月, Atahualpa 的将领 Quizquiz 和 Chalcuchimac 在 Quipaipan (Cusco 附近) 大败 Huáscar 军, 俘虏了 Huáscar 本人。Atahualpa 当时正在北部 Cajamarca (今秘鲁北部山区小城) 温泉休养, 准备南下 Cusco 加冕。

EN: Huayna Capac left two more sons by different mothers:
- **Huáscar** (south, Cusco line, principal-wife mother) — backed by Cusco nobility
- **Atahualpa** (north, Quito line, mother was a Quito princess) — backed by the Quito armies (because Huayna Capac had spent his last decade in Quito, and the troops were loyal to Atahualpa)

Five years of civil war, 1527–1532. In April 1532, Atahualpa's generals Quizquiz and Chalcuchimac crushed Huáscar's army at Quipaipan near Cusco and captured Huáscar himself. Atahualpa was resting at the hot springs of Cajamarca (a small town in the northern Peruvian highlands), preparing to march south to Cusco for his coronation.

### 13.5 Cajamarca 1532.11.16 — 时间线交错的灾难 / Cajamarca: the disaster of overlapping timelines

CN: 这是关键的 careful framing: Atahualpa 在 1532 年 11 月 16 日走进 Cajamarca 广场时, 内战刚结束 6 个月——他还没正式加冕, 帝国还在战后疲惫, 天花刚扫过 5 年, 行政骨架松散。Pizarro 168 个西班牙人就是在这个时间点出现的。如果晚 2 年, Atahualpa 已稳坐 Cusco, 8 万常备军 fully mobilized——结果可能完全不同。Matthew Restall《Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest》(Oxford 2003) 反复强调: conquest 不是西班牙「天命」, 是 timeline 的偶然交错。

EN: Here is the critical careful framing: when Atahualpa walked into the plaza of Cajamarca on November 16, 1532, the civil war had ended only six months earlier. He had not yet been formally crowned. The empire was war-weary, smallpox had swept through five years before, the administrative skeleton was loose. Pizarro's 168 Spaniards arrived at exactly this moment. Two years later, with Atahualpa secure in Cusco and 80,000 standing troops fully mobilized, the outcome might have been totally different. Matthew Restall (*Seven Myths of the Spanish Conquest*, Oxford 2003) hammers this point: conquest was not Spanish "destiny"—it was a contingent overlap of timelines.

## §14 Pizarro 1532 + Inca 帝国终结 / Pizarro 1532 and the end of the Inca empire

### 14.1 Pizarro 1502–1532 — 30 年 Panama 准备 / Thirty years of preparation in Panama

CN: Francisco Pizarro (c. 1471–1541) 不是新手。他 1502 年 31 岁到 Hispaniola, 1513 年跟 Balboa 越过 Panama 看到太平洋, 1524–1528 三次南下勘察秘鲁海岸——他听说南方有「黄金帝国」。1528 年他回西班牙见 Charles V, 拿到 capitulación (征服许可状)。1532 年初他从 Panama 出发时, 带着一个明确的模板: **Cortés 1519–1521 怎么征服 Aztec, 我就怎么征服 Inca**——抓首领、利用内战、找盟友、打心理战。

EN: Francisco Pizarro (c. 1471–1541) was not a novice. He arrived in Hispaniola in 1502 at age 31, crossed Panama with Balboa in 1513 to see the Pacific, and made three southern voyages 1524–1528 scouting the Peruvian coast—he had heard of a "golden empire" to the south. In 1528 he returned to Spain to meet Charles V and obtained a *capitulación* (royal contract of conquest). When he sailed from Panama in early 1532, he carried a clear template: **whatever Cortés did to the Aztec 1519–1521, I will do to the Inca**—seize the ruler, exploit civil war, find allies, wage psychological warfare.

### 14.2 1532.11.16 Cajamarca 广场设伏 / The ambush in the plaza

CN: Pizarro 168 人 (62 骑兵 + 106 步兵 + 几门小炮 + 火枪 + 战马 + 战犬)。Atahualpa 同意在 Cajamarca 广场会面, 带 ~6,000 仪仗队 (没披甲, 因为是仪式)——大部队 ~8 万扎营在城外 5 公里。

11 月 16 日下午, 多明我会修士 Vicente de Valverde 走上前, 通过 Felipillo (一个被绑架的沿海少年翻译, 西班牙语半通) 给 Atahualpa 念 *Requerimiento* (西班牙王室的归顺通牒), 递给他一本拉丁文圣经。Atahualpa 翻了几页, 听不懂, 把书扔在地上。这就是 Pizarro 等的开战借口。

史料: Pedro Pizarro (Francisco 的远房表弟, 当时 16 岁在场)《Relación del descubrimiento y conquista de los reinos del Perú》1571 + Miguel de Estete《Noticia del Perú》1535 + Francisco de Xerez《Verdadera relación de la conquista del Perú》1534 (Pizarro 自己秘书的官方报告)。

EN: Pizarro had 168 men: 62 cavalry, 106 infantry, a few small cannons, harquebuses, war horses, and war dogs. Atahualpa agreed to meet in the plaza of Cajamarca with about 6,000 retainers (unarmored, since it was a ceremony)—the main army of ~80,000 was camped 5 km outside town.

On the afternoon of November 16, the Dominican friar Vicente de Valverde stepped forward and—through Felipillo, a coastal teenager kidnapped years earlier as an interpreter, with shaky Spanish—read the *Requerimiento* (the Spanish crown's ultimatum demanding submission) and handed Atahualpa a Latin Bible. Atahualpa flipped a few pages, couldn't read it, threw the book on the ground. That was Pizarro's signal.

Sources: Pedro Pizarro (Francisco's distant cousin, age 16 and present), *Relación del descubrimiento y conquista de los reinos del Perú* 1571 + Miguel de Estete, *Noticia del Perú* 1535 + Francisco de Xerez, *Verdadera relación de la conquista del Perú* 1534 (Pizarro's own secretary's official report).

### 14.3 屠杀与俘虏 / The massacre and capture

CN: 信号一响, 西班牙骑兵从广场四周建筑里冲出, 火炮齐放, 战犬扑出。6,000 仪仗队挤在广场上, 没武器没披甲, 两小时被屠杀大半 (估计 2,000–7,000 死, 史料数字差异大)。Atahualpa 本人被 Pizarro 亲手拽下轿, 抓为俘虏。城外 8 万大军——失去最高指挥, 不敢动。

EN: At the signal, Spanish cavalry burst from the buildings around the plaza, cannons fired, war dogs lunged. The 6,000 retainers, packed into the square unarmed and unarmored, were slaughtered—estimates range 2,000 to 7,000 dead within two hours (sources disagree wildly). Pizarro personally pulled Atahualpa from his litter and took him prisoner. The 80,000 outside the town—leaderless—did not move.

### 14.4 黄金赎金 1532.11–1533.6 / The gold ransom

CN: Atahualpa 被关在 Cajamarca 一座小房间里 (今天还在, 叫 *El Cuarto del Rescate*——「赎金房」)。他向 Pizarro 提出: 我装满这间房一房黄金 + 两房白银, 你放我。Pizarro 答应。

接下来 7 个月, Inca 信使 (chasqui) 从 Cusco、Pachacamac、Quito 各地神庙剥黄金运来——估计最终 ~1.3 吨黄金 + 2.6 吨白银 (Pedro Pizarro 1571 + Estete 1535)。这是人类史上单次最大的勒索。

但 Pizarro 不打算放。1533 年 7 月 26 日, 以「Atahualpa 密谋反叛 + 杀害 Huáscar」为由 (Huáscar 是在 Atahualpa 被俘期间被秘密处死的, 由谁下令至今争议), 西班牙人审判 Atahualpa——绞刑 (原本判火刑, Atahualpa 临刑前同意洗礼成基督徒, 改绞刑——因为 Inca 信仰认为身体被烧就无法在死后世界重生)。

EN: Atahualpa was held in a small chamber in Cajamarca (still standing today, called *El Cuarto del Rescate*—"the Ransom Room"). He offered Pizarro: fill this room once with gold and twice with silver, and let me go. Pizarro agreed.

Over the next seven months, Inca runners (*chasqui*) brought gold stripped from temples in Cusco, Pachacamac, and Quito—an estimated 1.3 tons of gold and 2.6 tons of silver in the end (Pedro Pizarro 1571 + Estete 1535). It was the single largest ransom in human history.

But Pizarro never intended to release him. On July 26, 1533, accusing Atahualpa of "plotting rebellion and ordering the murder of Huáscar" (Huáscar had been secretly killed during Atahualpa's captivity—who ordered it remains disputed), the Spaniards tried Atahualpa and sentenced him to burning at the stake. At the last moment Atahualpa agreed to be baptized a Christian, and the sentence was commuted to garroting—because Inca belief held that a burned body could not be reborn in the afterlife.

### 14.5 1533.11 Pizarro 进 Cusco — Inca 帝国正式终结 / Pizarro enters Cusco — formal end of the Inca empire

CN: Atahualpa 死后 Pizarro 立 Manco Inca Yupanqui (Huáscar 的弟弟) 为傀儡 Sapa Inca, 11 月进 Cusco——Inca 帝国名义上终结。三年后 1536 年 Manco Inca 反叛, 围攻 Cusco 10 个月, 最后退入 Vilcabamba 山区, 那个 *neo-Inca state* 一直撑到 1572 年才被西班牙剿灭。

EN: After Atahualpa's death, Pizarro installed Manco Inca Yupanqui (Huáscar's younger brother) as a puppet *Sapa Inca*, and in November 1533 entered Cusco—the formal end of the Inca empire. Three years later, in 1536, Manco Inca rebelled, besieged Cusco for ten months, then retreated into the Vilcabamba mountains, where the *neo-Inca state* held out until 1572.

### 14.6 careful framing — 不是「168 vs 8 万」/ Not "168 versus 80,000"

CN: 中学教科书爱讲「168 西班牙人征服 8 万 Inca」, 这是 myth。John Hemming《The Conquest of the Incas》(Macmillan 1970) 和 Restall 2003 都拆解过——Inca 真正的崩溃是 multi-cause:
1. **天花 1525–1527** 已先杀死 Huayna Capac + Ninan Cuyochi + 大量贵族行政官
2. **内战 1527–1532** 帝国军队和行政内耗 5 年
3. **战术不对称**: 火枪、骑兵、钢剑、战犬 vs 矛戈棍棒
4. **同盟政治**: Pizarro 后来联合 Huáscar 系残部 + Cañari + Chachapoyas 反 Atahualpa 系 (跟 Cortés + Tlaxcala 同 pattern)
5. **神学震撼**: Atahualpa 自己的占卜师之前预言过帝国将由「白胡须海上来人」终结 (Sarmiento 1572 转述)——Inca 上层一开始没立刻战斗反应, 部分跟这有关

168 人本身没意义——意义是 168 人踩在以上 4 个倍增器上。

EN: Middle-school textbooks love "168 Spaniards conquered 80,000 Inca." That's a myth. John Hemming (*The Conquest of the Incas*, Macmillan 1970) and Restall 2003 both dismantle it. The real collapse was multi-causal:
1. **Smallpox 1525–1527** had already killed Huayna Capac, Ninan Cuyochi, and large numbers of nobles and administrators
2. **Civil war 1527–1532** drained the army and administration for five years
3. **Tactical asymmetry**: harquebuses, cavalry, steel swords, war dogs vs. wooden clubs and slings
4. **Alliance politics**: Pizarro later allied with Huáscar's surviving faction + Cañari + Chachapoyas peoples against the Atahualpa faction (same pattern as Cortés + Tlaxcala)
5. **Theological shock**: Atahualpa's own diviners had reportedly prophesied that the empire would end with "bearded men from the sea" (Sarmiento 1572 retells this)—Inca elites' delayed reaction is partly explained by this

The 168 number alone means nothing. It means something only when standing on top of those four multipliers.

## §15 Postclassic Maya 后续 1517–1697 — 「最后一个被征服」 / "The last to fall"

### 15.1 Yucatán 第一波 1517 / The first wave at Yucatán

CN: 1517 年 Francisco Hernández de Córdoba 从 Cuba 出航, 在 Yucatán 沿岸登陆——这是西班牙第一次接触 Maya。Maya 战士在 Champotón 击退他, Hernández 受伤, 回 Cuba 不久就死。这是 Tenochtitlán 1521 陷落前 4 年。Maya 不是「神秘消失」的——她们活得好好的, 见到欧洲人会反击。

EN: In 1517, Francisco Hernández de Córdoba sailed from Cuba and landed on the Yucatán coast—Spain's first contact with the Maya. Maya warriors drove him off at Champotón; Hernández was wounded and died shortly after returning to Cuba. This was four years before the fall of Tenochtitlán in 1521. The Maya did not "mysteriously vanish"—they were living, organized, and they fought back when Europeans arrived.

### 15.2 1527 Montejo 第一次失败 / Montejo's first failed attempt

CN: 1527 年 Francisco de Montejo el Adelantado (「先锋蒙特霍」) 拿到 Yucatán 征服许可, 第一次远征——失败。Yucatán 不像 Aztec 或 Inca 有一个中心首都可以一击拿下: 它是 16 个独立 Maya 城邦 (Roys 1957)——你打下一个, 还有 15 个, 而且没有黄金诱惑、没有水、热带病肆虐。Montejo 1535 年彻底放弃。

EN: In 1527, Francisco de Montejo *el Adelantado* ("the Pioneer") obtained the *capitulación* for Yucatán and led his first expedition—it failed. Yucatán was not like the Aztec or Inca: there was no single capital you could decapitate. It was sixteen independent Maya city-states (Roys 1957)—you conquered one, fifteen more remained, and there was no gold to motivate, no water in the dry season, and tropical disease everywhere. Montejo gave up entirely in 1535.

### 15.3 1542 Mérida 立基 — 北 Yucatán 入西班牙手 / Mérida founded — northern Yucatán falls

CN: 1542 年 Francisco de Montejo el Mozo (「小蒙特霍」, Adelantado 的儿子) 第二次成功, 在 Maya Tihó 城旧址建立 Mérida (今 Yucatán 州首府)。北部 Yucatán Maya 人口被纳入 *encomienda* 体系 (西班牙强制劳役 + 贡赋制)。

但: 这只是 *北部*。中部 Petén 雨林地带的 Itzá Maya 城邦 (在 Lake Petén 中央岛屿 Tayasal/Nojpetén), 西班牙人 *根本进不去*——太远、太湿、没路、没经济回报。

EN: In 1542, Francisco de Montejo *el Mozo* ("the Younger", the Adelantado's son) succeeded on the second try, founding Mérida (today the capital of Yucatán state) on the ruins of the Maya city of Tihó. Northern Yucatán Maya were absorbed into the *encomienda* system (Spanish forced-labor and tribute regime).

But: this was only the *north*. The central Petén rainforest, where the Itzá Maya city-state sat on the island of Tayasal/Nojpetén in Lake Petén, was simply *unreachable* for the Spanish—too far, too wet, no roads, no economic payoff.

### 15.4 1697.3.13 Tayasal 陷落 — 美洲最后一个独立原住民国家 / Tayasal falls — the last independent Indigenous state

CN: Itzá 抵抗到 **1697 年 3 月 13 日**——Martín de Ursúa 率西班牙水陆联合部队渡 Lake Petén, 攻破 Tayasal。

时间感: Aztec 1521 陷落, Inca 1533 陷落, Itzá 1697 陷落——Maya 城邦比 Aztec 多撑了 **176 年**。1697 年是什么时候? Newton 已经发表《Principia》10 年, 中国康熙帝在位, 北美 13 殖民地已经成熟。Maya 不是衰落得最早, Maya 是抵抗得最久。

EN: The Itzá held out until **March 13, 1697**, when Martín de Ursúa led a combined Spanish land-and-water force across Lake Petén and stormed Tayasal.

Timeline check: Aztec fall 1521, Inca fall 1533, Itzá fall 1697—the Maya city-states held out **176 years longer** than the Aztec. What was 1697? Newton had published the *Principia* ten years earlier; the Kangxi Emperor was on the throne in China; the 13 colonies in North America were mature. The Maya were not the first to decline. They were the last to resist.

### 15.5 Lens 3 anchor — Ix Mutal 的时间感 / Ix Mutal's timeline

CN: 「我 Ix Mutal, 1480 年生在 Postclassic Yucatán 一个小村子。我 38 岁 (1518), 第一波天花从沿海传到我们这里, 我母亲死了。我 41 岁 (1521), 听商人说北边有一座大湖中的城 Tenochtitlán 陷落了——我们没去过, 但消息传来。我 47 岁 (1527), Montejo 第一次到 Yucatán, 我们村躲进雨林。我 60 岁 (1540), Mérida 还没立基, 但西班牙人离我们越来越近。我活着的每一天, Yucatán Maya 还要再撑 155 年才彻底落入西班牙手——但那时候我已经死了 100 多年。」

EN: "I am Ix Mutal. I was born in 1480 in a small Postclassic village in Yucatán. At 38 (1518), the first smallpox wave reached us from the coast and my mother died. At 41 (1521), traders told us a great city on a lake in the north—Tenochtitlán—had fallen. We had never been there, but the news came. At 47 (1527), Montejo's men first came to Yucatán and our village hid in the forest. At 60 (1540), Mérida had not yet been founded, but the Spaniards were closing in. Every day I lived, Yucatán Maya had 155 more years to hold out—but I would be dead for over a century by then."

## §16 Ix Mutal 1521–1540 — Lens 3 主角段 / Ix Mutal as the receiving-end witness ⭐

### 16.1 1521 — 41 岁, 北方传来的消息 / Age 41, news from the north

CN: 1521 年 8 月, Tenochtitlán 陷落。三个月后, 消息通过沿海贸易商沿 Yucatán 海岸传到 Ix Mutal 的村落 (假设在今 Campeche 内陆)。商人说: 「Mexica 王 (他们不知道叫 Cuauhtémoc) 被白胡须海上来的人抓了, 大城烧了, 湖水变红了。」村里长老听完, 沉默了很久, 然后说: 「准备粮食, 男人去森林里挖洞藏火药——不, 我们没有火药——藏粮食。」Ix Mutal 那年 41 岁, 三个孩子, 丈夫是 Maya 商人, 那年也在沿海——回来时说: 「白胡须的人会再来, 不是会不会, 是什么时候。」

EN: August 1521: Tenochtitlán fell. Three months later, the news traveled along the Yucatán coast through trading networks to Ix Mutal's village (let's place it in inland Campeche). Traders said: "The Mexica king [they did not know his name was Cuauhtémoc] was captured by bearded men from the sea, the great city is burned, the lake water has turned red." The village elders listened in silence, then said: "Store food. Men, dig pits in the forest to hide the gunpowder—no, we have no gunpowder—hide the food." Ix Mutal was 41 that year, with three children. Her husband was a Maya trader who had been on the coast that month. When he came back he said: "The bearded ones will come back. Not *if*. *When*."

### 16.2 1527 — 47 岁, Montejo 第一次到 Yucatán / Age 47, Montejo's first wave

CN: 1527 年 Montejo 远征队登陆 Yucatán 北岸。Ix Mutal 村离海岸 80 公里, 没直接打到——但难民开始从北边涌来, 带着伤、带着病。村里多收了 30 个人, 粮食立刻紧张。Ix Mutal 那年开始织 *huipil* (Maya 女性传统绣花长袍) 给难民——不是怜悯, 是因为她爷爷教过她: 「衣服上的图案是文字。我们没纸, 但我们有布。布会被穿、被传、被记住。」Ix Mutal 织进 huipil 的图案是村里 Postclassic Maya 的星历记号——后来这种 huipil 在 17–20 世纪 Maya 抵抗运动里反复出现, 到今天 Yucatán 妇女还在织。

EN: In 1527 Montejo's expedition landed on the north Yucatán coast. Ix Mutal's village was 80 km inland and was not directly attacked—but refugees began streaming south, wounded and sick. The village absorbed 30 extra people; food got tight immediately. That year Ix Mutal started weaving *huipil* (the Maya woman's traditional embroidered tunic) for the refugees—not out of pity, but because her grandfather had taught her: "The patterns on the cloth are writing. We have no paper, but we have cloth. Cloth gets worn, gets passed down, gets remembered." The patterns Ix Mutal wove into the *huipil* were the Postclassic Maya star-calendar marks of her village—and these *huipil* would reappear across 17th-to-20th century Maya resistance movements, and Yucatán women still weave them today.

### 16.3 1530s — 50 岁, 第二波天花 + 麻疹 / Age 50, second epidemic wave

CN: 1530 年代 Yucatán 第二波瘟疫——这一次是天花 + 麻疹叠加 (Cook 1998)。Ix Mutal 村 1500 年左右大约 800 人, 1540 年估计剩 150 人——80% 死亡率。Ix Mutal 自己活下来了 (后来发现部分人有部分免疫力), 但她三个孩子里只剩一个女儿。她丈夫死于 1533 年的麻疹。村里老人几乎全没了——而老人是 Maya 口述知识的载体: 历法、星象、家族系谱、Chilam Balam 文本的口头版本。一代人记忆, 在 10 年里断了链。

EN: In the 1530s, a second epidemic swept Yucatán—smallpox and measles together (Cook 1998). Ix Mutal's village had perhaps 800 people around 1500; by 1540 maybe 150 remained—roughly 80% mortality. Ix Mutal herself survived (some people, we now know, carried partial immunity), but of her three children only one daughter lived. Her husband died of measles in 1533. Almost all the village elders died—and the elders were the carriers of Maya oral knowledge: the calendar, the stars, family genealogies, the oral versions of what would later become the *Chilam Balam* texts. A generation's memory broke its chain in ten years.

### 16.4 1542 — 62 岁, Mérida 立基 / Age 62, Mérida is founded

CN: 1542 年 Mérida 立基。Ix Mutal 村被划入北 Yucatán *encomienda* 系统——一个西班牙 *encomendero* (领主) 远在 Mérida, 每年派人来收贡赋: 玉米、棉布、蜂蜜、火鸡。Ix Mutal 那年 62 岁——按 Postclassic Maya 平民女性平均寿命算, 她已经超长寿了。她学了几个 Spanish 词 ("señor", "tributo", "Dios"), 但她在自己女儿和孙女面前从来只说 Yucatec Maya, 织 huipil 时讲老故事。

她 1545 年死, 65 岁。村里没有人写下她的名字。Mérida 教区档案 1545 年的记录里, 只有「印第安女性, 老, 死于咳嗽」一行。

EN: In 1542 Mérida was founded. Ix Mutal's village was assigned to the northern Yucatán *encomienda* system—a Spanish *encomendero* (lord) lived far away in Mérida and sent men each year to collect tribute: maize, cotton cloth, honey, turkeys. Ix Mutal was 62 that year—well past the average life expectancy for a Postclassic Maya commoner woman. She picked up a few Spanish words ("*señor*", "*tributo*", "*Dios*"), but with her daughter and granddaughter she only ever spoke Yucatec Maya, telling the old stories as she wove *huipil*.

She died in 1545 at 65. No one in the village wrote down her name. The Mérida parish registry for 1545 contains only one line: "Indian woman, old, died of cough."

### 16.5 careful framing — Ix Mutal 是 composite, 不是真人 / Ix Mutal is a composite, not a single person

CN: Ix Mutal 这个名字和故事是虚构 composite, 不是真人——但她代表的群体真实存在: 16 世纪 Yucatán Maya 平民女性约 100 万 (Cook 1998 + Roys 1957), 1500 年估计 80 万, 1600 年剩 ~30 万。她的故事每一个具体细节——huipil 织法、村落规模、瘟疫死亡率、encomienda 贡赋、Maya 老人的口述传承——都来自史料。她的名字没传下来, 不是因为她不重要, 是因为殖民档案没记她。

但: Munro Edmonson《Heaven Born Merida and Its Destiny: The Book of Chilam Balam of Chumayel》(University of Texas Press 1986) 翻译的 Chilam Balam 文本——那是 16–18 世纪 Yucatec Maya 人用拉丁字母拼写自己语言写下的口传知识——里面到处是 Ix Mutal 这样的人留下的回声。Edmonson 1986 引言说: 「这些文本不是 Maya 精英的史诗, 是村落的集体记忆——哪个老妇女讲的, 我们不知道, 但她们的句子在文本里。」

你 21 世纪的 7 年级学生, 通过 Edmonson 1986 的英译, 还能听到 Ix Mutal 这一代人的语调。

EN: The name Ix Mutal and her story are a fictional composite, not a single person—but the population she represents is real: roughly 1 million Yucatán Maya commoner women in the 16th century (Cook 1998 + Roys 1957), perhaps 800,000 in 1500 falling to ~300,000 by 1600. Every concrete detail in her story—*huipil* weaving methods, village size, epidemic mortality rates, *encomienda* tribute, Maya oral transmission through elders—comes from the sources. Her name was not preserved, not because she did not matter, but because the colonial archive did not record her.

But: Munro Edmonson's *Heaven Born Merida and Its Destiny: The Book of Chilam Balam of Chumayel* (University of Texas Press 1986) translates *Chilam Balam* texts—oral Maya knowledge that 16th-to-18th-century Yucatec Maya wrote down in their own language using the Latin alphabet—and those texts are full of echoes of women like Ix Mutal. Edmonson's 1986 introduction says: "These texts are not the epics of a Maya elite; they are the collective memory of villages—we do not know which old woman spoke which line, but their sentences are in the text."

You, a 21st-century 7th grader, can still hear the cadence of Ix Mutal's generation through Edmonson's 1986 English translation.

### 16.6 Lens 3 narrator's voice — 「我没有名字」/ "I have no name"

CN: 「我活到 65 岁。我的名字没进任何档案。Mérida 教区只写了『印第安女性, 老, 死于咳嗽』。但我教我的女儿织 huipil, 她教她的女儿, 她又教她的女儿——5 个世纪后, 21 世纪的 Yucatán 妇女在市场卖 huipil 时, 上面的星历图案还是我那一代织进去的。

我也教我女儿讲故事——口头的, 不写。后来 17 世纪有人用拉丁字母把我们村的故事抄进 *Chilam Balam* 文本——他们没写我的名字, 但他们写下了我讲过的句子。Edmonson 1986 把那些句子翻译成英文。

你 7 年级在课堂上读 Edmonson 翻译的句子时, 你听到的不是『一个文明』, 是一个 65 岁老妇女在 1540 年代某个夜里, 一边织 huipil 一边告诉孙女的话。

我没有名字。但我有声音。」

EN: "I lived to 65. My name went into no archive. The Mérida parish only wrote 'Indian woman, old, died of cough.' But I taught my daughter to weave *huipil*, she taught hers, and hers taught hers—five centuries later, when 21st-century Yucatán women sell *huipil* in the market, the star-calendar patterns on them are the ones my generation wove in.

I also taught my daughter to tell stories—oral, not written. Later, in the 17th century, someone wrote our village's stories into the *Chilam Balam* texts using the Latin alphabet—they did not write my name, but they wrote down sentences I had spoken. Edmonson 1986 translates those sentences into English.

When you, in 7th grade, read an Edmonson sentence in class, you are not hearing 'a civilization.' You are hearing what a 65-year-old woman said to her granddaughter on some night in the 1540s while weaving a *huipil*.

I have no name. But I have a voice."

## §17 跨 Topic 全图回挂 + reflection / Cross-Topic synthesis and reflection

### 17.1 八条跨 Topic 回挂 / Eight cross-Topic threads

#### (1) Mongol 1206 Genghis vs Inca 1438 Pachacuti — 同 perpetrator-actor pattern
CN: Genghis Khan 1206 起兵, 25 年扩张到中亚 + 中国北方; Pachacuti 1438 即位, 30 年扩张 Inca 从 Cusco 一城到 Tawantinsuyu 帝国。两者都是 perpetrator-actor 视角的 Lens 1: 暴力的扩张者, 但同时是建制者 (蒙古驿站 yam ↔ Inca 驿站 chasqui + 大路 Qhapaq Ñan)。
EN: Genghis Khan rose in 1206 and expanded across Central Asia and northern China within 25 years; Pachacuti took the throne in 1438 and expanded the Inca from a single city to the Tawantinsuyu empire in 30 years. Both are perpetrator-actor Lens 1: violent expansionists who were also institution-builders (Mongol *yam* postal relay ↔ Inca *chasqui* runners and the *Qhapaq Ñan* royal road).

#### (2) Mali 1413 Aisha 商人记账 vs Inca quipu — 非文字行政同 pattern
CN: Mali Topic Lens 2 Aisha 用阿拉伯字母 + Mande 口语混合记账; Inca 用 quipu (打结的彩绳) 做行政记录——都是「非纸面文字」状态下的复杂帝国会计系统。
EN: In the Mali Topic, Lens 2 Aisha keeps trade accounts mixing Arabic script with spoken Mande; the Inca used *quipu* (knotted, color-coded cords) for administrative record-keeping—both are complex imperial accounting systems operating in "not-paper" states.

#### (3) Black Death 1346 鼠疫 vs Pre-Columbian 1518 天花 — McNeill 1976 同 pattern
CN: William McNeill《Plagues and Peoples》(Anchor 1976) 把这两件事放在同一个 frame: 大型流行病改写权力地图。1346–1353 黑死病杀死 1/3 欧洲人, 西欧农奴制崩溃; 1518–1600 美洲传染病杀死 80–95% 原住民, 殖民帝国奠基。生物事件 → 政治结构。
EN: William McNeill (*Plagues and Peoples*, Anchor 1976) placed both events in a single frame: large-scale epidemics rewrite political maps. The 1346–1353 Black Death killed a third of Europe and broke serfdom in Western Europe; 1518–1600 epidemics in the Americas killed 80–95% of the Indigenous population and laid the foundations of colonial empire. Biological event → political structure.

#### (4) Renaissance 1455 Gutenberg 印刷 vs Cortés 1522《Cartas de relación》 — 印刷决定 conquest narrative
CN: Gutenberg 1455 印《42 行圣经》, 70 年后 Cortés 1522 寄回西班牙的 5 封信《Cartas de relación》立刻被印刷传遍欧洲——印刷术决定了 *谁的版本* 成为欧洲对 conquest 的认知。Bernal Díaz 的版本 (士兵视角) 1568 写成, 但 1632 才出版——晚了 110 年。Restall 2003 一再说: conquest 的 myth 不是来自事件本身, 来自 *谁先印*。
EN: Gutenberg printed the 42-line Bible in 1455. Seventy years later, Cortés's five *Cartas de relación* (1519–1526) were printed and circulated across Europe immediately—the press decided *whose version* became Europe's understanding of conquest. Bernal Díaz's soldier's-eye account was written in 1568 but only published in 1632—110 years late. Restall 2003 repeats: the myths of conquest do not come from the event; they come from *who prints first*.

#### (5) Constantinople 1453 陷落 vs Tenochtitlán 1521.8.13 陷落 — 文明终结 pattern + 68 年之差
CN: 1453.5.29 Constantinople 被奥斯曼攻陷, 拜占庭帝国终结; 1521.8.13 Tenochtitlán 被 Cortés + Tlaxcala 攻陷, Aztec 帝国终结。两件事相隔 68 年, 都是 8 月、都是围城最后一天皇帝/王战死或被俘 (Constantine XI 战死 ↔ Cuauhtémoc 被俘)。两件事都被欧洲叙事处理成 「文明的终结」, 但两件事的 *延续性* 都被低估——拜占庭文化进入俄罗斯 + 奥斯曼; Aztec/Mexica 文化进入 mestizo 墨西哥 (Townsend 2006)。
EN: On May 29, 1453, Constantinople fell to the Ottomans, ending the Byzantine empire; on August 13, 1521, Tenochtitlán fell to Cortés and Tlaxcala, ending the Aztec empire. The two events are 68 years apart, both in late summer, and in both the emperor/ruler was killed or captured on the final day of the siege (Constantine XI died fighting ↔ Cuauhtémoc captured). Both were narrated in European writing as "the end of a civilization," but in both cases the *continuity* is underrated—Byzantine culture flowed into Russia and the Ottomans; Aztec/Mexica culture flowed into mestizo Mexico (Townsend 2006).

#### (6) Crusades 1099 内部 alliance shifts vs 1521 Tlaxcala 反 Aztec — 同 pattern
CN: 1099 第一次十字军攻陷耶路撒冷, 关键不是「西方 vs 东方」, 是十字军内部 + 与 Fatimid Egypt + Byzantine 之间不断换 alliance。1521 Tenochtitlán 陷落, 关键不是「西班牙 vs Aztec」, 是 Tlaxcala + Texcoco + Cholula 中央 Mexico 内部 200 年的旧怨被 Cortés 利用。Lens 1 视角: 谁是 perpetrator, 不是「外人」, 是 *本地敌人 + 外来工具*。
EN: The First Crusade taking Jerusalem in 1099 is not really "West vs East"—it is shifting alliances among the Crusaders, the Fatimids of Egypt, and Byzantium. The fall of Tenochtitlán in 1521 is not really "Spain vs the Aztec"—it is 200 years of resentment among Tlaxcala, Texcoco, Cholula, and other central Mexican peoples, exploited by Cortés. Lens 1 perspective: the perpetrator is rarely the "outsider"; it is *local enemies plus foreign tools*.

#### (7) Reformation 1517 Erasmus vs Malintzin 1500–1529 — 同代 lonely-mediator
CN: Erasmus 1517 站在天主教改革 + Luther 之间, 翻译希腊文圣经, 双方都骂他; Malintzin 1519–1521 站在 Cortés + Aztec 之间, 翻译, 双方都骂她 (西班牙人不信任 *india*, Mexica 骂她叛徒)。两人同代, 同 archetype: lonely mediator across worlds, 谁都不归属, 谁都骂。
EN: In 1517 Erasmus stood between Catholic reform and Luther, translating the Greek New Testament; both sides attacked him. In 1519–1521 Malintzin stood between Cortés and the Aztec, interpreting; both sides attacked her (the Spanish distrusted her as an *india*; the Mexica called her a traitor). They were contemporaries, the same archetype: the lonely mediator between worlds, belonging to neither, attacked by both.

#### (8) AoE 1492 Columbus vs Pre-Columbian Topic 直接前置
CN: AoE Topic Lens 1 Columbus 1492; Pre-Columbian Topic Lens 1 Pachacuti 1438–1472 早 Columbus 几十年, Lens 2 Malintzin 1500–1529 直接接 Columbus 后果, Lens 3 Ix Mutal 1480–1545 见证整个 conquest 全过程。这两个 Topic 不是分离的——是同一个 1450–1550 全球化时刻的两面: 欧洲行动者 + 美洲承受者。
EN: The AoE Topic's Lens 1 is Columbus 1492; the Pre-Columbian Topic's Lens 1 is Pachacuti 1438–1472 (pre-dating Columbus by decades), Lens 2 is Malintzin 1500–1529 (the immediate downstream of Columbus), and Lens 3 is Ix Mutal 1480–1545 (a witness to the full conquest arc). The two Topics are not separate—they are two sides of the same 1450–1550 globalizing moment: European actors and American receivers.

### 17.2 给 Grade 7 / AP DBQ 的 5 个 thinking moves / Five thinking moves for the DBQ

#### (1) Follow the microbe / 跟着病菌走
CN: 1518–1600 美洲死 80–95% 不是「西班牙剑」, 是 microbes (天花、麻疹、流感、伤寒)。Cook 1998 估算 conquest 战争直接死亡 < 5%, 95%+ 死于疾病。任何 DBQ 写「conquest = 军事征服」都漏了 95%。
EN: The 80–95% Indigenous death toll between 1518 and 1600 was not from "the Spanish sword"; it was from microbes—smallpox, measles, influenza, typhus. Cook 1998 estimates direct combat deaths at less than 5% of the total, with disease accounting for 95%+. Any DBQ that frames conquest as "military victory" misses 95% of what happened.

#### (2) See the alliance / 看到同盟
CN: 没有 Tlaxcala, Cortés 进不了 Tenochtitlán; 没有 Huáscar 系残部 + Cañari, Pizarro 守不住 Cusco。Conquest 不是「外来 vs 本地」, 是「外来 + 部分本地 vs 另一部分本地」。
EN: Without Tlaxcala, Cortés never reaches Tenochtitlán. Without Huáscar's surviving faction and the Cañari, Pizarro never holds Cusco. Conquest is never "outsider vs native"; it is "outsider + part of the native against another part of the native."

#### (3) Hold contradictions / 拥抱矛盾
CN: Malintzin 既是 broker 也是奴隶; 既被骂叛徒, 也是 mestizo Mexico 的国母; 死时 29 岁, 留下两个混血孩子, 一个改写一个大陆的家族系谱。一个人可以同时是这些——别选一个标签。
EN: Malintzin was both a broker and an enslaved person; both vilified as traitor and named mother of mestizo Mexico; she died at 29, leaving two mixed-heritage children whose lineage rewrote a continent's family map. A person can be all of these at once—do not pick one label.

#### (4) Find the silenced / 找到被沉默的
CN: Postclassic Maya 平民女性 (Ix Mutal) 没有名字——殖民档案没记。但 Chilam Balam 文本是她们的回声, huipil 织法是她们的指纹。「找不到史料」不等于「不存在」, 等于「档案是谁建的、为谁建的」。
EN: Postclassic Maya commoner women (Ix Mutal) have no names in the archive—the colonial registry did not record them. But the *Chilam Balam* texts are their echo, and *huipil* weaving is their fingerprint. "No source" does not mean "did not exist." It means "who built the archive, and for whom."

#### (5) Reframe what counts as "discovery" / 重新框架「发现」
CN: 1492 是「Columbus discovery of America」吗? 还是 「美洲 ~6,000 万原住民 (Mann 2005《1491》) 第一次被欧洲卷入全球化」? 后者更准确。Discovery 是 *谁站在岸上的视角*——站在 Hispaniola 沙滩上, 看到 Columbus 三艘船的 Taíno 人, 也是 discoverer——他们发现了欧洲。
EN: Was 1492 "Columbus's discovery of America"? Or was it "America's ~60 million Indigenous peoples (Mann 2005, *1491*) being pulled into globalization for the first time by Europeans"? The second is more accurate. "Discovery" is *whoever stands on the beach watching*—the Taíno standing on the Hispaniola sand watching three Spanish ships were also discoverers. They discovered Europe.

## §18 Topic 收束 / Topic close

CN: 三个 lens, 三个时代:
- **Lens 1 Pachacuti (1438–1472)**: perpetrator-actor — 一个 30 年扩张 Inca 帝国的人, 同时是建制者
- **Lens 2 Malintzin (1500–1529)**: lonely mediator — 一个 dragged across worlds 的 19 岁翻译, 同时是奴隶 + broker + 母亲
- **Lens 3 Ix Mutal (1480–1545) ⭐ defaultLens**: receiving-end witness — 一个没有名字的 65 岁 Postclassic Maya 平民女性, 同时是 huipil 织者 + Chilam Balam 口传载体

三种 lens 共同覆盖 1438–1545 一百年, 从 Inca 帝国扩张, 到 Aztec/Inca 双双陷落, 到 Postclassic Maya 缓慢吞没——这是 Pre-Columbian Americas 的最后一百年, 也是被扭曲最严重的一百年。

你 7 年级第一次接触这段历史。请记住三件事:
1. 美洲 1491 不是空地——是 6,000 万人, 三个大帝国, 上千个城邦, 平行欧亚
2. Conquest 不是英雄故事, 是 microbes + alliances + timeline 的偶然
3. 没有名字的女性留下了文本和布——你今天还能听到她们

EN: Three lenses, three eras:
- **Lens 1 Pachacuti (1438–1472)**: perpetrator-actor — a man who expanded the Inca empire over 30 years, also an institution-builder
- **Lens 2 Malintzin (1500–1529)**: lonely mediator — a 19-year-old interpreter dragged across worlds, also enslaved + broker + mother
- **Lens 3 Ix Mutal (1480–1545) ⭐ defaultLens**: receiving-end witness — a nameless 65-year-old Postclassic Maya commoner woman, also a *huipil* weaver and a carrier of the oral *Chilam Balam*

The three lenses together cover one hundred years, 1438–1545: from the Inca expansion, through the twin falls of Aztec and Inca, into the slow strangling of Postclassic Maya. These are the last hundred years of Pre-Columbian America, and the most distorted hundred years in popular memory.

You are in 7th grade meeting this history for the first time. Remember three things:
1. The Americas in 1491 were not empty land—they were 60 million people, three great empires, thousands of city-states, paralleling Eurasia
2. Conquest is not a hero story; it is microbes + alliances + the contingency of timelines
3. Women without names left texts and cloth behind—you can still hear them today
