## §0 author note — 这个 Topic 怎么读

### 课标对位

这一章对 HSS-7.3（唐宋中国）和 AP-WHAP-1.2（东亚宋朝）。两个课标合并起来覆盖 ~661 年——618 年唐高祖李渊建国，到 1279 年崖山海战南宋灭亡。中间夹着 53 年的五代十国（907-960）和 北宋-南宋 320 年（960-1279）。

### 4 个关键时刻锚点

整段历史不能平铺直叙。我挑 4 个时刻当锚点：

1. **626 年玄武门之变**——唐太宗李世民 28 岁夺位，开贞观之治。"founder violence" → "good governance" 的中国王朝典型 pattern。
2. **755 年安史之乱**——大唐由盛转衰。8 年战乱，北方人口减半（《旧唐书》记载从 5288 万降到约 1700 万——数字争议大，但减半的量级学界无大异议）。
3. **1126 年靖康之变**——北宋亡。徽宗、钦宗父子被金军掳到东北，《清明上河图》刚画完十几年。
4. **1279 年崖山海战**——南宋亡。陆秀夫背 8 岁的小皇帝赵昺跳海。中华史上第一次完全被外族征服。

### 3 个 lens 推荐

* **宋徽宗赵佶（perpetrator-actor）**——艺术天才、政治灾难。1100 年 18 岁即位，1126 年 44 岁亡国。瘦金体 + 《瑞鹤图》 + 蔡京 + 花石纲 + 方腊起义——同一个人。
* **苏轼（lonely-mediator）**——1037-1101，跨神宗、哲宗、徽宗三朝。乌台诗案差点死，黄州 / 惠州 / 儋州三次贬谪。新党旧党都不容他——"莫听穿林打叶声"。
* **李清照（receiving-end）**——1084-1155 左右。北宋末年闺秀词人，靖康之变后跟丈夫赵明诚南渡，丈夫死，文物散，57 岁左右改嫁张汝舟，几个月就离婚——南宋法律里寡妇离婚要坐牢，她坐了 9 天。

### 跨 Topic 五面体——information preservation

李清照-赵明诚的《金石录》文物南运（1126-1129），跟另外 4 个 Topic 拼成"信息保存五面体"：

1. **Mali al-Sa'di**——1655 年廷巴克图学者 al-Sa'di 写《Ta'rikh al-Sudan》（苏丹史），保存 Mali 帝国口述史。
2. **文艺复兴 1440 印刷术**——古登堡。
3. **Reformation 95 Theses 1517**——路德 + 印刷术放大 = 信息病毒式扩散。
4. **AoE 1493 Inter Caetera 教皇诏书**——殖民地划分依靠书面文件。
5. **李清照《金石录》南运 1126-1129**——15 车文物从青州出发，过淮河长江，丈夫病死建康，文物在战乱中散尽。她写《金石录后序》——保存的不是文物，是失去的故事。

5 个 Topic 各从不同角度：北非用口述-阿拉伯文混合，欧洲用印刷术放大文本，殖民帝国用诏书法律化，中国用私人收藏 + 序跋追忆——5 面体每一面都是同一个问题"信息怎么熬过暴力"。

## §0.5 Tang-Song 在 14 世纪 cross-cultural 全球网络的位置

### Joseph Needham Question

英国剑桥的科技史家 Joseph Needham（李约瑟，1900-1995）写了 27 卷《Science and Civilisation in China》，提出一个问题：12 世纪的中国（北宋）和 14 世纪的 Mali 帝国，技术、商业、城市规模都领先同代欧洲——为什么 18 世纪欧洲反超？

Needham 的回答是：不是文明缺陷，是政治选择。明朝海禁（1371 年起）、清朝闭关（1757 年起）——主动关门。Mali 帝国 1591 年被摩洛哥火枪兵击溃——政治崩溃不等于文明落后。

这跟课本里"东方停滞 / 西方进步"的 19 世纪 Hegel 史观完全不同。7 年级学生第一次读到中国宋朝时，要先卸掉这个隐性 framing。

### 1271-1279 蒙元征服的时空相邻坐标

* **1271 年**——忽必烈建国号"大元"，定都大都（今北京）。
* **1276 年**——元军攻破临安（今杭州），南宋恭帝赵㬎 5 岁被俘。
* **1279 年**——崖山海战，南宋亡。
* **1324 年**——Mali 帝国 Mansa Musa 朝觐麦加，路过开罗散金 6 个月让金价崩盘。
* **1352 年**——Ibn Battuta 抵达 Mali 帝国首都尼亚尼。

宋亡（1279）跟 Mansa Musa 朝觐（1324）相距 45 年——同代人记忆里两件事并存。元朝（1271-1368）跟 Mali 黄金时代（1235-1645）正好叠合 ~100 年。

### 北宋四大发明的 1492 / 1517 链条

宋朝 320 年里成熟的 4 项技术，每一项都给后世全球史奠基：

* **火药**——9 世纪炼丹副产品，11 世纪宋朝军用，蒙古西征传到中亚 → 14 世纪欧洲 → 1492 后殖民征服的核心工具。
* **活字印刷**——毕昇 1041-1048 年泥活字。古登堡 1440 金属活字独立发明，但概念已在丝路传播。1517 年路德 95 条论纲靠印刷术 14 天传遍德意志——技术链回溯到宋。
* **罗盘**——宋朝沈括《梦溪笔谈》（1088）记载磁针偏角。13 世纪传到阿拉伯航海者 → 14 世纪欧洲 → 1492 哥伦布。
* **纸钞**（*jiaozi* 交子）——成都商人 1023 年发行世界最早纸币。元朝大规模推广，马可波罗游记惊叹。

7 年级要记的不是"中国先进"，是"技术不属于哪个文明，技术在网络里流动"。

### "崖山之后无中华"——historiography 钢筋

这句话出处不明，民国时期流行。学界有两派：

* **钱穆（1895-1990）**——1942 年《国史大纲》反对此说，认为元明清仍是中华文化主体。
* **刘子健（1919-1993）**——1962 年提出 "中国转向内在"（China Turning Inward），同意宋以后中华文明 character 发生质变——内向、保守、士大夫文化定型化。

这是 historiography（史学史）问题——不是"事实"，是 "怎么解释事实"。7 年级开始接触 historiography 概念：同一段历史，不同史家有不同 frame。

## §1 Hook — 626 年 7 月 2 日玄武门之变

### 凌晨 4 点的 9 个人

626 年 7 月 2 日（武德九年六月初四）凌晨 4 点，长安皇宫北门"玄武门"。秦王李世民 28 岁，带 9 名亲信武将——尉迟恭、长孙无忌、侯君集等——在门内树下埋伏。

弓弦上箭。马蹄声从远处传来。

太子李建成（长兄，38 岁）和齐王李元吉（四弟，24 岁）从东宫出发，要进宫向父亲唐高祖李渊（60 岁）上朝奏事。两人不知道弟弟已经在门内等他们。

### 死亡的次序

进了玄武门，李世民现身，喊一声。李建成回头——李世民一箭射中，死。李元吉抓弓还击，箭软，三发不中——尉迟恭追上，杀。

宫内池塘上，李渊正划船消暑。尉迟恭穿着血甲闯进来报告：太子和齐王作乱，秦王已经把他们除掉。请陛下下诏。

李渊在船上听完，沉默。当天下诏立李世民为皇太子。两个月后（626 年 9 月 4 日）禅位。李世民登基，年号"贞观"。

### Careful framing——不血腥但承认

这件事不能用"龙颜大悦"或"神武英断"这类语言粉饰。事实是：

* 哥哥死了。
* 弟弟死了。
* 父亲被迫退位。

这是中国王朝史上的 "founder violence"（开创者暴力）——不是个例。汉高祖刘邦杀韩信、彭越、英布；明太祖朱元璋杀胡惟庸、李善长、蓝玉；雍正夺位疑案——开国 / 转型时期的暴力，是中国王朝政治的常见 pattern，不是中国独有的污点。罗马帝国从凯撒到屋大维，奥斯曼帝国苏丹兄弟相残（"fratricide law"）——比比皆是。

### 但接下来 23 年

李世民登基后 23 年（626-649），史称"贞观之治"：

* 减税（租庸调制定型）。
* 完善科举。
* 平突厥（630 年东突厥灭，太宗被尊"天可汗"）。
* 编《贞观政要》——后世明君手册。

"founder violence" → "good governance"——历史 pattern。这不是说暴力对了，是说"政治结果"和"政治起点"未必同向。7 年级要学会的是：评价历史人物，不能只看一刻。

但也别滑到另一极——说"为了好结果，过程不重要"。李建成不是无能之辈，他主持北边防务多年；李元吉是秦王军事集团的对手。胜利者写历史，《旧唐书·建成传》对太子的描写有多少是抹黑——是史学未解的问题。

* **618-626 高祖李渊**——晋阳起兵 617，称帝 618，长安立都。

* **后梁 907-923**——朱温 → 朱友珪弑父 → 朱友贞。
* **后唐 923-936**——李存勖 → 李嗣源 → 李从厚 → 李从珂。
* **后晋 936-947**——石敬瑭割燕云十六州给契丹，留下 400 年北方边患。
* **后汉 947-951**——刘知远 → 刘承祐。
* **后周 951-960**——郭威 → 柴荣（五代最有为之君，39 岁早逝）→ 7 岁恭帝。
* **十国**——南方相对稳定，南唐李煜（后主，"问君能有几多愁"）。

### 北宋 960-1126（167 年）

* **960-976 太祖赵匡胤**——陈桥兵变黄袍加身，"杯酒释兵权"（961）解决武将专权。
* **976-997 太宗赵光义**——"烛影斧声"夺位疑案，979 年灭北汉完成统一。
* **997-1022 真宗赵恒**——1004-1005 澶渊之盟（宋向辽岁币 30 万——和平 100 年）。
* **1022-1063 仁宗赵祯**——庆历新政（1043 范仲淹改革失败）。
* **1063-1067 英宗赵曙**——4 年早逝。
* **1067-1085 神宗赵顼**——王安石变法（1069-1085 熙宁变法），新党旧党之争。
* **1085-1100 哲宗赵煦**——元祐党争（高太后听政废新法 1085-1093 → 哲宗亲政复新法 1093-1100）。
* **1100-1126 徽宗赵佶**——25 岁即位，蔡京六任宰相，花石纲、方腊起义（1120）、宋江（1119）。1125 金军南下，徽宗禅位钦宗。
* **1125-1127 钦宗赵桓**——1126 年 12 月 / 1127 年 1 月靖康之变，徽钦二帝被掳东北。

### 南宋 1127-1279（152 年）

* **1127-1162 高宗赵构**——徽宗第九子，南渡称帝。1129 苗刘兵变（兵变废帝 26 天）。1141 绍兴和议（向金称臣 + 岁币）。1142 岳飞死狱中。
* **1162-1189 孝宗赵昚**——非高宗亲生（高宗无子，过继太祖后裔）。隆兴北伐（1163）败，但内政清明，史称"乾淳之治"。
* **1189-1194 光宗赵惇**——5 年精神病发作，被孝宗逼禅位。
* **1194-1224 宁宗赵扩**——韩侂胄北伐失败（1206 开禧北伐）。1200 朱熹死。
* **1224-1264 理宗赵昀**——理学正式列为国学（1241 朱熹从祀孔庙）。
* **1264-1274 度宗赵禥**——贾似道专权，蒙元开始全面南下。
* **1274-1276 恭帝赵㬎**——5 岁被俘北上，1276 临安陷。
* **1276-1278 端宗赵昰**——8 岁，文天祥、陆秀夫、张世杰拥立，福州 → 泉州 → 广州一路南撤。
* **1278-1279 帝昺赵昺**——8 岁。1279 年 3 月 19 日崖山海战，陆秀夫背帝跳海，10 万军民投海。

### 元朝 1271-1368（briefly）

* **1271 忽必烈建元**——1276 临安陷，1279 崖山。
* **1294 忽必烈死**——元朝权力快速衰落。
* **1351 红巾军起义**——刘福通、韩山童。
* **1368 朱元璋灭元**——明朝建立。元朝 ~97 年，是中国第一个完全外族王朝（辽金都只占北方）。

## §0 Author Note — How to Read This Topic

### Standards Alignment

This unit covers HSS-7.3 (Tang/Song China) and AP-WHAP-1.2 (East Asian Song Dynasty). Combined, they span ~661 years — from 618, when *Gaozu* Li Yuan founded the Tang, to 1279, when the Southern Song fell at the naval battle of Yashan. Sandwiched between are 53 years of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms (907-960) and 320 years of Northern + Southern Song (960-1279).

### Four Anchor Moments

The story can't be read as a flat timeline. I pick four moments as anchors:

1. **626 — Xuanwu Gate Incident**. *Taizong* Li Shimin, age 28, seizes the throne and inaugurates the Zhenguan reign — a Chinese-dynastic *founder-violence* → *good-governance* pattern.
2. **755 — An Lushan Rebellion**. Tang's collapse from peak. Eight years of war; northern population roughly halved (the *Old Tang History* records a drop from ~52.88 million to ~17 million — exact numbers debated, but the order of magnitude is uncontested).
3. **1126 — Jingkang Incident**. Northern Song falls. Emperors *Huizong* and *Qinzong* are taken north as captives — just over a decade after Zhang Zeduan finishes *Along the River During the Qingming Festival*.
4. **1279 — Battle of Yashan**. Southern Song ends. Chancellor Lu Xiufu jumps into the sea carrying the 8-year-old child emperor Bing on his back — China's first complete conquest by a non-Han power.

### Three Recommended Lenses

* ***Huizong* Zhao Ji (perpetrator-actor)** — artistic genius, political catastrophe. Took the throne at 18 in 1100; lost the empire at 44 in 1126. *Slender-gold* calligraphy + *Auspicious Cranes* painting + Chancellor Cai Jing + the Flower-and-Rock Network + Fang La's rebellion — all the same person.
* **Su Shi (lonely-mediator)** — 1037-1101, lived through three reigns. The Crow Terrace poetry case nearly killed him. Three exiles: Huangzhou, Huizhou, Danzhou. Neither the New Policy faction nor the conservatives could stand him.
* **Li Qingzhao (receiving-end)** — c. 1084-1155. Late-Northern-Song *ci* poet; fled south with husband Zhao Mingcheng after the Jingkang Incident; husband died, the antiquities collection scattered. Around age 57 she remarried Zhang Ruzhou and divorced him within months. Southern Song law jailed widows who initiated divorce — she served 9 days.

### Cross-Topic Pentahedron — Information Preservation

Li Qingzhao and Zhao Mingcheng's southern transport of the *Records on Metal and Stone* collection (1126-1129) joins four other Topics to form an *information-preservation pentahedron*:

1. **Mali al-Sa'di** — in 1655, the Timbuktu scholar al-Sa'di writes the *Ta'rikh al-Sudan*, preserving Mali oral history.
2. **Renaissance printing, 1440** — Gutenberg.
3. **Reformation, 95 Theses, 1517** — Luther + the press = viral information.
4. **Age of Exploration, 1493 *Inter Caetera*** — colonial division by written papal bull.
5. **Li Qingzhao's *Records*, 1126-1129** — fifteen cartloads of antiquities transported from Qingzhou across the Huai and Yangtze; her husband dies in Jiankang; the collection scatters in the chaos. Her *Postface* preserves not the objects, but the story of their loss.

Five Topics, five faces of the same question: *how does information survive violence?* North Africa uses oral-Arabic hybrid; Europe uses the printing press; colonial empires use papal-legal documents; China uses private collections + prefatory remembrance. Each face is a different surface of the same solid.

## §0.5 Tang-Song's Position in the 14th-Century Cross-Cultural Network

### The Joseph Needham Question

The Cambridge historian of science Joseph Needham (1900-1995) wrote 27 volumes of *Science and Civilisation in China*. He posed the question: 12th-century China (Northern Song) and 14th-century Mali both led contemporary Europe in technology, commerce, and urban scale — why did 18th-century Europe surge ahead?

Needham's answer: not civilizational deficit, but political choice. The Ming sea ban (from 1371) and Qing closed-door policy (from 1757) were active closures. Mali fell in 1591 to Moroccan musketeers — political collapse is not civilizational backwardness.

This contradicts the 19th-century Hegelian frame of *stagnant East / progressive West* that quietly seeps into many textbooks. A 7th-grader reading Song China for the first time should drop that framing first.

### 1271-1279 Mongol Conquest — Adjacent Coordinates

* **1271** — Kublai Khan declares the dynasty *Yuan*, capital at *Dadu* (modern Beijing).
* **1276** — Yuan forces take Lin'an (modern Hangzhou); the 5-year-old emperor *Gongdi* Zhao Xian is captured.
* **1279** — Battle of Yashan; Southern Song ends.
* **1324** — Mali's Mansa Musa makes the *hajj*; passes through Cairo and crashes the gold price for six months.
* **1352** — Ibn Battuta reaches Niani, capital of Mali.

The fall of Song (1279) and Musa's *hajj* (1324) are 45 years apart — within one person's living memory. The Yuan dynasty (1271-1368) overlaps the Mali golden age (1235-1645) for roughly 100 years.

### Four Northern Song Inventions, the 1492 / 1517 Chain

Four mature Song-era technologies each lay groundwork for later global history:

* **Gunpowder** — alchemical byproduct in the 9th century, militarized by Song in the 11th, transmitted via Mongol expansion to Central Asia in the 13th, to Europe in the 14th — a core tool of post-1492 colonial conquest.
* **Movable type** — Bi Sheng, clay type, 1041-1048. Gutenberg's 1440 metal type was an independent invention, but the concept had already moved along the Silk Road. Luther's 95 Theses spread across the German lands in 14 days in 1517 — the technology chain traces back to Song.
* **Compass** — Shen Kuo's *Mengxi Bitan* (1088) records magnetic declination. Reaches Arab navigators in the 13th century, Europe in the 14th — Columbus 1492.
* **Paper money (*jiaozi*)** — Chengdu merchants issue the world's first paper currency in 1023. Yuan dynasty scales it; Marco Polo's *Travels* marvels at it.

The takeaway for a 7th-grader is not *China was advanced*; it is *technology belongs to no civilization — it flows in the network*.

### "After Yashan, No More China" — Historiography Rebar

The phrase has no clear single origin; it became popular in Republican-era China. Two camps:

* **Qian Mu (1895-1990)** — in his 1942 *Outline of National History*, rejects the claim, holding that Yuan-Ming-Qing remained the principal carrier of Chinese culture.
* **James T. C. Liu (1919-1993)** — in 1962 proposed *China Turning Inward*, agreeing that post-Song Chinese civilization changed character — inward-turning, conservative, *shidafu* (literati-official) culture ossified.

This is *historiography* — not the facts themselves, but how historians frame them. 7th grade is when students first meet this distinction: the same history admits multiple framings.

## §1 Hook — 2 July 626, the Xuanwu Gate Incident

### Nine Men at 4 AM

2 July 626 (the 4th day of the 6th month, Wude year 9), 4 AM, the north gate of the Tang palace at Chang'an — the *Xuanwu* Gate. *Qin Wang* (Prince of Qin) Li Shimin, age 28, lies in ambush inside with nine trusted officers — Yuchi Gong, Zhangsun Wuji, Hou Junji among them.

Bowstrings drawn. Hoofbeats approach in the distance.

The Crown Prince Li Jiancheng (eldest brother, 38) and the Prince of Qi Li Yuanji (fourth brother, 24) ride from the Eastern Palace toward the throne hall to attend court before their father, *Gaozu* Li Yuan (60). Neither knows their brother is waiting for them.

### Order of Deaths

Inside the gate, Li Shimin emerges and shouts. Li Jiancheng turns — Li Shimin's arrow finds him; he dies. Li Yuanji draws his bow but it is slack; three shots miss. Yuchi Gong rides him down; he dies.

In a pavilion on the palace lake, Gaozu is rowing a boat to escape the heat. Yuchi Gong, in blood-stained armor, bursts in: the Crown Prince and Prince of Qi rebelled, *Qin Wang* has dispatched them, please issue the edict.

Gaozu listens on the boat. Silent. That same day he names Li Shimin Crown Prince. Two months later (4 September 626) he abdicates. Li Shimin takes the throne; the era is renamed *Zhenguan*.

### Careful Framing — Acknowledged, Not Sanitized

This cannot be wrapped in language like *the imperial countenance gleamed* or *divine martial decisiveness*. The facts are:

* The elder brother died.
* The younger brother died.
* The father was forced to abdicate.

This is *founder violence* in Chinese dynastic history — not an isolated case. Han Gaozu Liu Bang killed Han Xin, Peng Yue, Ying Bu; Ming Taizu Zhu Yuanzhang killed Hu Weiyong, Li Shanchang, Lan Yu; the Yongzheng succession remains contested. Founding-era violence is a recurring pattern in Chinese politics, not a uniquely Chinese stain. Roman succession from Caesar to Octavian; Ottoman *fratricide law* — the pattern is global.

### What Came Next — 23 Years

Across the 23 years of Li Shimin's reign (626-649), the *Zhenguan* era saw:

* Tax reform (the *zu-yong-diao* system stabilized).
* Civil-service exam refinement.
* Eastern Turkic Khaganate destroyed (630); Taizong honored as *Tian Kehan* (Heavenly Khan).
* Compilation of the *Essentials of Government of the Zhenguan Era* — a manual for later emperors.

*Founder violence* → *good governance*. Note the pattern, but don't simplify. To say *the outcome was good, so the process didn't matter* slides too far. Li Jiancheng was no incompetent; he had managed northern frontier defense for years. Li Yuanji led a rival military faction. The victors write history; how much of the *Old Tang History*'s portrait of Jiancheng is character assassination is an open question in the field.

## §2 661 Years — Compressed Timeline

### Tang 618-907 (289 years)

* **618-626 Gaozu Li Yuan** — Jinyang uprising 617, declared emperor 618, capital at Chang'an.
* **626-649 Taizong Li Shimin** — Xuanwu Gate 626, Zhenguan reign, defeated Eastern Turks 630, took Gaochang 640.
* **650-683 Gaozong Li Zhi** — Yonghui era, *Yonghui Code* (zenith of Chinese law); his late reign saw Wu Zetian's growing power.
* **690-705 Wu Zetian** — China's only female emperor; renamed dynasty *Zhou*; deposed 705, Tang restored.
* **705-712 Zhongzong / Ruizong** — Empress Wei, Princess Taiping, factional chaos.
* **712-756 Xuanzong Li Longji** — Kaiyuan flourishing (713-741); An Lushan rebellion erupts in 755 when he is 70.
* **755-763 An Lushan Rebellion** — eight years of war; northern population roughly halved.
* **763-805 mid-Tang** — *jiedushi* warlordism, eunuch dominance (Sweet Dew Incident, 835).
* **875-884 Huang Chao Rebellion** — Chang'an taken twice.
* **907 Zhu Wen usurps Tang** — Later Liang founded; the Five Dynasties begin.

### Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms 907-960 (53 years)

* **Later Liang 907-923** — Zhu Wen → Zhu Yougui (patricide) → Zhu Youzhen.
* **Later Tang 923-936** — Li Cunxu → Li Siyuan → Li Conghou → Li Congke.
* **Later Jin 936-947** — Shi Jingtang ceded the Sixteen Prefectures of Yan-Yun to the Khitan, leaving a 400-year northern border problem.
* **Later Han 947-951** — Liu Zhiyuan → Liu Chengyou.
* **Later Zhou 951-960** — Guo Wei → Chai Rong (the most capable Five Dynasties ruler, died at 39) → 7-year-old Gongdi.
* **Ten Kingdoms** — relatively stable south; Southern Tang's last emperor Li Yu (the *ci* poet "How much sorrow can one bear?").

### Northern Song 960-1126 (167 years)

* **960-976 Taizu Zhao Kuangyin** — Chenqiao mutiny, the *yellow robe* placed on him; "wine-cup release of military power" (961) defused the warlord problem.
* **976-997 Taizong Zhao Guangyi** — the *Candle-shadow and Axe-sound* succession mystery; conquered Northern Han 979 to complete unification.
* **997-1022 Zhenzong Zhao Heng** — 1004-1005 Treaty of Chanyuan (Song pays Liao 300,000 in annual tribute — 100 years of peace).
* **1022-1063 Renzong Zhao Zhen** — Qingli Reform (1043, Fan Zhongyan, failed).
* **1063-1067 Yingzong Zhao Shu** — died after 4 years.
* **1067-1085 Shenzong Zhao Xu** — Wang Anshi's New Policies (1069-1085 *Xining* reform); New vs. Old Party conflict.
* **1085-1100 Zhezong Zhao Xu** — Yuanyou faction war (Empress Dowager Gao reverses the New Policies 1085-1093 → Zhezong restores them 1093-1100).
* **1100-1126 Huizong Zhao Ji** — took the throne at 25; Cai Jing serves as chancellor six times; Flower-and-Rock Network, Fang La rebellion (1120), Song Jiang (1119). Jin invasion in 1125; Huizong abdicates to Qinzong.
* **1125-1127 Qinzong Zhao Huan** — December 1126 / January 1127 Jingkang Incident; Huizong and Qinzong taken north.

### Southern Song 1127-1279 (152 years)

* **1127-1162 Gaozong Zhao Gou** — Huizong's ninth son, declared emperor in the south. 1129 Miao-Liu mutiny (deposed for 26 days). 1141 Treaty of Shaoxing (vassal status to Jin + tribute). 1142 Yue Fei dies in prison.
* **1162-1189 Xiaozong Zhao Shen** — not Gaozong's biological son (Gaozong sonless; adopted from Taizu's line). Longxing Northern Expedition (1163) failed, but his domestic governance was clean — the *Qiandao-Chunxi* prosperity.
* **1189-1194 Guangzong Zhao Dun** — five years of mental illness; forced to abdicate by Xiaozong.
* **1194-1224 Ningzong Zhao Kuo** — Han Tuozhou's failed Northern Expedition (1206 Kaixi). Zhu Xi dies in 1200.
* **1224-1264 Lizong Zhao Yun** — Neo-Confucianism formally state ideology (1241 Zhu Xi enshrined in the Confucian temple).
* **1264-1274 Duzong Zhao Qi** — Chancellor Jia Sidao's monopoly; Mongol invasion intensifies.
* **1274-1276 Gongdi Zhao Xian** — captured at age 5; 1276 Lin'an falls.
* **1276-1278 Duanzong Zhao Shi** — age 8; Wen Tianxiang, Lu Xiufu, Zhang Shijie support him through Fuzhou → Quanzhou → Guangzhou retreat.
* **1278-1279 Bing Di Zhao Bing** — age 8. 19 March 1279, Battle of Yashan; Lu Xiufu jumps into the sea carrying him; 100,000 soldiers and civilians follow.

### Yuan 1271-1368 (briefly)

* **1271 Kublai founds Yuan** — 1276 Lin'an falls, 1279 Yashan.
* **1294 Kublai dies** — Yuan power declines rapidly.
* **1351 Red Turban Rebellion** — Liu Futong, Han Shantong.
* **1368 Zhu Yuanzhang destroys Yuan** — Ming founded. Yuan ruled ~97 years; the first dynasty in which a non-Han power held the entire Chinese realm (Liao and Jin had only held the north).
