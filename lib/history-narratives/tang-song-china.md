## §0 author note — 这个 Topic 怎么读

### 课标对位

这一章对 HSS-7.3（唐宋中国）和 AP-WHAP-1.2（东亚宋朝）。两个课标合并起来覆盖 ~661 年——618 年唐高祖李渊建国，到 1279 年崖山海战南宋灭亡。中间夹着 53 年的五代十国（907-960）和 北宋-南宋 320 年（960-1279）。

### 4 个关键时刻锚点

整段历史不能平铺直叙。我挑 4 个时刻当锚点：

1. **626 年玄武门之变**——唐太宗李世民 28 岁夺位，开贞观之治。"founder violence" → "good governance" 的中国王朝典型 pattern。
2. **755 年安史之乱**——大唐由盛转衰。8 年战乱，北方人口减半（《旧唐书》记载从 5288 万降到约 1700 万——数字争议大，但减半的量级学界无大异议）。
3. **1126 年靖康之变**——北宋亡。徽宗、钦宗父子被金军掳到东北，《清明上河图》刚画完十几年。
4. **1279 年崖山海战**——南宋亡。陆秀夫背 8 岁的小皇帝赵昺跳海。中华史上第一次完全被外族征服。

### 3 个 lens 推荐

* **宋徽宗赵佶（perpetrator-actor）**——艺术天才、政治灾难。1100 年 18 岁即位，1126 年 44 岁亡国。瘦金体 + 《瑞鹤图》 + 蔡京 + 花石纲 + 方腊起义——同一个人。
* **苏轼（lonely-mediator）**——1037-1101，跨神宗、哲宗、徽宗三朝。乌台诗案差点死，黄州 / 惠州 / 儋州三次贬谪。新党旧党都不容他——"莫听穿林打叶声"。
* **李清照（receiving-end）**——1084-1155 左右。北宋末年闺秀词人，靖康之变后跟丈夫赵明诚南渡，丈夫死，文物散，1132 年 49 岁改嫁张汝舟——这场婚姻只持续 100 天，她检举丈夫并诉讼。按宋律"妻告夫"即使胜诉也要坐牢两年，靠翰林学士綦崇礼营救，她坐了 9 天牢出狱。

### 跨 Topic 五面体——information preservation

李清照-赵明诚的《金石录》文物南运（1126-1129），跟另外 4 个 Topic 拼成"信息保存五面体"：

1. **Mali al-Sa'di**——1655 年廷巴克图学者 al-Sa'di 写《Ta'rikh al-Sudan》（苏丹史），保存 Mali 帝国口述史。
2. **文艺复兴 1440 印刷术**——古登堡。
3. **Reformation 95 Theses 1517**——路德 + 印刷术放大 = 信息病毒式扩散。
4. **AoE 1493 Inter Caetera 教皇诏书**——殖民地划分依靠书面文件。
5. **李清照《金石录》南运 1126-1129**——15 车文物从青州出发，过淮河长江，丈夫病死建康，文物在战乱中散尽。她写《金石录后序》——保存的不是文物，是失去的故事。

5 个 Topic 各从不同角度：北非用口述-阿拉伯文混合，欧洲用印刷术放大文本，殖民帝国用诏书法律化，中国用私人收藏 + 序跋追忆——5 面体每一面都是同一个问题"信息怎么熬过暴力"。

## §0.5 Tang-Song 在 14 世纪 cross-cultural 全球网络的位置

### Joseph Needham Question

英国剑桥的科技史家 Joseph Needham（李约瑟，1900-1995）写了 27 卷《Science and Civilisation in China》，提出一个问题：12 世纪的中国（北宋）和 14 世纪的 Mali 帝国，技术、商业、城市规模都领先同代欧洲——为什么 18 世纪欧洲反超？

Needham 的回答是：不是文明缺陷，是政治选择。明朝海禁（1371 年起）、清朝闭关（1757 年起）——主动关门。Mali 帝国 1591 年被摩洛哥火枪兵击溃——政治崩溃不等于文明落后。

这跟课本里"东方停滞 / 西方进步"的 19 世纪 Hegel 史观完全不同。7 年级学生第一次读到中国宋朝时，要先卸掉这个隐性 framing。

### 1271-1279 蒙元征服的时空相邻坐标

* **1271 年**——忽必烈建国号"大元"，定都大都（今北京）。
* **1276 年**——元军攻破临安（今杭州），南宋恭帝赵㬎 5 岁被俘。
* **1279 年**——崖山海战，南宋亡。
* **1324 年**——Mali 帝国 Mansa Musa 朝觐麦加，路过开罗散金 6 个月让金价崩盘。
* **1352 年**——Ibn Battuta 抵达 Mali 帝国首都尼亚尼。

宋亡（1279）跟 Mansa Musa 朝觐（1324）相距 45 年——同代人记忆里两件事并存。元朝（1271-1368）跟 Mali 黄金时代（1235-1645）正好叠合 ~100 年。

### 北宋四大发明的 1492 / 1517 链条

宋朝 320 年里成熟的 4 项技术，每一项都给后世全球史奠基：

* **火药**——9 世纪炼丹副产品，11 世纪宋朝军用，蒙古西征传到中亚 → 14 世纪欧洲 → 1492 后殖民征服的核心工具。
* **活字印刷**——毕昇 1041-1048 年泥活字。古登堡 1440 金属活字独立发明，但概念已在丝路传播。1517 年路德 95 条论纲靠印刷术 14 天传遍德意志——技术链回溯到宋。
* **罗盘**——宋朝沈括《梦溪笔谈》（1088）记载磁针偏角。13 世纪传到阿拉伯航海者 → 14 世纪欧洲 → 1492 哥伦布。
* **纸钞**（*jiaozi* 交子）——1005 年成都 16 户富商首发交子（民间私印），1023 年北宋官府接管设交子务（世界第一种国家纸钞）。元朝大规模推广，马可波罗游记惊叹。

7 年级要记的不是"中国先进"，是"技术不属于哪个文明，技术在网络里流动"。

### 注释家 vs 改革家——朱熹 1190 与 Luther 1517 的对照

宋儒朱熹 1190 年完成《四书章句集注》——他没有写新经，只是重新注释《大学》《中庸》《论语》《孟子》四部已经存在 1500 年的古书。但他这一次"重读"，决定了后来 700 年（1313 元仁宗指定为科举范本 → 1905 清末废科举）东亚知识分子如何理解儒家。朝鲜、日本、越南都把朱熹的注释作为官方教科书。

327 年后，欧洲发生一件结构相似的事。马丁·路德 1517 年贴出《九十五条论纲》，他没有写新福音，只是重新注释《新约·马太福音》第 4 章里希腊文 *metanoeite*——主流拉丁文翻译为"做忏悔" (do penance)，路德认为应译作"内心悔改" (turn your heart)。一个译法的差别，180 年后终结了天主教在北欧的垄断。

两次"重新注释经典塑造 700 年正统"——朱熹用程朱理学统一东亚，路德用因信称义裂开西欧。同一种历史机制 (解释权 = 影响力)，在两个文明里 327 年时差里各跑一次。这是 §3.6 朱熹和 Topic Reformation 1517 之间的直接钩子。

### "崖山之后无中华"——historiography 钢筋

这句话出处不明，民国时期流行。学界有两派：

* **钱穆（1895-1990）**——1942 年《国史大纲》反对此说，认为元明清仍是中华文化主体。
* **刘子健（1919-1993）**——1962 年提出 "中国转向内在"（China Turning Inward），同意宋以后中华文明 character 发生质变——内向、保守、士大夫文化定型化。

这是 historiography（史学史）问题——不是"事实"，是 "怎么解释事实"。7 年级开始接触 historiography 概念：同一段历史，不同史家有不同 frame。

## §1 Hook — 626 年 7 月 2 日玄武门之变

### 凌晨 4 点的 9 个人

626 年 7 月 2 日（武德九年六月初四）凌晨 4 点，长安皇宫北门"玄武门"。秦王李世民 28 岁，带 9 名亲信武将——尉迟恭、长孙无忌、侯君集等——在门内树下埋伏。

弓弦上箭。马蹄声从远处传来。

太子李建成（长兄，38 岁）和齐王李元吉（四弟，24 岁）从东宫出发，要进宫向父亲唐高祖李渊（60 岁）上朝奏事。两人不知道弟弟已经在门内等他们。

### 死亡的次序

进了玄武门，李世民现身，喊一声。李建成回头——李世民一箭射中，死。李元吉抓弓还击，箭软，三发不中——尉迟恭追上，杀。

宫内池塘上，李渊正划船消暑。尉迟恭穿着血甲闯进来报告：太子和齐王作乱，秦王已经把他们除掉。请陛下下诏。

李渊在船上听完，沉默。当天下诏立李世民为皇太子。两个月后（626 年 9 月 4 日）禅位。李世民登基，年号"贞观"。

### Careful framing——不血腥但承认

这件事不能用"龙颜大悦"或"神武英断"这类语言粉饰。事实是：

* 哥哥死了。
* 弟弟死了。
* 父亲被迫退位。

这是中国王朝史上的 "founder violence"（开创者暴力）——不是个例。汉高祖刘邦杀韩信、彭越、英布；明太祖朱元璋杀胡惟庸、李善长、蓝玉；雍正夺位疑案——开国 / 转型时期的暴力，是中国王朝政治的常见 pattern，不是中国独有的污点。罗马帝国从凯撒到屋大维，奥斯曼帝国苏丹兄弟相残（"fratricide law"）——比比皆是。

### 但接下来 23 年

李世民登基后 23 年（626-649），史称"贞观之治"：

* 减税（租庸调制定型）。
* 完善科举。
* 平突厥（630 年东突厥灭，太宗被尊"天可汗"）。
* 编《贞观政要》——后世明君手册。

"founder violence" → "good governance"——历史 pattern。这不是说暴力对了，是说"政治结果"和"政治起点"未必同向。7 年级要学会的是：评价历史人物，不能只看一刻。

但也别滑到另一极——说"为了好结果，过程不重要"。李建成不是无能之辈，他主持北边防务多年；李元吉是秦王军事集团的对手。胜利者写历史，《旧唐书·建成传》对太子的描写有多少是抹黑——是史学未解的问题。

## §2 661 年 timeline 浓缩

### 唐 618-907（289 年）

* **618-626 高祖李渊**——晋阳起兵 617，称帝 618，长安立都。
* **626-649 太宗李世民**——玄武门 626，贞观之治，灭东突厥 630，灭高昌 640。
* **650-683 高宗李治**——永徽之治，永徽律疏（中华法律巅峰），但晚年武则天专政。
* **690-705 武则天**——中国唯一女皇，国号"周"。废为太上皇 705，唐复辟。
* **705-712 中宗 / 睿宗**——韦后乱政、太平公主乱政。
* **712-756 玄宗李隆基**——开元盛世（713-741），到 755 年 70 岁时安史之乱爆发。
* **755-763 安史之乱**——8 年战乱，北方人口减半。
* **763-805 中唐**——藩镇割据形成，宦官专权（甘露之变 835）。
* **875-884 黄巢起义**——长安两次陷落。
* **907 朱温篡唐**——后梁开始，五代登场。

### 五代十国 907-960（53 年）

* **后梁 907-923**——朱温 → 朱友珪弑父 → 朱友贞。
* **后唐 923-936**——李存勖 → 李嗣源 → 李从厚 → 李从珂。
* **后晋 936-947**——石敬瑭割燕云十六州给契丹，留下 400 年北方边患。
* **后汉 947-951**——刘知远 → 刘承祐。
* **后周 951-960**——郭威 → 柴荣（五代最有为之君，39 岁早逝）→ 7 岁恭帝。
* **十国**——南方相对稳定，南唐李煜（后主，"问君能有几多愁"）。

### 北宋 960-1126（167 年）

* **960-976 太祖赵匡胤**——陈桥兵变黄袍加身，"杯酒释兵权"（961）解决武将专权。
* **976-997 太宗赵光义**——"烛影斧声"夺位疑案，979 年灭北汉完成统一。
* **997-1022 真宗赵恒**——1004-1005 澶渊之盟（宋向辽岁币 30 万——和平 100 年）。
* **1022-1063 仁宗赵祯**——庆历新政（1043 范仲淹改革失败）。
* **1063-1067 英宗赵曙**——4 年早逝。
* **1067-1085 神宗赵顼**——王安石变法（1069-1085 熙宁变法），新党旧党之争。
* **1085-1100 哲宗赵煦**——元祐党争（高太后听政废新法 1085-1093 → 哲宗亲政复新法 1093-1100）。
* **1100-1126 徽宗赵佶**——18 岁即位（哲宗 25 岁无嗣崩，端王赵佶以神宗第十一子继位），蔡京六任宰相，花石纲、方腊起义（1120）、宋江（1119）。1125 金军南下，徽宗禅位钦宗。
* **1125-1127 钦宗赵桓**——1126 年 12 月 / 1127 年 1 月靖康之变，徽钦二帝被掳东北。

### 南宋 1127-1279（152 年）

* **1127-1162 高宗赵构**——徽宗第九子，南渡称帝。1129 苗刘兵变（兵变废帝 26 天）。1141 绍兴和议（向金称臣 + 岁币）。1142 岳飞死狱中。
* **1162-1189 孝宗赵昚**——非高宗亲生（高宗无子，过继太祖后裔）。隆兴北伐（1163）败，但内政清明，史称"乾淳之治"。
* **1189-1194 光宗赵惇**——5 年精神病发作，被孝宗逼禅位。
* **1194-1224 宁宗赵扩**——韩侂胄北伐失败（1206 开禧北伐）。1200 朱熹死。
* **1224-1264 理宗赵昀**——理学正式列为国学（1241 朱熹从祀孔庙）。
* **1264-1274 度宗赵禥**——贾似道专权，蒙元开始全面南下。
* **1274-1276 恭帝赵㬎**——5 岁被俘北上，1276 临安陷。
* **1276-1278 端宗赵昰**——8 岁，文天祥、陆秀夫、张世杰拥立，福州 → 泉州 → 广州一路南撤。
* **1278-1279 帝昺赵昺**——8 岁。1279 年 3 月 19 日崖山海战，陆秀夫背帝跳海，10 万军民投海。

### 元朝 1271-1368（briefly）

* **1271 忽必烈建元**——1276 临安陷，1279 崖山。
* **1294 忽必烈死**——元朝权力快速衰落。
* **1351 红巾军起义**——刘福通、韩山童。
* **1368 朱元璋灭元**——明朝建立。元朝 ~97 年，是中国第一个完全外族王朝（辽金都只占北方）。

## §0 Author Note — How to Read This Topic

### Standards Alignment

This unit covers HSS-7.3 (Tang/Song China) and AP-WHAP-1.2 (East Asian Song Dynasty). Combined, they span ~661 years — from 618, when *Gaozu* Li Yuan founded the Tang, to 1279, when the Southern Song fell at the naval battle of Yashan. Sandwiched between are 53 years of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms (907-960) and 320 years of Northern + Southern Song (960-1279).

### Four Anchor Moments

The story can't be read as a flat timeline. I pick four moments as anchors:

1. **626 — Xuanwu Gate Incident**. *Taizong* Li Shimin, age 28, seizes the throne and inaugurates the Zhenguan reign — a Chinese-dynastic *founder-violence* → *good-governance* pattern.
2. **755 — An Lushan Rebellion**. Tang's collapse from peak. Eight years of war; northern population roughly halved (the *Old Tang History* records a drop from ~52.88 million to ~17 million — exact numbers debated, but the order of magnitude is uncontested).
3. **1126 — Jingkang Incident**. Northern Song falls. Emperors *Huizong* and *Qinzong* are taken north as captives — just over a decade after Zhang Zeduan finishes *Along the River During the Qingming Festival*.
4. **1279 — Battle of Yashan**. Southern Song ends. Chancellor Lu Xiufu jumps into the sea carrying the 8-year-old child emperor Bing on his back — China's first complete conquest by a non-Han power.

### Three Recommended Lenses

* ***Huizong* Zhao Ji (perpetrator-actor)** — artistic genius, political catastrophe. Took the throne at 18 in 1100; lost the empire at 44 in 1126. *Slender-gold* calligraphy + *Auspicious Cranes* painting + Chancellor Cai Jing + the Flower-and-Rock Network + Fang La's rebellion — all the same person.
* **Su Shi (lonely-mediator)** — 1037-1101, lived through three reigns. The Crow Terrace poetry case nearly killed him. Three exiles: Huangzhou, Huizhou, Danzhou. Neither the New Policy faction nor the conservatives could stand him.
* **Li Qingzhao (receiving-end)** — c. 1084-1155. Late-Northern-Song *ci* poet; fled south with husband Zhao Mingcheng after the Jingkang Incident; husband died, the antiquities collection scattered. In 1132 at age 49 she remarried Zhang Ruzhou — the marriage lasted only 100 days. She filed a criminal complaint against him; Southern Song law required prison time even for a wife who won her case, but the Hanlin academician Qi Chongli intervened on her behalf and she served only 9 days.

### Cross-Topic Pentahedron — Information Preservation

Li Qingzhao and Zhao Mingcheng's southern transport of the *Records on Metal and Stone* collection (1126-1129) joins four other Topics to form an *information-preservation pentahedron*:

1. **Mali al-Sa'di** — in 1655, the Timbuktu scholar al-Sa'di writes the *Ta'rikh al-Sudan*, preserving Mali oral history.
2. **Renaissance printing, 1440** — Gutenberg.
3. **Reformation, 95 Theses, 1517** — Luther + the press = viral information.
4. **Age of Exploration, 1493 *Inter Caetera*** — colonial division by written papal bull.
5. **Li Qingzhao's *Records*, 1126-1129** — fifteen cartloads of antiquities transported from Qingzhou across the Huai and Yangtze; her husband dies in Jiankang; the collection scatters in the chaos. Her *Postface* preserves not the objects, but the story of their loss.

Five Topics, five faces of the same question: *how does information survive violence?* North Africa uses oral-Arabic hybrid; Europe uses the printing press; colonial empires use papal-legal documents; China uses private collections + prefatory remembrance. Each face is a different surface of the same solid.

## §0.5 Tang-Song's Position in the 14th-Century Cross-Cultural Network

### The Joseph Needham Question

The Cambridge historian of science Joseph Needham (1900-1995) wrote 27 volumes of *Science and Civilisation in China*. He posed the question: 12th-century China (Northern Song) and 14th-century Mali both led contemporary Europe in technology, commerce, and urban scale — why did 18th-century Europe surge ahead?

Needham's answer: not civilizational deficit, but political choice. The Ming sea ban (from 1371) and Qing closed-door policy (from 1757) were active closures. Mali fell in 1591 to Moroccan musketeers — political collapse is not civilizational backwardness.

This contradicts the 19th-century Hegelian frame of *stagnant East / progressive West* that quietly seeps into many textbooks. A 7th-grader reading Song China for the first time should drop that framing first.

### 1271-1279 Mongol Conquest — Adjacent Coordinates

* **1271** — Kublai Khan declares the dynasty *Yuan*, capital at *Dadu* (modern Beijing).
* **1276** — Yuan forces take Lin'an (modern Hangzhou); the 5-year-old emperor *Gongdi* Zhao Xian is captured.
* **1279** — Battle of Yashan; Southern Song ends.
* **1324** — Mali's Mansa Musa makes the *hajj*; passes through Cairo and crashes the gold price for six months.
* **1352** — Ibn Battuta reaches Niani, capital of Mali.

The fall of Song (1279) and Musa's *hajj* (1324) are 45 years apart — within one person's living memory. The Yuan dynasty (1271-1368) overlaps the Mali golden age (1235-1645) for roughly 100 years.

### Four Northern Song Inventions, the 1492 / 1517 Chain

Four mature Song-era technologies each lay groundwork for later global history:

* **Gunpowder** — alchemical byproduct in the 9th century, militarized by Song in the 11th, transmitted via Mongol expansion to Central Asia in the 13th, to Europe in the 14th — a core tool of post-1492 colonial conquest.
* **Movable type** — Bi Sheng, clay type, 1041-1048. Gutenberg's 1440 metal type was an independent invention, but the concept had already moved along the Silk Road. Luther's 95 Theses spread across the German lands in 14 days in 1517 — the technology chain traces back to Song.
* **Compass** — Shen Kuo's *Mengxi Bitan* (1088) records magnetic declination. Reaches Arab navigators in the 13th century, Europe in the 14th — Columbus 1492.
* **Paper money (*jiaozi*)** — In 1005 a consortium of 16 Chengdu merchants issued the first *jiaozi* notes (privately printed); in 1023 the Northern Song court took the issue over and set up the *Jiaozi* Office, making *jiaozi* the world's first state-issued paper currency. Yuan dynasty scales it; Marco Polo's *Travels* marvels at it.

The takeaway for a 7th-grader is not *China was advanced*; it is *technology belongs to no civilization — it flows in the network*.

### Commentator vs Reformer — Zhu Xi 1190 and Luther 1517

In 1190 Zhu Xi finished his *Collected Commentaries on the Four Books*. He wrote no new scripture; he only re-annotated four texts (*Great Learning*, *Doctrine of the Mean*, *Analects*, *Mencius*) that had already existed for 1,500 years. Yet that one re-reading dictated how East Asian scholars understood Confucianism for the next 700 years (from Yuan Renzong's 1313 imperial-exam canon to the abolition of the exam in 1905). Joseon Korea, Tokugawa Japan, and Le-dynasty Vietnam all adopted his commentaries as state textbooks.

327 years later, something structurally similar happened in Europe. Martin Luther in 1517 nailed up the *Ninety-five Theses* — and again, no new gospel, just a re-reading. The Greek word *metanoeite* in Matthew 4:17 had been translated into Latin as *poenitentiam agite*, "do penance." Luther argued it should be "turn your heart" — inner repentance, not outer sacrament. That single gloss broke the Catholic Church's monopoly in northern Europe within 180 years.

Two cases of "re-annotating an old canon to reshape 700 years of orthodoxy." Zhu Xi unifies East Asia through Cheng-Zhu Neo-Confucianism; Luther fractures Western Europe through justification by faith. Same mechanism (interpretive authority equals influence) running once on each civilizational track, with a 327-year offset. This is the direct hook from §3.6 (Zhu Xi) into Topic Reformation 1517.

### "After Yashan, No More China" — Historiography Rebar

The phrase has no clear single origin; it became popular in Republican-era China. Two camps:

* **Qian Mu (1895-1990)** — in his 1942 *Outline of National History*, rejects the claim, holding that Yuan-Ming-Qing remained the principal carrier of Chinese culture.
* **James T. C. Liu (1919-1993)** — in 1962 proposed *China Turning Inward*, agreeing that post-Song Chinese civilization changed character — inward-turning, conservative, *shidafu* (literati-official) culture ossified.

This is *historiography* — not the facts themselves, but how historians frame them. 7th grade is when students first meet this distinction: the same history admits multiple framings.

## §1 Hook — 2 July 626, the Xuanwu Gate Incident

### Nine Men at 4 AM

2 July 626 (the 4th day of the 6th month, Wude year 9), 4 AM, the north gate of the Tang palace at Chang'an — the *Xuanwu* Gate. *Qin Wang* (Prince of Qin) Li Shimin, age 28, lies in ambush inside with nine trusted officers — Yuchi Gong, Zhangsun Wuji, Hou Junji among them.

Bowstrings drawn. Hoofbeats approach in the distance.

The Crown Prince Li Jiancheng (eldest brother, 38) and the Prince of Qi Li Yuanji (fourth brother, 24) ride from the Eastern Palace toward the throne hall to attend court before their father, *Gaozu* Li Yuan (60). Neither knows their brother is waiting for them.

### Order of Deaths

Inside the gate, Li Shimin emerges and shouts. Li Jiancheng turns — Li Shimin's arrow finds him; he dies. Li Yuanji draws his bow but it is slack; three shots miss. Yuchi Gong rides him down; he dies.

In a pavilion on the palace lake, Gaozu is rowing a boat to escape the heat. Yuchi Gong, in blood-stained armor, bursts in: the Crown Prince and Prince of Qi rebelled, *Qin Wang* has dispatched them, please issue the edict.

Gaozu listens on the boat. Silent. That same day he names Li Shimin Crown Prince. Two months later (4 September 626) he abdicates. Li Shimin takes the throne; the era is renamed *Zhenguan*.

### Careful Framing — Acknowledged, Not Sanitized

This cannot be wrapped in language like *the imperial countenance gleamed* or *divine martial decisiveness*. The facts are:

* The elder brother died.
* The younger brother died.
* The father was forced to abdicate.

This is *founder violence* in Chinese dynastic history — not an isolated case. Han Gaozu Liu Bang killed Han Xin, Peng Yue, Ying Bu; Ming Taizu Zhu Yuanzhang killed Hu Weiyong, Li Shanchang, Lan Yu; the Yongzheng succession remains contested. Founding-era violence is a recurring pattern in Chinese politics, not a uniquely Chinese stain. Roman succession from Caesar to Octavian; Ottoman *fratricide law* — the pattern is global.

### What Came Next — 23 Years

Across the 23 years of Li Shimin's reign (626-649), the *Zhenguan* era saw:

* Tax reform (the *zu-yong-diao* system stabilized).
* Civil-service exam refinement.
* Eastern Turkic Khaganate destroyed (630); Taizong honored as *Tian Kehan* (Heavenly Khan).
* Compilation of the *Essentials of Government of the Zhenguan Era* — a manual for later emperors.

*Founder violence* → *good governance*. Note the pattern, but don't simplify. To say *the outcome was good, so the process didn't matter* slides too far. Li Jiancheng was no incompetent; he had managed northern frontier defense for years. Li Yuanji led a rival military faction. The victors write history; how much of the *Old Tang History*'s portrait of Jiancheng is character assassination is an open question in the field.

## §2 661 Years — Compressed Timeline

### Tang 618-907 (289 years)

* **618-626 Gaozu Li Yuan** — Jinyang uprising 617, declared emperor 618, capital at Chang'an.
* **626-649 Taizong Li Shimin** — Xuanwu Gate 626, Zhenguan reign, defeated Eastern Turks 630, took Gaochang 640.
* **650-683 Gaozong Li Zhi** — Yonghui era, *Yonghui Code* (zenith of Chinese law); his late reign saw Wu Zetian's growing power.
* **690-705 Wu Zetian** — China's only female emperor; renamed dynasty *Zhou*; deposed 705, Tang restored.
* **705-712 Zhongzong / Ruizong** — Empress Wei, Princess Taiping, factional chaos.
* **712-756 Xuanzong Li Longji** — Kaiyuan flourishing (713-741); An Lushan rebellion erupts in 755 when he is 70.
* **755-763 An Lushan Rebellion** — eight years of war; northern population roughly halved.
* **763-805 mid-Tang** — *jiedushi* warlordism, eunuch dominance (Sweet Dew Incident, 835).
* **875-884 Huang Chao Rebellion** — Chang'an taken twice.
* **907 Zhu Wen usurps Tang** — Later Liang founded; the Five Dynasties begin.

### Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms 907-960 (53 years)

* **Later Liang 907-923** — Zhu Wen → Zhu Yougui (patricide) → Zhu Youzhen.
* **Later Tang 923-936** — Li Cunxu → Li Siyuan → Li Conghou → Li Congke.
* **Later Jin 936-947** — Shi Jingtang ceded the Sixteen Prefectures of Yan-Yun to the Khitan, leaving a 400-year northern border problem.
* **Later Han 947-951** — Liu Zhiyuan → Liu Chengyou.
* **Later Zhou 951-960** — Guo Wei → Chai Rong (the most capable Five Dynasties ruler, died at 39) → 7-year-old Gongdi.
* **Ten Kingdoms** — relatively stable south; Southern Tang's last emperor Li Yu (the *ci* poet "How much sorrow can one bear?").

### Northern Song 960-1126 (167 years)

* **960-976 Taizu Zhao Kuangyin** — Chenqiao mutiny, the *yellow robe* placed on him; "wine-cup release of military power" (961) defused the warlord problem.
* **976-997 Taizong Zhao Guangyi** — the *Candle-shadow and Axe-sound* succession mystery; conquered Northern Han 979 to complete unification.
* **997-1022 Zhenzong Zhao Heng** — 1004-1005 Treaty of Chanyuan (Song pays Liao 300,000 in annual tribute — 100 years of peace).
* **1022-1063 Renzong Zhao Zhen** — Qingli Reform (1043, Fan Zhongyan, failed).
* **1063-1067 Yingzong Zhao Shu** — died after 4 years.
* **1067-1085 Shenzong Zhao Xu** — Wang Anshi's New Policies (1069-1085 *Xining* reform); New vs. Old Party conflict.
* **1085-1100 Zhezong Zhao Xu** — Yuanyou faction war (Empress Dowager Gao reverses the New Policies 1085-1093 → Zhezong restores them 1093-1100).
* **1100-1126 Huizong Zhao Ji** — took the throne at 18 (Zhezong died at 25 without an heir; Prince Duan Zhao Ji, eleventh son of Shenzong, succeeded). Cai Jing serves as chancellor six times; Flower-and-Rock Network, Fang La rebellion (1120), Song Jiang (1119). Jin invasion in 1125; Huizong abdicates to Qinzong.
* **1125-1127 Qinzong Zhao Huan** — December 1126 / January 1127 Jingkang Incident; Huizong and Qinzong taken north.

### Southern Song 1127-1279 (152 years)

* **1127-1162 Gaozong Zhao Gou** — Huizong's ninth son, declared emperor in the south. 1129 Miao-Liu mutiny (deposed for 26 days). 1141 Treaty of Shaoxing (vassal status to Jin + tribute). 1142 Yue Fei dies in prison.
* **1162-1189 Xiaozong Zhao Shen** — not Gaozong's biological son (Gaozong sonless; adopted from Taizu's line). Longxing Northern Expedition (1163) failed, but his domestic governance was clean — the *Qiandao-Chunxi* prosperity.
* **1189-1194 Guangzong Zhao Dun** — five years of mental illness; forced to abdicate by Xiaozong.
* **1194-1224 Ningzong Zhao Kuo** — Han Tuozhou's failed Northern Expedition (1206 Kaixi). Zhu Xi dies in 1200.
* **1224-1264 Lizong Zhao Yun** — Neo-Confucianism formally state ideology (1241 Zhu Xi enshrined in the Confucian temple).
* **1264-1274 Duzong Zhao Qi** — Chancellor Jia Sidao's monopoly; Mongol invasion intensifies.
* **1274-1276 Gongdi Zhao Xian** — captured at age 5; 1276 Lin'an falls.
* **1276-1278 Duanzong Zhao Shi** — age 8; Wen Tianxiang, Lu Xiufu, Zhang Shijie support him through Fuzhou → Quanzhou → Guangzhou retreat.
* **1278-1279 Bing Di Zhao Bing** — age 8. 19 March 1279, Battle of Yashan; Lu Xiufu jumps into the sea carrying him; 100,000 soldiers and civilians follow.

### Yuan 1271-1368 (briefly)

* **1271 Kublai founds Yuan** — 1276 Lin'an falls, 1279 Yashan.
* **1294 Kublai dies** — Yuan power declines rapidly.
* **1351 Red Turban Rebellion** — Liu Futong, Han Shantong.
* **1368 Zhu Yuanzhang destroys Yuan** — Ming founded. Yuan ruled ~97 years; the first dynasty in which a non-Han power held the entire Chinese realm (Liao and Jin had only held the north).

### §3 主角群 — 9 个关键人物

#### 3.1 唐太宗 李世民 (599-649)

**出身**
李渊次子，关陇贵族世家，母窦氏。少年随父征战，17 岁起兵随李渊从太原出发，参与建立唐朝。

**关键事件**
- 626 年 7 月 2 日玄武门之变：李世民在长安宫城北门玄武门设伏，射杀长兄太子李建成、四弟齐王李元吉。同年 9 月 4 日继位，28 岁。
- 627-649 年贞观之治：在位 23 年，重用房玄龄、杜如晦、魏徵。魏徵直谏 200 余次，太宗多次采纳。
- 641 年文成公主入藏：嫁吐蕃赞普松赞干布，带工匠、佛经、医书，开启唐蕃通婚交流。
- 645-649 年三征高句丽：晚年战事消耗国力，未能克东北。
- 649 年崩于含风殿，51 岁，葬昭陵。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
贞观之治成为后世帝王模板，《贞观政要》传至日本朝鲜。「水能载舟，亦能覆舟」的政治哲学被纳入儒家教科书，影响中国政治伦理 1200 年。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
始于政变的合法性焦虑——靠后续治理实绩来重写历史评价，这种「founder violence → 长期善治」的模式，在很多国家的开国史叙事里都能看到对照。

#### 3.2 唐玄宗 李隆基 (685-762)

**出身**
唐睿宗第三子，太宗曾孙，武则天孙。710 年与姑姑太平公主联手发动唐隆政变诛韦后，27 岁助父登基。712 年继位，28 岁。

**关键事件**
- 712-741 年开元盛世：在位 29 年，用姚崇、宋璟、张九龄三任贤相。长安人口约 100 万，外国使节、商人、留学生云集。
- 745 年纳杨玉环为贵妃，时玄宗 60 岁，杨贵妃 26 岁。
- 752 年起用杨国忠（杨贵妃堂兄）为右相。
- 755 年 11 月 9 日安禄山在范阳（今北京）起兵，史称安史之乱。
- 756 年 6 月 18 日长安陷落，玄宗 71 岁带杨贵妃西逃蜀地。
- 756 年 7 月 13 日马嵬坡兵变，禁军将领陈玄礼率众杀杨国忠，逼玄宗缢杀杨贵妃，时年 38 岁。
- 762 年崩于太极宫，77 岁。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
开元盛世是中国古代经济文化的双高峰之一。安史之乱是中国中古史的分水岭——人口减半，藩镇割据从此持续 150 年，唐由盛转衰只用了一个夏天。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
盛世顶点同时埋藏崩溃种子的范式——前期 30 年治理成就被晚期 8 年用人失误几乎全部抵消，是国家治理研究里反复被引用的案例。

#### 3.3 杜甫 (712-770)

**出身**
河南巩县（今巩义）人，祖父杜审言为初唐诗人。开元年间科举不第，长期漫游齐鲁吴越。

**关键事件**
- 746 年 35 岁到长安求仕，困顿 10 年。
- 755 年安史之乱爆发，正在长安郊外，被叛军俘往长安城内。
- 757 年 4 月逃出长安投奔肃宗，授左拾遗。
- 757-759 年因直谏房琯事件被贬华州司功参军，后弃官西行。
- 759 年到成都，760 年在浣花溪畔建草堂。
- 761 年秋《茅屋为秋风所破歌》写于成都草堂——「安得广厦千万间，大庇天下寒士俱欢颜」。
- 765 年离蜀漂泊，766-768 年在夔州（今重庆奉节）写《秋兴八首》《登高》。
- 770 年冬死于湘江舟中，59 岁，贫病交加。一生写诗约 1500 首，存世 1400 余首。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
杜甫是安史之乱最重要的诗史见证者。「国破山河在，城春草木深」（《春望》写于 757 年长安）成为后世亡国诗的母题。北宋苏轼、黄庭坚都奉杜甫为宗，定型「诗圣」称号。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
个人命运被卷入历史大事件的写作者——颠沛流离不是文学题材而是日常生活，这种第一人称的战乱书写，跨越 1300 年仍然击中读者。

#### 3.4 王安石 (1021-1086)

**出身**
江西临川人，进士出身。1042 年 21 岁中进士第四名，任地方官 16 年，以基层经验闻名。

**关键事件**
- 1058 年 37 岁上《万言书》给仁宗，提出系统改革主张，未被采纳。
- 1069 年 48 岁被宋神宗任命参知政事，开始变法。
- 1069-1076 年新法陆续推出：青苗法（春放秋收，年息 20-30%）、市易法（官府收购囤积货物平抑物价）、保甲法（农村军事化保甲互保）、募役法（以钱代役）、方田均税法（重新丈量土地）。
- 1074 年因旱灾和反对派攻击第一次罢相。
- 1075-1076 年再度拜相，1076 年因长子王雱去世彻底辞官，回江宁（今南京）。
- 1085 年神宗崩，哲宗继位，太皇太后高氏听政，司马光为相，新法基本被废。
- 1086 年 5 月王安石死，65 岁。同年 9 月司马光也死。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
新旧党争从此撕裂北宋政坛 60 年——元祐党争（1086-1094）、绍圣党争（1094-1100）、靖康前夜的蔡京党争。变法本身成败争议至今未息：钱穆视之为亡国根源，邓广铭视之为先进改革。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
顶层设计型改革者的两难——理论自洽但执行扭曲，在地方层面催生新型官僚寻租，是经济政策史里反复出现的结构性问题。

#### 3.5 苏轼 (1037-1101) — lens 2 候选

**出身**
四川眉山人，苏洵长子，苏辙之兄。1057 年 21 岁中进士，主考官欧阳修惊为天人。父子三人「三苏」皆名列唐宋八大家。

**关键事件**
- 1071 年因反对王安石新法自请外放，任杭州、密州、徐州、湖州地方官。
- 1079 年 7 月「乌台诗案」，因诗中讥讽新法被捕入御史台狱 130 天，险被处死。
- 1080 年贬黄州（今湖北黄冈）任团练副使，无实权。在黄州 4 年写《赤壁赋》《后赤壁赋》《念奴娇·赤壁怀古》，自号东坡居士。
- 1086 年元祐党争，旧党司马光得势，召苏轼回京任翰林学士。但苏轼又反对全废新法，再度被排挤。
- 1094 年新党再起，蔡京等人重列「元祐党人碑」，苏轼贬惠州（今广东惠州），59 岁。
- 1097 年再贬儋州（今海南儋州），62 岁。儋州当时是流放最远之地，瘴疠之乡。渡海前在雷州与弟苏辙相别五日，作《与子由别》「与君世世为兄弟，更结来生未了因」——这是兄弟二人相处的最后一面。
- 1100 年徽宗即位大赦北还。
- 1101 年 7 月 28 日死于常州，66 岁。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
三度流放成就北宋文化最高峰——黄州 4 年、惠州 3 年、儋州 3 年。诗、词、文、书、画、美食（东坡肉）、园林皆开宗立派。被誉为「中国 11 世纪文艺复兴的化身」。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
政治失意和文化创造的悖论——三度流放反而催生最高产的创作期，跨文化的「困境美学」原型，从但丁流放到曼德尔施塔姆都能找到回声。

#### 3.6 朱熹 (1130-1200)

**出身**
徽州婺源（今属江西）人，南宋初期生于福建尤溪。父朱松为太学博士。19 岁中进士。

**关键事件**
- 1148-1163 年师从李侗，承二程（程颢、程颐）洛学。
- 1175 年与陆九渊在江西鹅湖寺辩论，「鹅湖之会」开理学心学分野。
- 1190 年集注《四书章句集注》——《大学》《中庸》《论语》《孟子》——为元明清三代科举标准教材。
- 1196 年「庆元党禁」，韩侂胄打击道学，朱熹被诬「伪学」，门生李闳祖等被流放。
- 1200 年 4 月死于建阳考亭，70 岁。
- 1209 年朝廷为朱熹平反追谥「文」。1241 年从祀孔庙。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
程朱理学经元代统治者采纳后成为官方意识形态，影响中国 700 年（1313 元仁宗指定《四书》为科举范本到 1905 年废科举）。东亚文化圈：朝鲜李朝（1392-1910）、日本江户幕府（1603-1868）、越南后黎朝（1428-1789）皆奉朱子学为官学。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
注释家的影响力——朱熹本人没有写过新书，但通过重新注释 4 部古典文本，重塑了东亚 700 年的思想框架，是「解释权决定影响力」的典型案例。

---

#### 3.7 宋徽宗 赵佶 (1082-1135) — lens 1 候选

**出身**
神宗第十一子，哲宗异母弟。1100 年哲宗 25 岁无嗣崩，徽宗以端王身份继位，时年 18 岁。

**关键事件**
- 1100-1126 年在位 26 年。重用蔡京、童贯、高俅。
- 创「瘦金体」书法，存世真迹《草书千字文》《秾芳诗帖》。
- 1112 年作《瑞鹤图》：画 20 只仙鹤盘旋宣德门顶，自题瘦金体记。
- 约 1110 年代《听琴图》：自画抚琴，蔡京题诗——皇帝与权臣的合像。
- 主持编《宣和书谱》《宣和画谱》，皇家画院（翰林图画院）培养画家如王希孟（《千里江山图》作者，18 岁完成此卷）。
- 1118-1125 年与金联合灭辽（海上之盟），辽 1125 年亡。
- 1126 年 1 月，金军南下围开封，徽宗禅位长子钦宗赵桓，自号太上皇。
- 1127 年 1 月（靖康元年闰十一月）开封陷，徽宗、钦宗、后妃、宗室、工匠约 3000 人被俘北上。
- 1135 年 6 月死于五国城（今黑龙江依兰），54 岁。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
中国艺术史最高峰皇帝之一，但同时是亡国之君。「靖康之耻」成为后世士大夫的精神创伤——岳飞《满江红》「靖康耻，犹未雪」即指此。北宋亡，南宋偏安 152 年。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
艺术天才与政治灾难同体——艺术成就和军事崩溃并存的悖论，让后人难以做单一道德评价，类似法国路易十六（钟表匠+断头台）和俄国尼古拉二世（家庭画家+革命受害者）。

#### 3.8 李清照 (1084-1155) — lens 3 候选

**出身**
山东济南人，父李格非为苏轼门生「苏门后四学士」之一，母王氏为状元王拱辰孙女。书香门第。

**关键事件**
- 1101 年 18 岁嫁太学生赵明诚 21 岁，赵父赵挺之时任尚书右丞。新婚共同搜集金石书画，开始撰写《金石录》（赵明诚主笔，李清照协助）。
- 1107 年赵挺之死，蔡京迫害，赵明诚夫妇返青州（今山东青州）老家，闲居 10 年。这 10 年是李清照创作高峰，词作如《醉花阴·薄雾浓云愁永昼》。
- 1117 年起赵明诚陆续出仕莱州、淄州知州。
- 1126-1127 年靖康之变，开封陷。李清照 43 岁、赵明诚 46 岁，开始大规模南运文物。
- 1128 年到江宁（今南京），文物存于金陵。
- 1129 年 8 月赵明诚赴湖州任途中染疫病死建康（今南京），49 岁。李清照 46 岁。**临终一幕（《金石录后序》自记）**：8 月 18 日赵明诚高烧，李清照从池阳赶到建康，船未到岸已闻噩耗。她见到丈夫时，他已自服胡椒散治疟，病势危重。她坐到床边——他临终却只顾"取笔作诗，绝笔而终"，竟没有半点"分香卖履"式为她安排后路的意思（曹操《遗令》将余香分赠诸妾、令妾辈卖履谋生，是那个时代丈夫为妻妾托付后事的典故；李清照含着遗憾记下这一句——"殊无分香卖履之意"）。这是夫妻 28 年金石共著的终点，也是李清照下一个 26 年的起点——她从一个收藏家变成一个无人托付的保存者。
- 1129-1132 年南奔流离，文物大半散失或被盗。
- 1132 年 5 月在杭州 49 岁再婚张汝舟。婚后发现张觊觎文物且有欺诈前科——这场婚姻持续 100 天。李清照检举诉讼,按宋律「妻告夫」即使胜诉也要服刑两年。靠翰林学士綦崇礼营救,她坐牢 9 天后获释。
- 1134 年完成《金石录后序》，记录与赵明诚的金石收藏始末，成千古名文。
- 约 1155 年死于杭州，71 岁。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
中国古典词坛公认第一女词人，「易安体」开创婉约派新境。《金石录》是中国金石学奠基著作之一。她的人生轨迹——从靖康前的金石收藏家到靖康后的颠沛流离——是北宋南宋之际士大夫家庭命运的缩影。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
战乱中文物保护者的个人代价——文化承担和身体流离同时发生，跨越性别和时代，让人想到二战期间的犹太收藏家或叙利亚博物馆员。

#### 3.9 文天祥 (1236-1283)

**出身**
江西吉州（今吉安）人。1256 年 20 岁中进士第一名（状元），殿试时理宗亲擢。

**关键事件**
- 1259-1275 年任地方官，多次因抨击权臣贾似道被罢官。
- 1275 年元军南下，文天祥 39 岁散尽家财在赣州募兵勤王。
- 1276 年 1 月被任右丞相赴元营谈判被扣留，2 月临安陷恭帝降。
- 1276 年 3 月文天祥从镇江脱险，辗转福建、广东组织抗元。
- 1278 年 12 月 20 日在五坡岭（今广东海丰）被元军张弘范俘获。文天祥服冰片自杀未遂。
- 1279 年 3 月被押至崖山海战现场，目睹宋军覆灭，写《过零丁洋》「人生自古谁无死，留取丹心照汗青」。
- 1279-1283 年囚于大都（今北京）兵马司土牢 4 年，元世祖忽必烈亲自劝降许以丞相，文天祥拒。
- 1281 年在狱中作《正气歌》「天地有正气，杂然赋流形」共 60 句。
- 1283 年 1 月 9 日（至元 19 年腊月初九）斩于柴市，47 岁。

**对 Tang-Song 影响**
文天祥与陆秀夫、张世杰并称「宋末三杰」。《正气歌》《过零丁洋》成为中国忠义气节的精神原型。明清之际、抗战时期都被反复援引。

**21 世纪 cross-cultural anchor**
个人对集体命运的最后承担——明知不可为而为之，结局已定但仍按内在原则行动，是道德哲学里「义务论 vs 后果论」的经典实例。

### §4 4 关键时刻详写

#### 4.1 626 年 7 月 2 日 玄武门之变 + 627-649 贞观之治

**当天细节**

626 年 7 月 2 日清晨（武德九年六月初四），唐高祖李渊三个儿子的内斗到达顶点。太子李建成 38 岁，秦王李世民 28 岁，齐王李元吉 24 岁。李世民事先已被告知建成、元吉计划在出征突厥的饯行宴上加害自己。

按《资治通鉴》卷 191 记载：李世民率长孙无忌、尉迟敬德、房玄龄、杜如晦、侯君集等 10 余人，在长安宫城北门玄武门内伏兵。建成、元吉骑马入朝至临湖殿，察觉变化欲返。世民呼，元吉张弓三发不中（因紧张未拉满弦），世民射建成毙之。元吉逃，被尉迟敬德追射坠马杀之。

随后尉迟敬德全副甲胄入太极宫，见高祖李渊于船上湖中：「秦王以太子、齐王作乱，举兵诛之。」高祖被迫下诏。

**贞观之治 23 年**

626 年 9 月 4 日李世民继位，年号贞观。次年起开始的 23 年治理（627-649）成为后世帝王模板：
- **房玄龄**（579-648）：宰相 22 年，以「精于吏治」闻名。
- **杜如晦**（585-630）：与房玄龄并称「房谋杜断」，可惜 46 岁早逝。
- **魏徵**（580-643）：原为太子建成幕僚，玄武门后被太宗收用。在位 17 年直谏 200 余次。最著名一次：643 年魏徵死，太宗叹「以铜为镜可正衣冠，以人为镜可知得失，今魏徵殁，朕亡一镜矣」。
- **641 年文成公主入藏**：嫁吐蕃赞普松赞干布，带工匠、医书、佛经、释迦牟尼 12 岁等身像（即后世大昭寺主供）。开启唐蕃 200 年通婚。
- **贞观律**：650 年由房玄龄等编定的《永徽律疏》（即《唐律疏议》）的前身，影响日本《大宝律令》（701）、朝鲜半岛新罗律令、越南李朝律令。

**「水能载舟亦能覆舟」**

太宗与魏徵对话见于《贞观政要》卷一：「君者舟也，人者水也。水能载舟，亦能覆舟。」此句虽出自《荀子·王制》，但因贞观君臣的政治实践被赋予新生命，成为儒家政治哲学的核心命题之一，影响中国政治伦理 1200 年。

**careful framing**

玄武门之变的伦理评价历来分歧：传统史学倾向于太宗「不得已」叙事，宋代司马光《资治通鉴》已开始批评太宗「篡改实录」。现代史学大多承认两点同时为真——政变的暴力是真实的，贞观之治的成就也是真实的。这两者之间的张力，本身就是制度史研究的重要对象。

#### 4.2 755-763 安史之乱

**755 年 11 月 9 日 范阳起兵**

755 年 11 月 9 日（天宝十四年十一月初九），范阳节度使安禄山在范阳（今北京一带）起兵反唐。安禄山时年约 52 岁，粟特血统，身兼范阳、平卢、河东三镇节度使，掌兵 15 万。起兵借口是「奉密诏讨杨国忠」。

玄宗时年 71 岁，得报后初不信。叛军 12 月攻陷洛阳，756 年 1 月安禄山在洛阳称帝，国号大燕。

**756 年 6 月 18 日 长安陷落 + 7 月 13 日 马嵬坡**

756 年 6 月 9 日潼关失守，守将哥舒翰被俘。6 月 13 日清晨玄宗仓促西逃，带杨贵妃、杨国忠、皇子皇孙、宫人 1000 余人。6 月 18 日叛军入长安。

7 月 13 日（六月十四日）至马嵬驿（今陕西兴平），禁军将领陈玄礼鼓动士兵哗变。先杀杨国忠及其子杨暄，再围驿站要求处死杨贵妃。高力士从内劝玄宗：「贵妃诚无罪，然将士已杀国忠，而贵妃在陛下左右，岂敢自安？」玄宗下令缢杀杨贵妃于驿前佛堂梨树下，时年 38 岁。

玄宗继续西行入蜀，太子李亨北上灵武。7 月 12 日（与马嵬坡几乎同时）李亨在灵武即位，是为肃宗，玄宗被尊太上皇。

**757 年 长安收复 + 763 年 平定**

757 年 9 月唐军在郭子仪、李光弼率领下，借回纥兵收复长安。10 月收复洛阳。

但叛军未灭。757 年正月安庆绪杀父安禄山，759 年史思明又杀安庆绪，761 年史朝义又杀史思明。直到 763 年 1 月史朝义在莫州（今河北任丘）自杀，安史之乱才告结束，前后 7 年 2 个月。

**人口减半的数字**

天宝十四年（755）唐朝在籍户数 891 万，约 5290 万人（一说 8000 万含未登记人口）。
广德二年（764）在籍户数 293 万，约 1690 万人（一说 2000 万）。

减半数字包含战死、瘟疫、流亡未户籍化、北方士族大规模南迁等多重因素。这是中国中古史最严重的人口断崖之一。

**杜甫《春望》同代见证**

757 年 3 月，杜甫困居长安城内（叛军占领期），写下《春望》：「国破山河在，城春草木深。」此诗成为后世亡国诗的母题。

**careful framing**

安史之乱是唐由盛转衰的瞬间，但「转衰」是结构性的而非戏剧性的——藩镇割据从此持续 150 年（直到 907 年朱温灭唐），中央集权在唐后半期实际上从未真正恢复。开元盛世和安史之乱之间，相隔不到 15 年。

#### 4.3 1126-1127 靖康之变

**1125 年 金灭辽 + 海上之盟的反噬**

1118 年起，宋徽宗、童贯主导「海上之盟」联金灭辽。1125 年 2 月金军俘辽天祚帝，辽亡。但金军发现宋军在收复燕云十六州过程中表现极弱，遂转兵南下。

**1126 年 1 月 第一次开封之围**

1125 年 12 月金军兵分两路南下：东路完颜宗望、西路完颜宗翰。1126 年 1 月东路金军逼近开封。徽宗惊惶，1 月 18 日（宣和七年十二月二十三）禅位长子赵桓（钦宗），时钦宗 25 岁。徽宗带亲信近臣南逃镇江。

1126 年 1 月底金军围开封，李纲组织防御。2 月金军要求割地、岁币、人质，宋朝同意。3 月金军撤围。

**1126 年 11 月 第二次开封之围 + 1127 年 1 月 城陷**

1126 年 8 月金军再次南下，11 月围开封。这次开封守备不足。

1127 年 1 月 9 日（靖康元年闰十一月二十五）开封外城陷。钦宗赴金营谈判被扣。
1127 年 2 月 6 日（靖康二年正月十日）金废徽宗、钦宗为庶人。
1127 年 4 月 20 日（靖康二年三月二十七）金军押徽宗、钦宗、后妃、宗室、工匠、技艺人约 3000 人北上，包括徽宗 9 子、钦宗皇后朱氏、徽宗皇后郑氏。北宋 167 年（960-1127）亡。

**1127 年 5 月 高宗南逃 + 南宋建立**

1127 年 5 月 1 日（靖康二年五月初一）徽宗第九子康王赵构在应天府（今河南商丘）即位，时年 21 岁，是为高宗，年号建炎。

1127-1138 年高宗辗转扬州、临安（今杭州）、越州、明州（今宁波）、海上、临安，被金军追击。
1129 年「苗刘兵变」，护卫将领苗傅、刘正彦在杭州逼高宗禅位 3 岁太子，月余被韩世忠等平定。
1138 年定都临安。
1141 年 11 月「绍兴和议」，宋向金称臣纳贡，年贡银 25 万两、绢 25 万匹，划淮河-大散关为界。
1142 年 1 月岳飞被赐死风波亭，39 岁。
1135 年 6 月徽宗死于五国城（今黑龙江依兰），54 岁。1156 年 6 月钦宗死于金，57 岁。

**careful framing**

靖康之变的悖论：北宋是中国古代经济文化最繁荣的朝代之一（1100 年开封人口 100 万，年财政收入约 1.6 亿贯），却在军事上几乎没有抵抗就崩溃。这种「文明顶点 + 武力崩溃」的同时性，是宋史研究的核心命题。岳飞《满江红》「靖康耻，犹未雪」一句的情感力量，源自对这种文明断裂的反复回望。

#### 4.4 1276-1279 宋元之交

**1276 年 2 月 临安降**

1271 年忽必烈建国号大元。1273 年元军破襄阳。1275 年元军主帅伯颜大举南下。

1276 年 2 月 4 日（德祐二年正月十八）元军兵临临安。谢太后（理宗皇后）以 5 岁恭帝赵显的名义递交降表。临安陷，南宋实质亡国（仅福州、广东、崖山等地小朝廷继续抗元 3 年）。

**1278 年 12 月 文天祥被俘 + 1283 年 1 月 大都殉国**

1276 年 5 月益王赵昰在福州即位，是为端宗，时 7 岁。文天祥任右丞相奉端宗辗转福建、江西、广东抗元。

1278 年 5 月端宗病死，弟赵昺 8 岁继位（生于 1272.2.12，按虚岁计——下同）。

1278 年 12 月 20 日文天祥在五坡岭（今广东海丰）被张弘范元军俘获。文天祥服冰片自杀未遂。

1279 年 3 月文天祥被押至崖山海战现场，目睹宋军覆灭，作《过零丁洋》：
「辛苦遭逢起一经，干戈寥落四周星。」「人生自古谁无死，留取丹心照汗青。」

1279 年文天祥被押至大都（今北京），囚于兵马司土牢 4 年。元世祖忽必烈亲自劝降，许以宰相位。文天祥答：「天祥受宋恩为宰相，安事二姓？」

1281 年在狱中作《正气歌》：「天地有正气，杂然赋流形。」共 60 句。

1283 年 1 月 9 日（至元十九年腊月初九）文天祥被斩于大都柴市口，47 岁。临刑面南而拜：「吾事毕矣。」

**1279 年 3 月 19 日 崖山海战**

1279 年 3 月 19 日（祥兴二年二月初六）崖山海战结束。此前一个月宋元水师对峙于今广东江门崖门一带，宋军主帅张世杰、陆秀夫，宋元宋军船只 1000 余艘，元军主帅张弘范率 500 余艘进攻。

3 月 19 日清晨元军总攻，宋军大败。陆秀夫见大势已去，先驱妻子入海，再背 8 岁少帝赵昺投海。其后宋军及随从军民跳海者据《宋史·陆秀夫传》记载约 10 余万人，「越七日，浮尸出于海十余万人」。

南宋 152 年（1127-1279）亡。中国第一次完全被北方游牧民族征服。

**「崖山之后无中华」的争议**

「崖山之后无中华」一语近年在网络流传，但学界对此命题有较大争议：
- 此说被认为始于明末清初遗民语境，但清代实际文献中「中华」概念依然延续。
- 1942 年钱穆在西南联大讲史时明确反对此种本质化命题：「中华文明从未中断」。
- 1962 年美籍华裔学者刘子健（James T.C. Liu）在 *China Turning Inward* 中提出南宋是中国「内向化」转折点，但未支持「中华灭亡」说。
- 21 世纪学界主流不接受这种本质化命题——朝代更替不等于文明终结，元代继承宋代财政、官制、儒学传统，明代继续制度化朱子学。

**careful framing**

崖山之战的悲剧性是真实的——8 岁皇帝投海、10 万军民殉国都是史实。但「崖山之后无中华」是后世建构的政治叙事，需要与具体史实区分。元代之后中国并未消失，但宋代那种以士大夫为主体的政治文化形态确实发生了深刻转变，这是宋元之际研究的核心命题之一。

### §3 Key Figures — 9 Protagonists

#### 3.1 Tang Taizong (Li Shimin, 599-649)

**Origin**
Second son of Li Yuan, from the aristocratic Guanlong clan. Joined his father's military campaigns at 17 when Li Yuan rebelled against Sui from Taiyuan and helped found the Tang dynasty.

**Key events**
- July 2, 626 Xuanwu Gate Incident: Li Shimin ambushed and killed his elder brother Crown Prince Li Jiancheng and younger brother Li Yuanji at the northern gate of Chang'an's palace. Took the throne September 4 at age 28.
- 627-649 Zhenguan era: 23-year reign with chief ministers Fang Xuanling, Du Ruhui, and the famous remonstrating official Wei Zheng (who memorialized over 200 direct criticisms of the emperor).
- 641 Princess Wencheng's marriage to Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo, bringing artisans, Buddhist scriptures, and medical texts.
- 645-649 Three failed campaigns against Goguryeo drained the treasury in his late years.
- Died 649 at age 51, buried at Zhao Mausoleum.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
The Zhenguan era became the template for later emperors. *Zhenguan Zhengyao* (Essentials of Zhenguan Governance) was transmitted to Japan and Korea. The political philosophy "water can carry the boat or capsize it" entered Confucian textbooks and shaped Chinese political ethics for 1,200 years.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
A founder whose legitimacy began with violence but was rewritten by sustained good governance — this "founder violence to long-term competent rule" pattern recurs across many national founding narratives.

---

#### 3.2 Tang Xuanzong (Li Longji, 685-762)

**Origin**
Third son of Tang Ruizong, great-grandson of Taizong, grandson of Wu Zetian. In 710 at age 27, he and his aunt Princess Taiping launched the Tanglong coup against Empress Wei. Took the throne in 712 at age 28.

**Key events**
- 712-741 Kaiyuan era: 29-year reign with three successive worthy ministers (Yao Chong, Song Jing, Zhang Jiuling). Chang'an's population reached approximately one million; foreign envoys, merchants, and students gathered there.
- 745 took Yang Yuhuan as imperial consort (Yang Guifei). Xuanzong was 60, Yang Guifei 26.
- 752 promoted Yang Guozhong (Yang Guifei's cousin) as right chancellor.
- November 9, 755: An Lushan rebelled at Fanyang (today's Beijing area), launching the An-Shi Rebellion.
- June 18, 756: Chang'an fell. Xuanzong, age 71, fled west to Sichuan with Yang Guifei.
- July 13, 756: Mawei Slope mutiny. Imperial guards killed Yang Guozhong and forced Xuanzong to order Yang Guifei strangled in front of a Buddhist altar; she was 38.
- Died 762 at age 77.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
The Kaiyuan era stands among ancient China's economic and cultural peaks. The An-Shi Rebellion was the watershed of medieval Chinese history — population halved, regional military governors gained autonomy that would last 150 years. The Tang shifted from peak to decline in a single summer.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
The paradigm of a peak that simultaneously seeds collapse — 30 years of governance achievement nearly canceled by 8 years of personnel failures, a case repeatedly cited in governance studies.

---

#### 3.3 Du Fu (712-770)

**Origin**
Born in Gongxian, Henan (today's Gongyi). His grandfather Du Shenyan was an early Tang poet. Failed the imperial examinations during the Kaiyuan era and spent years wandering through Qi, Lu, Wu, and Yue.

**Key events**
- 746: Arrived in Chang'an at age 35 seeking office; struggled there for 10 years.
- 755: Caught in the suburbs of Chang'an when the An-Shi Rebellion erupted; captured and brought into the city by rebels.
- April 757: Escaped Chang'an, joined Emperor Suzong, appointed left reminder.
- 757-759: Demoted after defending the disgraced Fang Guan; later resigned and traveled west.
- 759: Reached Chengdu. 760: Built a thatched cottage by Huanhua Stream.
- Autumn 761: Wrote "Song of My Cottage Wrecked by Autumn Wind" at the Chengdu cottage.
- 765: Left Sichuan. 766-768 in Kuizhou (today's Fengjie, Chongqing) wrote "Eight Autumn Meditations" and "Climbing High."
- Winter 770: Died on a boat on the Xiang River, age 59, in poverty and illness. Wrote about 1,500 poems in his lifetime, with 1,400+ surviving.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
Du Fu was the most important poetic witness to the An-Shi Rebellion. His "Spring View" (written in occupied Chang'an in 757) — "The state is broken, mountains and rivers remain; spring comes to the city, grass and trees grow deep" — became the archetype for later poems on national fall. Northern Song masters Su Shi and Huang Tingjian both treated Du Fu as patriarch, cementing his title "Sage of Poetry."

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
A writer whose personal fate became entangled with major historical events — displacement was not literary subject matter but daily life. This first-person witness writing of war still strikes readers across 1,300 years.

---

#### 3.4 Wang Anshi (1021-1086)

**Origin**
From Linchuan, Jiangxi. Passed the *jinshi* examination in 1042 at age 21, ranking fourth. Served as a local official for 16 years, becoming known for grassroots experience.

**Key events**
- 1058: At age 37 submitted his "Ten Thousand Word Memorial" to Emperor Renzong proposing systematic reform; not adopted.
- 1069: At age 48 appointed by Emperor Shenzong as participant in deliberating affairs of state, beginning the New Policies.
- 1069-1076: New Policies rolled out, including the Green Sprouts Law (state loans to peasants at 20-30% annual interest), the State Trade Law (government purchase and resale to stabilize prices), the Baojia Law (rural mutual-responsibility militia organization), the Hired Service Law (commutation of corvée to cash), and the Square Field Equal Tax Law (land resurveying).
- 1074: First dismissed as chancellor due to drought and opposition attacks.
- 1075-1076: Restored to chancellorship; resigned permanently in 1076 after his eldest son Wang Pang died, retiring to Jiangning (today's Nanjing).
- 1085: Emperor Shenzong died. Emperor Zhezong succeeded; Grand Empress Dowager Gao governed; Sima Guang became chancellor; the New Policies were largely abolished.
- May 1086: Wang Anshi died at 65. Sima Guang died that September.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
The factional struggle between New and Old parties tore Northern Song politics for 60 years — the Yuanyou faction war (1086-1094), the Shaosheng faction war (1094-1100), and the Cai Jing faction politics on the eve of Jingkang. The reform's success or failure remains debated: Qian Mu saw it as the root cause of dynastic collapse, while Deng Guangming saw it as progressive reform.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
The dilemma of top-down reformers — theoretically coherent but distorted in execution, generating new bureaucratic rent-seeking at the local level. A recurring structural problem in economic policy history.

---

#### 3.5 Su Shi (1037-1101) — lens 2 candidate

**Origin**
From Meishan, Sichuan. Eldest son of Su Xun, elder brother of Su Zhe. Passed the *jinshi* in 1057 at age 21; chief examiner Ouyang Xiu was astonished by his talent. The "Three Sus" all numbered among the Eight Masters of Tang-Song prose.

**Key events**
- 1071: Requested provincial assignment in opposition to Wang Anshi's New Policies; served as local official in Hangzhou, Mizhou, Xuzhou, and Huzhou.
- July 1079: "Crow Terrace Poetry Case" — arrested for poems read as satirical criticism of the New Policies; held 130 days in the Censorate prison; nearly executed.
- 1080: Demoted to Huangzhou (today's Huanggang, Hubei) as deputy military training commissioner without real authority. During his four years in Huangzhou wrote "Red Cliff Rhapsody," "Latter Red Cliff Rhapsody," and "Charm of Niannu: Recalling the Past at the Red Cliff." Adopted the name Layman of Eastern Slope (*Dongpo*).
- 1086: Yuanyou faction war. Old Party's Sima Guang in power; Su Shi recalled as Hanlin academician. But Su Shi opposed wholesale abolition of the New Policies and was sidelined again.
- 1094: New Party returned. Cai Jing and others compiled the "Yuanyou Faction Stele." Su Shi demoted to Huizhou (today's Huizhou, Guangdong) at 59.
- 1097: Demoted again to Danzhou (today's Danzhou, Hainan) at 62. Danzhou was then the most distant exile, a malarial frontier. Before crossing the strait, he met his younger brother Su Zhe at Leizhou for five days and wrote "Parting from Ziyou" — *"With you I will be brothers in life after life, tying again the unfinished bond of the next world."* It was the last time the two brothers saw each other.
- 1100: Emperor Huizong's amnesty allowed return north.
- July 28, 1101: Died in Changzhou at 66.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
Three exiles produced Northern Song's cultural peak — four years in Huangzhou, three in Huizhou, three in Danzhou. He founded schools in poetry, lyric verse, prose, calligraphy, painting, cuisine (*Dongpo* pork), and garden design. Often called "the embodiment of an 11th-century Chinese renaissance."

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
The paradox of political failure and cultural creation — three exiles instead generated the most productive creative period. A cross-cultural archetype of "aesthetics of adversity" with echoes from Dante's exile to Mandelstam.

---

#### 3.6 Zhu Xi (1130-1200)

**Origin**
From Wuyuan, Huizhou (today in Jiangxi), born in Youxi, Fujian early in the Southern Song. Father Zhu Song was an academician at the imperial university. Passed the *jinshi* at 19.

**Key events**
- 1148-1163: Studied under Li Tong, inheriting the Luo school of the Cheng brothers (Cheng Hao, Cheng Yi).
- 1175: Debated Lu Jiuyuan at Goose Lake Temple in Jiangxi. The "Goose Lake Meeting" marked the divide between the school of principle (*li*) and the school of mind (*xin*).
- 1190: Compiled *Collected Commentaries on the Four Books* — *Great Learning*, *Doctrine of the Mean*, *Analects*, *Mencius* — which became the standard examination text for the Yuan, Ming, and Qing dynasties.
- 1196: "Qingyuan ban on the Way." Han Tuozhou suppressed the Learning of the Way; Zhu Xi was accused of "false learning"; disciples like Li Hongzu were exiled.
- April 1200: Died at Kaoting, Jianyang, age 70.
- 1209: Court rehabilitated Zhu Xi with the posthumous title "Wen." 1241: Enshrined in the Confucian temple.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
Cheng-Zhu Neo-Confucianism, adopted by the Yuan rulers, became official ideology and shaped China for 700 years (from Yuan Renzong's 1313 designation of the *Four Books* as examination canon to the abolition of the imperial examinations in 1905). Across the East Asian cultural sphere: Joseon Korea (1392-1910), Edo-period Japan (1603-1868), and Later Le Vietnam (1428-1789) all treated Zhu Xi's learning as state orthodoxy.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
The reach of a commentator — Zhu Xi himself wrote no entirely new book, yet by re-annotating four classical texts he reshaped East Asia's intellectual framework for 700 years. A textbook case of "interpretive authority equals influence."

---

#### 3.7 Song Huizong (Zhao Ji, 1082-1135) — lens 1 candidate

**Origin**
Eleventh son of Emperor Shenzong, half-brother of Emperor Zhezong. In 1100 when the 25-year-old Zhezong died without an heir, Huizong, then known as Prince Duan, succeeded at age 18.

**Key events**
- 1100-1126: Reigned 26 years. Relied on Cai Jing, Tong Guan, and Gao Qiu.
- Created the "slender gold" (*shoujin*) calligraphic style; surviving works include *Cursive Thousand Character Classic* and *Nongfang Poem*.
- 1112: Painted *Auspicious Cranes* — twenty cranes circling the Xuande Gate, with a self-inscription in slender gold.
- Around 1110s: *Listening to the Qin* — a self-portrait playing the *qin*, with an inscription by Cai Jing — emperor and chief minister combined in one image.
- Sponsored the compilation of *Xuanhe Calligraphy Catalog* and *Xuanhe Painting Catalog*. The imperial painting academy (Hanlin Painting Academy) trained painters such as Wang Ximeng (creator of *A Thousand Li of Rivers and Mountains*, completed at age 18).
- 1118-1125: Allied with the Jin to destroy the Liao (Maritime Alliance). The Liao fell in 1125.
- January 1126: Jin armies pushed south and besieged Kaifeng. Huizong abdicated to his eldest son Emperor Qinzong (Zhao Huan) and titled himself Retired Emperor.
- January 1127: Kaifeng fell. Huizong, Qinzong, consorts, imperial clan, and craftsmen — about 3,000 people — were taken north as captives.
- June 1135: Died at Wuguocheng (today's Yilan, Heilongjiang) at age 54.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
One of the highest peaks in Chinese art history was reached under an emperor who also lost his dynasty. The "Humiliation of Jingkang" became a lasting trauma for the literati class — Yue Fei's "Manjianghong" line "the Jingkang shame is not yet washed clean" refers to this event. Northern Song fell, and Southern Song held only the south for 152 years.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
Artistic genius and political catastrophe in one body — the coexistence of artistic peak and military collapse makes simple moral judgment difficult. Comparable to France's Louis XVI (clockmaker on the way to the guillotine) or Russia's Nicholas II (family painter and revolutionary victim).

#### 3.8 Li Qingzhao (1084-1155) — lens 3 candidate

**Origin**
From Jinan, Shandong. Father Li Gefei was a disciple of Su Shi and one of the "Four Late Disciples of Su." Mother née Wang was the granddaughter of *zhuangyuan* Wang Gongchen. A scholarly household.

**Key events**
- 1101: Married the imperial university student Zhao Mingcheng at age 18; he was 21. Zhao's father Zhao Tingzhi was then right vice-minister. The newly married couple began collecting epigraphy and paintings together and started compiling *Records of Inscriptions on Bronze and Stone* (Zhao as principal author, Li Qingzhao assisting).
- 1107: Zhao Tingzhi died. Cai Jing's persecution drove the couple back to their ancestral home in Qingzhou (today's Qingzhou, Shandong) for ten years of retirement. These ten years were Li Qingzhao's creative peak, including the lyric "Drunk in the Shade of Flowers."
- From 1117: Zhao Mingcheng successively served as prefect of Laizhou and Zizhou.
- 1126-1127 Jingkang Incident: Kaifeng fell. Li Qingzhao (43) and Zhao Mingcheng (46) began the large-scale southward transport of their collection.
- 1128: Reached Jiangning (today's Nanjing); the collection was stored there.
- August 1129: Zhao Mingcheng died of illness in Jiankang (today's Nanjing) at 49 while traveling to take up a Huzhou post. Li Qingzhao was 46. **The deathbed scene (recorded in her own *Postface*)**: on August 18 Zhao spiked a high fever; Li set out from Chiyang and reached Jiankang while the boat was still off shore — by the time she landed she had already heard he was dying. When she found him, he had dosed himself with a *hujiao* (pepper) decoction for malaria and was no longer fully lucid. She sat at the bedside — but at the end he only "took up his brush, wrote a poem, and with that final stroke was gone," making not the slightest *fenxiang maili* — "share out the perfume, sell the slippers" — kind of arrangement for her future. (The phrase alludes to Cao Cao's last testament dividing his remaining incense among his consorts and asking them to support themselves by selling sandals — the era's convention for a husband providing for the women he leaves behind; Li Qingzhao records, with quiet grief, that there was *殊无分香卖履之意* — "no such intent to share the perfume and sell the slippers" at all.) That was the end of 28 years of joint collecting, and the start of Li Qingzhao's next 26 years: from collector to a preserver with no one to lean on.
- 1129-1132: Fleeing south through the chaos, most of the collection was lost or stolen.
- May 1132: At age 49 in Hangzhou, remarried Zhang Ruzhou. Soon discovered Zhang coveted the remaining collection and had a fraud record. The marriage lasted 100 days. She filed a criminal complaint — under Song law, even a wife's successful suit against her husband meant two years' imprisonment. Through the intervention of the Hanlin academician Qi Chongli, she was released after 9 days in jail.
- 1134: Completed the *Postface to Records of Inscriptions on Bronze and Stone*, recording her life of collecting with Zhao Mingcheng — one of the great essays in Chinese literature.
- Around 1155: Died in Hangzhou at 71.

**Impact on Tang-Song**
Considered the foremost female lyricist in classical Chinese literature; her "Yi'an style" opened a new dimension within the *wanyue* (delicate-restrained) school. *Records of Inscriptions on Bronze and Stone* is a foundational text in Chinese epigraphic studies. Her trajectory — from pre-Jingkang collector to post-Jingkang refugee — epitomizes the fate of literati households at the Northern-to-Southern Song transition.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
The personal cost of cultural preservation in wartime — cultural responsibility and physical displacement happening at once. Across gender and era, evokes Jewish collectors in WWII or museum staff in Syria.

#### 3.9 Wen Tianxiang (1236-1283)

**Origin**
From Jizhou, Jiangxi (today's Ji'an). Took first place (*zhuangyuan*) in the *jinshi* examination in 1256 at age 20; Emperor Lizong personally elevated him during the palace examination.

**Key events**
- 1259-1275: Served as local official; repeatedly dismissed for criticizing chief minister Jia Sidao.
- 1275: Mongol forces pushed south. Wen Tianxiang at 39 sold his family property in Ganzhou to raise troops in defense of the throne.
- January 1276: Appointed right chancellor; sent to negotiate at the Mongol camp and was detained. February: Lin'an fell, the boy emperor Gongdi surrendered.
- March 1276: Wen Tianxiang escaped from Zhenjiang and made his way through Fujian and Guangdong organizing resistance.
- December 20, 1278: Captured at Wupoling (today's Haifeng, Guangdong) by Zhang Hongfan's Mongol forces. Wen Tianxiang attempted suicide by ingesting bingpian (borneol) but failed.
- March 1279: Brought to the site of the Yamen sea battle; witnessed the destruction of the Song fleet. Wrote "Crossing Lonely Ocean": "Since ancient times who has not died? Let me leave a loyal heart to shine in the historical record."
- 1279-1283: Imprisoned for four years in the Bingmasi earth dungeon in Dadu (today's Beijing). Khubilai Khan personally tried to persuade him to surrender and offered the chancellorship; Wen refused.
- 1281: Wrote "Song of Righteous Spirit" in prison: "Heaven and earth have righteous spirit, mingled in all forms." Sixty lines total.
- January 9, 1283: Beheaded at Chaishi market in Dadu at age 47. Before execution he bowed south and said, "My task is done."

**Impact on Tang-Song**
Wen Tianxiang, Lu Xiufu, and Zhang Shijie are remembered together as the "Three Heroes of the Song's End." "Song of Righteous Spirit" and "Crossing Lonely Ocean" became prototypes for Chinese ideals of loyalty and integrity, repeatedly invoked at the Ming-Qing transition and during the Anti-Japanese War.

**21st century cross-cultural anchor**
An individual's final commitment to a collective fate — knowing it cannot succeed yet acting on internal principle. A classic case in moral philosophy of "deontology versus consequentialism."

### §4 Four Pivotal Moments

#### 4.1 July 2, 626 Xuanwu Gate Incident + 627-649 Zhenguan Era

**The day**

On the morning of July 2, 626 (4th day of the 6th month, 9th year of Wude), the rivalry among Emperor Gaozu Li Yuan's three sons reached its climax. Crown Prince Li Jiancheng was 38, Prince of Qin Li Shimin 28, Prince of Qi Li Yuanji 24. Li Shimin had been informed in advance that Jiancheng and Yuanji planned to harm him at a farewell banquet for a campaign against the Turks.

According to *Zizhi Tongjian* volume 191: Li Shimin led Zhangsun Wuji, Yuchi Jingde, Fang Xuanling, Du Ruhui, Hou Junji, and others, more than ten men, in setting an ambush inside the northern gate (Xuanwu Gate) of the Chang'an palace city. Jiancheng and Yuanji rode in to court and reached the Linhu Hall, but sensing trouble turned to leave. Shimin called out; Yuanji drew his bow three times but missed (under stress, unable to fully draw). Shimin shot Jiancheng dead. Yuanji fled; Yuchi Jingde rode him down and killed him.

Yuchi Jingde then entered the Taiji Palace in full armor and found Emperor Gaozu on a boat in the lake: "The Prince of Qin, because the Crown Prince and Prince of Qi rebelled, has raised troops and executed them." Gaozu was forced to issue an edict.

**Twenty-three years of Zhenguan governance**

On September 4, 626, Li Shimin took the throne under the era name Zhenguan. The 23-year reign that began the next year (627-649) became the template for later emperors:
- **Fang Xuanling** (579-648): Chancellor for 22 years, known as a master of administration.
- **Du Ruhui** (585-630): Paired with Fang Xuanling as "Fang the planner, Du the decider"; died early at 46.
- **Wei Zheng** (580-643): Originally an aide to Crown Prince Jiancheng, taken into Taizong's service after the Xuanwu Gate Incident. Memorialized over 200 direct criticisms during 17 years in office. Most famous: when Wei Zheng died in 643, Taizong sighed, "With bronze as a mirror one straightens one's clothes; with people as a mirror one knows gain and loss. Now Wei Zheng is gone, and I have lost a mirror."
- **641 Princess Wencheng to Tibet**: Married Tibetan king Songtsen Gampo, bringing artisans, medical books, Buddhist scriptures, and the Sakyamuni statue (depicting the Buddha at age 12), which became the central icon at the Jokhang Temple. Opened 200 years of Tang-Tibet matrimonial alliance.
- **Zhenguan Code**: The predecessor of the Yonghui Code (650), which became the Tang Code (*Tang Lü Shu Yi*). It influenced Japan's Taiho Code (701), Silla codes on the Korean Peninsula, and the codes of Vietnam's Ly dynasty.

**"Water can carry the boat or capsize it"**

The exchange between Taizong and Wei Zheng appears in *Zhenguan Zhengyao* volume one: "The ruler is the boat, the people the water. Water can carry the boat, and water can capsize it." Although the line originates in *Xunzi*'s "Wangzhi" chapter, the political practice of the Zhenguan ruler and ministers gave it new life as one of the central propositions of Confucian political philosophy, shaping Chinese political ethics for 1,200 years.

**careful framing**

The ethical assessment of the Xuanwu Gate Incident has long been disputed. Traditional historiography tended toward a "Taizong had no choice" narrative; Sima Guang's *Zizhi Tongjian* in the Song already began to criticize Taizong for "altering the veritable records." Modern historiography largely accepts that two things are simultaneously true — the violence of the coup was real, and the achievements of the Zhenguan era were real. The tension between them is itself an important object of institutional history.

#### 4.2 755-763 An-Shi Rebellion

**November 9, 755: Rebellion at Fanyang**

On November 9, 755 (9th day of the 11th month, 14th year of Tianbao), An Lushan, Military Commissioner of Fanyang, rebelled at Fanyang (around today's Beijing). An Lushan was about 52, of Sogdian origin, simultaneously holding Fanyang, Pinglu, and Hedong commissions and commanding 150,000 troops. The rebellion's pretext was "to carry out a secret edict and punish Yang Guozhong."

Emperor Xuanzong was 71 and at first refused to believe the report. Rebel forces took Luoyang in December; in January 756 An Lushan declared himself emperor of the Yan dynasty in Luoyang.

**June 18, 756 Chang'an Falls + July 13 Mawei Slope**

On June 9, 756 the Tongguan pass fell; defending general Geshu Han was captured. Early on June 13, Xuanzong fled west in haste with Yang Guifei, Yang Guozhong, princes and grandsons, and over a thousand palace attendants. Rebel forces entered Chang'an on June 18.

On July 13 (14th day of the 6th month) the imperial party reached Mawei Station (today's Xingping, Shaanxi). Imperial guard commander Chen Xuanli incited the soldiers to mutiny. They first killed Yang Guozhong and his son Yang Xuan, then surrounded the station demanding Yang Guifei's death. Chief eunuch Gao Lishi advised Xuanzong from inside: "The Honored Consort indeed has no crime, but the soldiers have already killed Guozhong, and as long as the Consort remains by Your Majesty's side, how can they feel safe?" Xuanzong ordered Yang Guifei strangled in front of a Buddhist altar beneath a pear tree; she was 38.

Xuanzong continued west into Sichuan; Crown Prince Li Heng went north to Lingwu. On July 12 (almost simultaneous with Mawei), Li Heng ascended the throne in Lingwu as Emperor Suzong; Xuanzong was honored as Retired Emperor.

**757 Chang'an Recovered + 763 Pacification**

In September 757 Tang armies under Guo Ziyi and Li Guangbi, with Uyghur cavalry, recovered Chang'an. Luoyang followed in October.

But the rebellion did not end. In the first month of 757 An Qingxu killed his father An Lushan; in 759 Shi Siming killed An Qingxu; in 761 Shi Chaoyi killed Shi Siming. Only when Shi Chaoyi committed suicide in Mozhou (today's Renqiu, Hebei) in January 763 did the An-Shi Rebellion end, after seven years and two months.

**The numbers behind a halved population**

In the 14th year of Tianbao (755), registered Tang households numbered 8.91 million, about 52.9 million people (some estimates including unregistered population reach 80 million).
In the 2nd year of Guangde (764), registered households were 2.93 million, about 16.9 million people (some estimates around 20 million).

The halving reflects multiple factors: war deaths, plague, refugees who fell off the registers, and large-scale southward migration of northern aristocratic clans. This was one of the most severe population cliffs in medieval Chinese history.

**Du Fu's "Spring View" as contemporary witness**

In March 757 Du Fu was trapped within Chang'an under rebel occupation. He wrote "Spring View": "The state is broken, mountains and rivers remain; spring comes to the city, grass and trees grow deep." The poem became the archetype for later poetry on national fall.

**careful framing**

The An-Shi Rebellion was the moment the Tang shifted from peak to decline, but the "decline" was structural rather than dramatic. Regional military governors retained autonomy for 150 years (until Zhu Wen ended the Tang in 907); central authority in the Tang's second half was never truly restored. The Kaiyuan era's peak and the rebellion were less than 15 years apart.

#### 4.3 1126-1127 Jingkang Incident

**1125 Jin Destroys Liao + the Maritime Alliance Backfires**

From 1118, Emperor Huizong and Tong Guan led the "Maritime Alliance" with the Jurchen Jin to destroy the Khitan Liao. In February 1125 Jin forces captured the Liao Tianzuo Emperor; the Liao fell. But the Jin also observed how poorly Song armies performed in the campaign to recover the Sixteen Prefectures of Yan and Yun, and turned south.

**January 1126: First Siege of Kaifeng**

In December 1125 Jin armies advanced south on two routes: eastern under Wanyan Zongwang, western under Wanyan Zonghan. By January 1126 the eastern army neared Kaifeng. Huizong panicked and on January 18 (23rd of the 12th month, 7th year of Xuanhe) abdicated to his eldest son Zhao Huan (Emperor Qinzong), who was 25. Huizong fled south to Zhenjiang with close attendants.

In late January Jin forces besieged Kaifeng. Li Gang organized defense. In February the Jin demanded territorial cessions, indemnities, and hostages, and the Song agreed. The Jin lifted the siege in March.

**November 1126: Second Siege + January 1127: City Falls**

In August 1126 Jin armies pushed south again; by November Kaifeng was again besieged. This time defensive preparations were inadequate.

On January 9, 1127 (25th day of the intercalary 11th month, 1st year of Jingkang) Kaifeng's outer city fell. Qinzong went to the Jin camp to negotiate and was detained.
On February 6, 1127 (10th day of the 1st month, 2nd year of Jingkang) the Jin demoted Huizong and Qinzong to commoners.
On April 20, 1127 (27th day of the 3rd month) Jin forces took Huizong, Qinzong, consorts, imperial clan, craftsmen, and skilled artisans — about 3,000 people — north as captives, including nine of Huizong's sons, Qinzong's empress née Zhu, and Huizong's empress née Zheng. The Northern Song's 167 years (960-1127) ended.

**May 1127: Gaozong Flees South + Southern Song Founded**

On May 1, 1127 (1st day of the 5th month, 2nd year of Jingkang) Huizong's ninth son, Prince Kang Zhao Gou, took the throne in Yingtian (today's Shangqiu, Henan) at age 21 as Emperor Gaozong, with the era name Jianyan.

From 1127 to 1138 Gaozong fled through Yangzhou, Lin'an (today's Hangzhou), Yuezhou, Mingzhou (today's Ningbo), the open sea, and back to Lin'an, pursued by Jin forces.
1129: "Miao-Liu Mutiny" — guard commanders Miao Fu and Liu Zhengyan in Hangzhou forced Gaozong to abdicate to his three-year-old crown prince. Han Shizhong and others suppressed the mutiny within a month.
1138: Capital fixed at Lin'an.
November 1141: Treaty of Shaoxing. Song acknowledged itself as Jin's vassal, paid annual tribute of 250,000 ounces of silver and 250,000 bolts of silk, and accepted the Huai River-Dasanguan line as the border.
January 1142: Yue Fei was given a death sentence at Fengbo Pavilion at 39.
June 1135: Huizong died at Wuguocheng (today's Yilan, Heilongjiang) at 54. June 1156: Qinzong died in Jin captivity at 57.

**careful framing**

The paradox of Jingkang: Northern Song was among the most economically and culturally prosperous dynasties in Chinese antiquity (Kaifeng had a population of one million in 1100; annual fiscal revenue was about 160 million strings of cash), yet it collapsed militarily with almost no resistance. This simultaneity of "civilizational peak and military collapse" is a central question of Song historiography. The emotional force of Yue Fei's "Manjianghong" line "the Jingkang shame is not yet washed clean" comes from repeatedly looking back at this civilizational rupture.

---

#### 4.4 1276-1279 Song-Yuan Transition

**February 1276: Lin'an Surrenders**

In 1271 Khubilai Khan declared the Yuan dynasty. In 1273 Yuan armies took Xiangyang. In 1275 the Yuan general Bayan launched a major southern campaign.

On February 4, 1276 (18th day of the 1st month, 2nd year of Deyou) Yuan forces reached Lin'an. Empress Dowager Xie (consort of Lizong) submitted a letter of surrender in the name of the five-year-old Emperor Gongdi Zhao Xian. Lin'an fell; Southern Song effectively ended (only small courts in Fuzhou, Guangdong, and Yamen continued resistance for three more years).

**December 1278: Wen Tianxiang Captured + January 1283: Martyred in Dadu**

In May 1276 Prince of Yi Zhao Shi was enthroned in Fuzhou as Emperor Duanzong at age 7. Wen Tianxiang served as right chancellor and accompanied the boy emperor through Fujian, Jiangxi, and Guangdong organizing resistance.

In May 1278 Duanzong died of illness; his eight-year-old brother Zhao Bing succeeded (born February 12, 1272 — ages here follow the Chinese traditional reckoning, *xusui*).

On December 20, 1278 Wen Tianxiang was captured at Wupoling (today's Haifeng, Guangdong) by Zhang Hongfan's Yuan forces. Wen attempted suicide with bingpian (borneol) but failed.

In March 1279 Wen Tianxiang was brought to the Yamen sea battle and witnessed the destruction of the Song fleet. He wrote "Crossing Lonely Ocean":
"Bitter toil from a single classic raised me up; for four wandering years amid sparse battle stars."
"Since ancient times who has not died? Let me leave a loyal heart to shine in the historical record."

In 1279 Wen Tianxiang was taken to Dadu (today's Beijing) and imprisoned for four years in the Bingmasi earth dungeon. Khubilai Khan personally tried to persuade him to surrender, offering the chancellorship. Wen replied, "Tianxiang received Song favor as chancellor — how could I serve a second house?"

In 1281 he wrote "Song of Righteous Spirit" in prison: "Heaven and earth have righteous spirit, mingled in all forms." Sixty lines total.

On January 9, 1283 (9th day of the 12th month, 19th year of Zhiyuan) Wen Tianxiang was beheaded at Chaishi market in Dadu at age 47. Before execution he bowed south: "My task is done."

**March 19, 1279: The Yamen Sea Battle**

The Yamen sea battle ended on March 19, 1279 (6th day of the 2nd month, 2nd year of Xiangxing). For the previous month Song and Yuan fleets had faced off in the waters of today's Yamen Pass area in Jiangmen, Guangdong. Song commanders Zhang Shijie and Lu Xiufu held over 1,000 vessels; Yuan commander Zhang Hongfan attacked with about 500.

In the early hours of March 19 the Yuan launched a general assault and the Song was crushed. Lu Xiufu, seeing all was lost, first drove his wife into the sea, then carried the eight-year-old boy emperor Zhao Bing on his back and leapt into the sea. According to *Songshi*'s biography of Lu Xiufu, soldiers and commoners who followed numbered over 100,000: "After seven days, more than 100,000 corpses floated to the surface of the sea."

The Southern Song's 152 years (1127-1279) ended. China was for the first time conquered in full by a northern steppe people.

**The "After Yamen, no more China" controversy**

The phrase "After Yamen, no more China" has circulated online in recent years, but it is much disputed in academic circles:
- The phrase is generally said to have arisen in the late Ming-early Qing loyalist context, but actual Qing-period writings continued to use the concept of *Zhonghua* without rupture.
- In 1942 Qian Mu, lecturing at Southwest Associated University, explicitly rejected such an essentialist proposition: "Chinese civilization has never been broken."
- In 1962 the Chinese-American scholar James T. C. Liu, in *China Turning Inward*, proposed that the Southern Song marked China's "inward turn," but did not endorse a "death of China" thesis.
- Mainstream 21st-century scholarship rejects the essentialist version — dynastic change is not the end of a civilization. The Yuan inherited Song fiscal, bureaucratic, and Confucian traditions; the Ming continued to institutionalize Zhu Xi's learning.

**careful framing**

The tragedy of Yamen is real — an 8-year-old emperor's plunge into the sea and 100,000 dead are historical facts. But "After Yamen, no more China" is a later political narrative, and needs to be distinguished from the events themselves. China did not vanish after the Yuan, but the literati-centered political-cultural form that defined the Song did undergo a deep transformation. That transformation is one of the central subjects of Song-Yuan transition studies.

### 5.1 杜甫《春望》—— 757 年,长安陷后第二年

**古文原文 (757):**

> 国破山河在,城春草木深。
> 感时花溅泪,恨别鸟惊心。
> 烽火连三月,家书抵万金。
> 白头搔更短,浑欲不胜簪。

**现代汉语翻译:**

国家[国 = country]破碎了,山河还在;京城[长安]进入春天,可是只见草木长得很深 [因为没人管,荒了]。感受到这个时世[时 = era],连花朵都让我流泪;痛恨着这场离别,连鸟叫声都让我心惊。战火[烽火 = beacon-fire,古代报警烽火]已经连续烧了三个月 [实际从 755 年 12 月安禄山起兵到 757 年春,整整一年半],一封家书可以抵得上万两黄金。我的白头发抓挠之后变得更短了,简直[浑欲 = nearly]都插不住发簪。

**English Translation (for grade 7 readers):**

The country is broken — but the mountains and rivers remain. Spring has come to the capital, and the grasses and trees grow thick [because no one tends them]. Feeling these times, even flowers make me weep; hating this separation, even bird-calls startle my heart. The war-fires have burned for three months on end. A letter from home is worth ten thousand pieces of gold. My white hair, scratched again, grows shorter still — I can scarcely fit the hairpin in.

**Background — 杜甫 (Du Fu) at 45:**

杜甫(712-770)在 755 年安禄山叛乱[An Lushan Rebellion]爆发后的第二年,正在长安城里被困。755 年 12 月安禄山从范阳(今北京)起兵,756 年 6 月攻陷长安,唐玄宗逃往四川。杜甫在出逃路上被叛军俘获,押回沦陷的长安。757 年春天,他在长安城内写下了这首诗。当时他 45 岁。

这首诗的力量在于它不是在描述战争——它是在描述战争之后的"日常":春天还会来,草还会长,鸟还在叫,但城市已经空了。这是 8 世纪中国诗歌中"国家失败"的最深刻一笔。

### 5.2 苏轼《念奴娇·赤壁怀古》—— 1082 年,黄州贬官

**古文原文 (1082) — 节选:**

> 大江东去,浪淘尽,千古风流人物。
> 故垒西边,人道是,三国周郎赤壁。
> 乱石穿空,惊涛拍岸,卷起千堆雪。
> 江山如画,一时多少豪杰。
>
> 遥想公瑾当年,小乔初嫁了,雄姿英发。
> 羽扇纶巾,谈笑间,樯橹灰飞烟灭。
> 故国神游,多情应笑我,早生华发。
> 人生如梦,一樽还酹江月。

**现代汉语翻译 — 节选:**

长江[大江]向东流去,浪头淘洗尽了千百年来的英雄人物。旧[故]堡垒的西边,人们说,那里是三国时候周瑜(字公瑾)在赤壁打仗的地方。乱石冲入天空,惊人的浪头拍打江岸,卷起像一千堆雪一样的浪花。江山美得像画,在那个时代有多少英雄豪杰啊。

我远远地想着周公瑾[周瑜]当年,他刚娶了小乔,英姿勃发。手拿羽毛扇、戴着青色头巾,在谈笑之间,曹操的船只[樯橹]就化成灰烟消散了。我神游在这片故[古]国土,多情之人应该笑我,头发早早就白了。人生像一场梦,我把这一杯酒洒[酹]给江上的月亮。

**English Translation (for grade 7 readers):**

The Great River flows east, and its waves have washed away a thousand years of heroes. West of the old fortress, people say, is the Red Cliff where Zhou Yu of the Three Kingdoms fought. Jagged rocks pierce the sky, fierce waves slap the shore, rolling up a thousand drifts of snow. The land is like a painting — how many heroes lived in that age! I think of Zhou Yu in his prime — Xiao Qiao newly his bride, his bearing brilliant and bold. Feather fan in hand, blue silk on his head, in the time of a laugh, the enemy's masts and oars turned to ash and smoke. I wander in spirit through this old country — those of feeling should laugh at me, my hair has whitened too soon. Life is like a dream — I pour one cup to the river's moon.

**Background — 苏轼 (Su Shi / Su Dongpo) at 46:**

苏轼(1037-1101)在 1080 年因"乌台诗案"[Wutai Poetry Case,他因诗中讽刺王安石变法被弹劾入狱]被贬到黄州(今湖北黄冈),做有名无实的"团练副使"。1082 年是他在黄州的第二年。当年他 46 岁。

这首词写的是赤壁——但不是真正三国时代周瑜打曹操的赤壁(那是在湖北蒲圻),而是黄州城外的一个"赤壁"。苏轼在贬谪、政治失败、年华老去的时候,借古来抒发"人生如梦"的感慨。它也代表宋词从"婉约派"(柳永之流)转向"豪放派"的转折点。

### 5.3 李清照《金石录后序》—— 1135 年,寡居写

李清照(1084-约 1155)是中国文学史上罕见的女性大词人。她和丈夫赵明诚在北宋末南宋初一起收藏金石(青铜器铭文 + 石碑拓本)和古书。1127 年金兵攻陷开封(靖康之变),北宋亡,他们带着收藏南逃。1129 年赵明诚在路上病死。1129 至 1135 年,李清照独自在战乱中辗转,丈夫毕生收集的文物大部分散失。

**古文原文 (1135) — 节选:**

> 然有有必有无,有聚必有散,乃理之常。人亡弓,人得之,又胡足道。所以区区记其终始者,亦欲为后世好古博雅者之戒云。

**现代汉语翻译:**

但是,有"拥有"就必定有"失去",有"聚集"就必定有"散失",这是道理的常态[即:这是必然规律]。一个人[楚国人]丢了弓,另一个人[也是楚国人]捡到了它,又有什么值得说的呢[此处用《孔子家语》中"楚人遗弓,楚人得之"的典故,意指失而不忧]。我之所以小心地[区区 = humbly]记录这些收藏从开始到散失的全过程,也是想给后世喜欢古物、博学高雅的人一个警戒罢了。

**English Translation (for grade 7 readers):**

But where there is having, there must be losing; where there is gathering, there must be scattering — this is the way of things. A man of Chu lost his bow, another man of Chu found it — what need is there to grieve? The reason I humbly record from beginning to end how these things came and went is to leave a warning for later lovers of antiquity, the learned and the refined.

**Background — 李清照 (Li Qingzhao) at ~51:**

李清照写《金石录后序》是在 1135 年前后,她已经寡居 6 年(赵明诚 1129 年卒)。她 1084 年生于山东济南,1101 年与赵明诚结婚。1127 年金兵攻陷开封,他们携带 15 车文物南逃。在江南六年之间,藏品在战乱、火灾、盗窃中一点点失散——李清照亲眼看着丈夫一辈子的工作消失。

**Knowledge Preservation Thread (information preservation 主题):**

这段史料属于 information preservation 这条贯穿多个 Topic 的线索。它跟以下事件可以并置阅读:

- **Mali al-Sa'di** — 17 世纪 Tarikh al-Sudan 的作者,在西非记录 Songhai 帝国的兴衰,因为 1591 年摩洛哥入侵后帝国知识体系正在消失 (Topic Mali Empire 1235)
- **Renaissance 印刷术** — 1450 年左右古登堡活字印刷机让欧洲文献保存量在 50 年内增加千倍 (Topic Black Death / Renaissance)
- **Reformation 95 Theses** — 1517 年路德把神学辩论用印刷术 4 周内传遍德国,这是知识保存与传播的工业化 (Topic Reformation 1517)
- **Age of Exploration 1493 信** — 哥伦布 1493 年回欧洲后写的信,8 个月内被翻成 7 种语言印刷,这是知识传播的近代化 (Topic Age of Exploration 1492)

李清照所写,是 12 世纪一个个体在文明动荡中保存知识的尝试——她用一篇序文,把已经失去的东西"记下来",这本身就是对抗遗忘的方式。这条主题在 7 个 Topic 中出现 5 次,值得读者注意。

### 5.4 文天祥《过零丁洋》—— 1279 年,被俘押北上

**古文原文 (1279):**

> 辛苦遭逢起一经,干戈寥落四周星。
> 山河破碎风飘絮,身世浮沉雨打萍。
> 惶恐滩头说惶恐,零丁洋里叹零丁。
> 人生自古谁无死,留取丹心照汗青。

**现代汉语翻译:**

我的辛苦经历,从读懂[起 = arise from]一部经书开始[一经 = 指通过科举]。干戈[兵器]稀少冷清[寥落]地度过了四个年头[四周星 = four years]。山河破碎得像风中飞舞的柳絮[飘絮],我个人的身世起伏不定像是雨水打着浮萍。在惶恐滩头我诉说着惶恐,在零丁洋上我叹息着孤单零丁[零丁 = lonely]。从古以来,人生哪有不死的呢?我要留下一颗赤诚之心[丹心 = red heart,即忠诚之心]来照亮历史[汗青 = 历史竹简]。

**English Translation (for grade 7 readers):**

My hardships began when I first opened the classics. War-arms have grown thin and few across four years. The mountains and rivers, broken like willow-fluff in wind; my own life rises and falls like duckweed under rain. At Huangkong Beach I spoke of fear; on Lingding Sea I sigh for being alone. From ancient times, who among the living has not died? Let me leave a red heart behind, to shine on history's scrolls.

**Background — 文天祥 (Wen Tianxiang) at 43:**

文天祥(1236-1283)是南宋末的丞相,1271 年元朝忽必烈正式立国后,他在 1275-1278 年率领残宋军队抵抗。1278 年 12 月在五坡岭(今广东海丰)被元军俘获。1279 年 1 月,押解他北上的元军船只经过零丁洋(今珠江口外伶仃洋),元将张弘范让他写信劝降还在崖山的宋军残部,他拒绝,反而写下这首诗。

写诗后两个多月,1279 年 3 月,崖山海战爆发,陆秀夫背着 8 岁的宋帝赵昺跳海,南宋亡。文天祥 1283 年 1 月在元大都(今北京)被斩首,享年 47 岁。这首诗距离他被斩前,还有 4 年。

文天祥这首诗,把"个人忠诚"与"历史书写"两件事直接联系在一起——他不期望活下来,但他要让"丹心"留在历史里。这是中国传统中"杀身成仁"思想的最具体一次表达。

## §5 EN: Primary Sources Deep Read

Four primary sources from 757 to 1279 — 522 years of Tang-Song writing.

### 5.1 EN: Du Fu, "Spring View" (757)

Du Fu (712-770) was 45 years old, trapped inside Chang'an after the An Lushan Rebellion of 755 broke the Tang dynasty's back. Rebel forces took Chang'an in June 756 and Emperor Xuanzong fled to Sichuan. Du Fu was captured and held in the fallen capital. In spring 757, he wrote: "The country is broken, but mountains and rivers remain. Spring fills the city — grass and trees grow thick. War-fires three months long. A letter from home is worth ten thousand in gold." The poem's power is not in describing war, but in describing the strange peace inside ruin: spring still arrives, but no one is there.

### 5.2 EN: Su Shi, "Memories of Red Cliff" (1082)

Su Shi (1037-1101), the most famous poet-official of the Northern Song, was 46 and serving a demoted exile in Huangzhou (modern Huanggang, Hubei). He had been arrested in 1079 in the "Crow Terrace Poetry Case" — accused of mocking Wang Anshi's reforms in his verses — and was now spending his second year in exile. Visiting a cliff that locals (mistakenly) said was the Three Kingdoms battle site of 208 CE, he wrote on the contrast between the heroes of that age and his own quiet defeat. The poem ends: "Life is like a dream — I pour one cup to the river's moon." This piece marks the shift in Song lyric verse from the delicate "graceful school" to the sweeping "bold school."

### 5.3 EN: Li Qingzhao, "Postface to Records on Metal and Stone" (1135)

Li Qingzhao (1084-c.1155) is one of the rare major women writers in the Chinese canon. She and her husband Zhao Mingcheng spent twenty years collecting bronze-inscriptions and rubbings from old steles. When Jin armies broke into Kaifeng in 1127 and the Northern Song fell, they fled south with 15 cartloads of antiquities. Zhao died of illness on the road in 1129. Between 1129 and 1135, Li lost most of the collection to war, fire, and theft. Her postface, written around 1135 when she was about 51, is calm: "Where there is having, there must be losing. Where there is gathering, there must be scattering. This is the way of things." She is recording the end of a lifetime's work to leave a warning for future scholars who love antiquities.

This text belongs to the "knowledge preservation" thread that runs across multiple topics: Mali's al-Sa'di writing the Tarikh al-Sudan in the 17th century, Renaissance printing after 1450, Luther's 95 Theses spreading across Germany in four weeks via printing in 1517, and Columbus's 1493 letter being printed in seven languages within eight months. Li Qingzhao's act — recording what is already gone — is one early move in that long human pattern.

### 5.4 EN: Wen Tianxiang, "Crossing Lingding Sea" (1279)

Wen Tianxiang (1236-1283) was the chief minister of the Southern Song in its last years. After Khubilai Khan declared the Yuan dynasty in 1271, Wen led resistance forces from 1275 to 1278. He was captured in December 1278. In January 1279, Yuan general Zhang Hongfan put him on a ship sailing north, passing Lingding Sea (today's Pearl River estuary). Zhang asked him to write to the last Song holdouts at Yashan asking them to surrender. Wen refused — and wrote this poem instead. Two months after the poem, the Battle of Yashan happened: minister Lu Xiufu carried the eight-year-old Song emperor on his back into the sea, and the Southern Song dynasty ended. Wen was executed in the Yuan capital (Beijing) in January 1283. The final couplet — "From ancient times, who among the living has not died? Let me leave a red heart behind, to shine on history's scrolls" — is the most cited two lines on personal loyalty in Chinese tradition.

## §6 影响 / Long-Term Consequences

### 6.1 短期影响 (10th–13th centuries)

唐朝 907 年正式覆亡之后,中国进入了 53 年的"五代十国"分裂时期(907-960)。北方相继出现五个短命王朝(后梁 907-923、后唐 923-936、后晋 936-947、后汉 947-951、后周 951-960),南方有十个区域性政权。960 年赵匡胤陈桥兵变建立北宋,统一中国大部分地区。

南宋 1279 年灭亡,是另一种短期影响:1271 年忽必烈[Khubilai Khan]在中国本部建立元朝,这是中国历史上第一次被外族(蒙古)完全统治。元朝持续到 1368 年,共 97 年。

### 6.2 长期影响:5 大科技输出

唐宋时期(618-1279)是中国科技对世界文明影响最大的一段。以下 5 项发明,塑造了 11 至 16 世纪的全球史:

**(1) 火药 [Gunpowder]:**
- 唐代末年(9 世纪)由炼丹士偶然发现
- 北宋《武经总要》(1044 年成书)记载多种火药配方,这是世界最早的火药军用文献
- 宋代用于"突火枪""火箭""震天雷"等武器
- 经阿拉伯人传到欧洲,1380 年代欧洲开始大规模军用
- 1517 年宗教改革之后,欧洲军事革命(火枪+火炮+步兵阵)依赖火药

**(2) 活字印刷 [Movable-Type Printing]:**
- 北宋毕昇 1040 年代发明胶泥活字
- 比德国古登堡(Gutenberg)1450 年早 410 年
- 但中国汉字常用字超过 5000,活字印刷在中国效率不如雕版
- 因此活字在中国一直是次要技术,真正改变文明的活字革命发生在欧洲(因为字母只有 26 个)
- 这是科技扩散史中一个重要案例:发明地不一定是受益最大的地方

**(3) 罗盘 [Magnetic Compass]:**
- 北宋《武经总要》(1044)首次描述指南鱼
- 沈括(1031-1095)在《梦溪笔谈》(1080 年代)中详细描述磁针指南并提到磁偏角
- 12 世纪通过阿拉伯水手传到欧洲
- 1492 年哥伦布跨大西洋航行,使用了源自唐宋的罗盘技术

**(4) 纸钞 [Paper Money]:**
- 1005 年成都 16 户富商联合首发"交子"(民间私印,以铁钱为锚)
- 1023 年北宋官府收归国有,设交子务于成都——这是世界第一种国家发行的纸钞
- 南宋发行"会子"
- 比欧洲第一张纸钞(瑞典 1661 年)早约 638 年
- 元朝继承这一制度,发行"交钞",但 1350 年代发生严重通货膨胀,纸钞体系崩溃
- 这是世界货币史上最早一次"无锚货币通胀"案例

**(5) 朱熹理学 [Neo-Confucianism]:**
- 朱熹(1130-1200)综合北宋理学家(周敦颐、张载、程颐、程颢)的思想
- 1313 年元仁宗下令科举以朱熹注《四书》为标准,延续至 1905 年清末废科举,共 592 年
- 影响范围远超中国:朝鲜李朝(1392-1897)、日本江户幕府(1603-1868)、越南后黎朝都将朱熹理学作为官方哲学
- 这是东亚 700 年共同思想框架的基础

### 6.3 跨文明直接连接

唐宋的影响通过 3 条线索直接进入了我们其他 Topic 的故事:

- **1492 哥伦布**:罗盘(11 世纪宋代)+ 火药(11 世纪宋代)是哥伦布跨大西洋航行的关键技术。没有唐宋,没有 1492。(Topic Age of Exploration 1492)
- **1517 路德**:活字印刷术虽然在中国发明,但古登堡 1450 年代在欧洲做出字母版的活字之后,1517 年路德的《九十五条论纲》在 4 周内传遍德国。中国发明 → 欧洲改造 → 改变信仰版图。(Topic Reformation 1517)
- **14 世纪货币网络**:1324 年马里曼萨穆萨开罗朝圣带去大量黄金,1350 年代元朝交钞通胀,这是 14 世纪欧亚非货币体系的两个极端事件——元朝展示了无锚货币的危险,马里展示了实物货币的传递。(Topic Mali Empire 1235)

## §6 EN: Long-Term Consequences

### 6.1 EN: Short-term aftermath

After the Tang collapsed in 907, China entered 53 years of fragmentation (the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms, 907-960) before the Song reunified it. After the Southern Song fell in 1279, the Mongol Yuan dynasty (1271-1368) ruled all of China — the first complete foreign conquest in Chinese history.

### 6.2 EN: Five technologies that changed the world

**Gunpowder.** Discovered by Tang alchemists in the 9th century. The Northern Song *Wujing Zongyao* (1044) gives the first written military formula. Spread westward through Arab intermediaries, gunpowder reached Europe in the 1380s. After the Reformation of 1517, the European "military revolution" — pikes, muskets, cannon — depended on it.

**Movable-type printing.** Invented by Bi Sheng in the 1040s, 410 years before Gutenberg in 1450. But Chinese script, with over 5,000 common characters, did not benefit from movable type as much as Europe's 26-letter alphabet did. The invention's birthplace was not the place of its largest impact — a recurring pattern in the history of technology.

**Magnetic compass.** First described in the *Wujing Zongyao* (1044). Detailed in Shen Kuo's *Dream Pool Essays* (1080s), which also notes magnetic declination. Reached Europe via Arab sailors in the 12th century. Columbus's 1492 voyage relied on this Tang-Song-derived technology.

**Paper money.** In 1005 a group of 16 Chengdu merchants pooled to issue the first *jiaozi* notes privately, backed by iron coins. In 1023 the Northern Song court nationalized the issue and set up a *Jiaozi* Office in Chengdu — making *jiaozi* the world's first state-issued paper currency, 638 years before Sweden's first paper note in 1661. The Yuan dynasty's *jiaochao* notes inflated catastrophically in the 1350s, an early case of unbacked-currency collapse.

**Neo-Confucianism.** Zhu Xi (1130-1200) synthesized the Northern Song philosophers into a system. In 1313 the Yuan court made his commentaries on the Four Books the basis for the imperial exam, which lasted until 1905 — 592 years. Through this lens, Korea's Joseon dynasty (1392-1897), Tokugawa Japan (1603-1868), and Vietnam's Le dynasty all built their official ideologies on Zhu Xi's framework. East Asian thought stands on Song foundations.

### 6.3 EN: Cross-cultural threads

Three direct links from Tang-Song to other Topics in this curriculum:

- **1492 Columbus**: compass and gunpowder, both Song technologies, made Atlantic crossings possible.
- **1517 Luther**: printing, Chinese in origin and European in adaptation, made the 95 Theses unstoppable.
- **14th-century money**: Mali's 1324 gold pilgrimage to Cairo and the Yuan's 1350s paper-money collapse mark the two ends of a global monetary spectrum, both visible in the same century.

## §7 历史辩论 / Historiographical Debates

唐宋研究在学界至少有 5 个长期未决的问题。每一个都值得 7 年级学生知道——不是为了找出答案,而是为了知道历史不是一本写好的书。

### 7.1 李约瑟之问 [Needham Question]

英国生物化学家、汉学家李约瑟(Joseph Needham, 1900-1995)从 1954 年开始出版《中国科学技术史》[*Science and Civilisation in China*],一共写了 7 卷。他提出一个让 20 世纪西方汉学界难以回答的问题:从 1 世纪到 15 世纪,中国在科技上一直领先西方;为什么 18 世纪以后中国被欧洲超越?

李约瑟自己倾向于回答:中国选择了科举官僚体系,把知识分子吸入政府,而不是吸入企业家阶层和发明家阶层;欧洲在 16 世纪以后则发展出独立的市民社会、独立的大学体系。这是制度选择,不是文明缺陷。

21 世纪的 Kenneth Pomeranz(《大分流》[*The Great Divergence*],2000)进一步指出:1750 年之前,中国江南与英国伦敦在生活水平、人均收入上是接近的;真正的"大分流"是 18 世纪末到 19 世纪的事情,跟煤炭、新大陆资源、工业革命相关。

这个问题没有标准答案,但它告诉我们:把文明的领先与落后看成"内在性格",几乎一定是错的。

### 7.2 王安石变法的评价

王安石(1021-1086)在 1069-1076 年主持北宋熙宁变法,推出青苗法、市易法、保甲法等。同代司马光(1019-1086)写《资治通鉴》,主张稳健保守,坚决反对变法。北宋朝堂从 1069 年起分裂为"新法党"和"旧党",一直延续到 1100 年代,严重削弱了北宋应对金兵的能力。

20 世纪学者邓广铭(1907-1998)倾向肯定王安石,认为他是中国历史上少见的有近代国家治理意识的改革家。21 世纪美国汉学家 Peter Bol(《历史的反思》[*Neo-Confucianism in History*],2008)则认为,新旧两党都是知识精英的内部争论,共同削弱了 11 世纪后期中国对北方军事威胁的应对能力。

这是一个"改革派 vs 保守派"在 11 世纪的具体辩论——而且没有谁赢。

### 7.3 重文轻武 vs 武力衰退

宋太祖赵匡胤在 961 年以"杯酒释兵权"[Cup of Wine to Release Military Power]让石守信等开国大将自愿交出兵权,确立了"以文制武"的基本国策。这让宋朝内部 300 年没有像唐朝那样的藩镇割据(安史之乱 + 黄巢起义型的内乱),但也让宋朝在面对辽、金、西夏、蒙古时军事力量始终薄弱。

历史学家钱穆(1895-1990)在《国史大纲》(1939)中既肯定这个选择带来的内部稳定,也批评它造成的对外软弱。这个张力——"内部稳定" vs "对外武力"——是任何长期帝国治理都要面对的根本两难。

### 7.4 「崖山之后无中华」的争议

20 世纪的一个流行说法是"崖山之后无中华"——意思是 1279 年崖山海战南宋亡后,真正的"中华文明"也就断了,后来的元明清只是接着旧名字。

这个说法历史上至少有 3 个学术回应:

- **钱穆 1942**:坚决反对。中华文明是连续的,元朝、明朝、清朝各有各的延续与转型。
- **刘子健 1962**(《中国转向内在》[*China Turning Inward*]):部分同意。他认为南宋之后中国思想从开放的世界主义转向内向的本土主义,这是一个真实的"内向化"。
- **21 世纪 globalist 史观**(Mark Edward Lewis、Timothy Brook 等):不接受文明本质化论。他们认为元明清都是中国史的有机一部分,把宋当作"真中华"是 19-20 世纪民族主义建构。

这是一个仍然在进行的辩论。21 世纪的中国学界、海外汉学界、东亚史学界都还在写论文回应它。

### 7.5 科举:精英流动还是意识形态机器?

科举从隋朝 605 年开始,在唐宋成熟,延续到 1905 年清末,共 1300 年。

支持科举正面评价的论点(钱穆、何炳棣):科举是世界历史上最早的、最大规模的"以考试代替血统"的精英筛选机制。何炳棣(1917-2012)在《明清社会史论》(1962)中通过家谱研究指出,明清科举录取者中相当比例来自非官宦家庭,科举确实带来一定的社会流动。

批评科举的论点(Benjamin Elman、Susan Mann):科举的考试内容是儒家经典,所有学生从小接受同一种思想训练,这本质上是一台思想标准化机器。20 世纪学者 Benjamin Elman(《文化、权力与政治》[*A Cultural History of Civil Examinations in Late Imperial China*],2000)强调,科举更多是"意识形态再生产"而不是"人才选拔"。

实际情况大概是两者都是。科举既给了平民一条窄路,也保证了帝国 1300 年的思想一致性。这两件事情同时发生。

## §7 EN: Historiographical Debates

### 7.1 EN: The Needham Question

Joseph Needham (1900-1995) asked in his *Science and Civilisation in China* (1954-): if China led the West in science from the 1st to the 15th centuries, why did Europe overtake China after the 18th? Needham's own answer: institutional choice. China absorbed its talent into the imperial exam bureaucracy; Europe developed independent universities and merchant classes. Kenneth Pomeranz's *The Great Divergence* (2000) refines this: as late as 1750, the lower Yangzi and the Thames basin had similar living standards; the real divergence is post-1800 and tied to coal, New World resources, and the industrial revolution. Either way, civilizational "essence" is the wrong frame.

### 7.2 EN: The Wang Anshi Reform Debate

Wang Anshi (1021-1086) led the Northern Song's reform program from 1069 to 1076. Sima Guang (1019-1086), the historian of the *Zizhi Tongjian*, opposed him. The court split into reform and conservative parties for the next forty years. Modern scholar Deng Guangming (1907-1998) tends to view Wang positively, as one of the rare premodern Chinese reformers with a sense of state administration. Harvard's Peter Bol (*Neo-Confucianism in History*, 2008) sees both factions as elite literati whose infighting weakened the empire's response to the Jin invasion.

### 7.3 EN: Civil over military?

In 961, the Song founder used the "Cup of Wine" banquet to ease his generals into retirement and lock in civilian-over-military rule. This gave the Song three centuries free of the warlord rebellions that had broken the Tang, but left the dynasty militarily weak against Liao, Jin, Xixia, and finally the Mongols. Qian Mu (1895-1990) in his *Outline of National History* (1939) argues this trade-off is the core tension of long-dynasty governance — internal stability paid for with external weakness.

### 7.4 EN: "After Yashan, there is no Zhonghua"

A 20th-century slogan claims that after the 1279 Battle of Yashan ended the Southern Song, the real "Chinese civilization" also ended, with Yuan-Ming-Qing being only continuations of a name. Three responses: Qian Mu (1942) firmly disagreed. James T. C. Liu (*China Turning Inward*, 1962) partly agreed — he saw real evidence of a shift from cosmopolitan openness to inward-turning. Recent globalist historians (Mark Edward Lewis, Timothy Brook) reject the essentialist framing entirely, treating Yuan-Ming-Qing as organic parts of Chinese history and the Song-as-true-China view as a 19th-20th-century nationalist construct. The debate is still active.

### 7.5 EN: Was the imperial exam meritocratic?

The imperial exam ran from 605 to 1905 — 1,300 years. Defenders (Qian Mu, Ho Ping-ti) point to genealogical evidence (Ho Ping-ti, *The Ladder of Success in Imperial China*, 1962) showing real social mobility under Ming-Qing exams. Critics (Benjamin Elman, *A Cultural History of Civil Examinations in Late Imperial China*, 2000) argue the exam was more about ideological reproduction than talent selection — every educated person received the same training in Confucian classics. Most likely both: a narrow ladder for commoners, and a thirteen-century guarantee of imperial ideological continuity.

## §8 跨文化对照 / Cross-Cultural Synchronic Frame

唐宋(618-1279)横跨 7 至 13 世纪。同时期的世界发生了什么?三个时间窗口:

### 8.1 600s–800s:Tang 早中期对照

- **Tang 618-907**:贞观之治(627-649)、开元盛世(713-741)、安史之乱(755-763)
- **加洛林王朝**:法兰克查理曼大帝[Charlemagne, 768-814 在位],800 年圣诞节在罗马加冕为"罗马人的皇帝",试图重建西方帝国。但他死后帝国 843 年分裂,远不如同期 Tang 的延续性
- **阿拔斯王朝**:巴格达智慧宫[House of Wisdom, 813 年起]在哈里发马蒙时代正式建立,系统翻译希腊、波斯、印度学问,这是伊斯兰黄金时代[Islamic Golden Age]的开端
- **拜占庭**:查士丁尼一世(527-565 在位)修复《查士丁尼法典》;希拉克略一世(610-641 在位)抵抗波斯。东罗马仍是地中海强权之一

唐朝长安(8 世纪人口 100 万+)与巴格达(9 世纪人口 100 万+)是 8-9 世纪 Eurasian 大陆的两极都市。两座城都是知识聚集地,都有大量外籍居民,都对周边产生强烈文化影响。

### 8.2 800s–1000s:五代 + 北宋早期对照

- **五代十国 907-960 + 北宋 960-1127**
- **科尔多瓦倭马亚 [Cordoba Umayyad] 756-1031**:伊比利亚半岛的伊斯兰文明高峰,科尔多瓦在 10 世纪是欧洲最大城市之一(可能 30-50 万人),拥有图书馆 70 余座
- **克吕尼改革 [Cluny Reform] 910 起**:法国克吕尼修道院启动天主教内部改革,11 世纪扩展为整个西欧的"格里高利改革"
- **玛雅古典末期 [Classic Maya Collapse] 800-1000**:中美洲玛雅古典低地城邦(蒂卡尔、卡拉克穆尔等)在 8-10 世纪相继崩溃,原因至今学界未有定论(干旱、战争、生态过载等假说)

### 8.3 1100s–1200s:北宋末 + 南宋对照

- **北宋末年 + 南宋 1127-1279**
- **十字军 1099**:1099 年第一次十字军攻陷耶路撒冷,这是基督教欧洲第一次主动向地中海东岸投射军事力量(详见 Topic Crusades 1099)
- **马里帝国 1235**:松迪亚塔·凯塔[Sundiata Keita]在西非建立马里帝国(详见 Topic Mali Empire 1235)
- **伊本·鲁世德 [Ibn Rushd / Averroes, 1126-1198]**:科尔多瓦的穆斯林哲学家,把亚里士多德重新引入欧洲思想,深刻影响 13 世纪经院哲学(阿奎那)
- **迈蒙尼德 [Maimonides, 1138-1204]**:科尔多瓦出生的犹太哲学家,《迷途指津》[*Guide for the Perplexed*]是中世纪犹太哲学最重要文本
- **印加 pre-Classic**:在南美洲安第斯山区的印加文明前身正在形成,真正的印加帝国 1438 年才正式建立

朱熹(1130-1200)与伊本·鲁世德(1126-1198)、迈蒙尼德(1138-1204)是 12 世纪三个文明的同代哲学综合者。三人都试图把宗教传统与理性思辨融合。这种同步性不是偶然——12 世纪是欧亚大陆思想综合的高峰期。

唐宋是 7-13 世纪 Eurasian 大网络的一极。它跟伊斯兰黄金时代(阿拔斯)、加洛林文艺复兴、拜占庭并行,跟 11-12 世纪的科尔多瓦、十字军、马里建国等同代。这不是"中国独立发展",这是世界史的一部分。

## §8 EN: Cross-Cultural Synchronic Frame

The Tang-Song span (618-1279) covers seven centuries. Three windows:

### 8.1 EN: 600s–800s — Tang and its peers

While Tang ruled from Chang'an, the Carolingian Charlemagne (r. 768-814) was crowned Holy Roman Emperor in 800, trying to rebuild a Western empire — though it fragmented by 843. The Abbasid caliphate established the House of Wisdom in Baghdad after 813, launching the Islamic Golden Age. Byzantium under Justinian (527-565) had codified Roman law; under Heraclius (610-641) it held off Persia. Chang'an and Baghdad were both million-person cities and the two poles of 8th-9th century Eurasia.

### 8.2 EN: 800s–1000s — Five Dynasties and early Song

While China cycled through the Five Dynasties and then unified under the Song, the Umayyad caliphate of Cordoba (756-1031) made Andalusia the cultural center of Western Europe. The Cluny reform began in 910, eventually triggering the Gregorian reform that reshaped Catholic governance. In the Americas, the Classic Maya cities of the southern lowlands collapsed between 800 and 1000 — the cause is still debated (drought, warfare, ecological overload).

### 8.3 EN: 1100s–1200s — late Northern and Southern Song

The First Crusade took Jerusalem in 1099 (Topic Crusades 1099). Sundiata Keita founded the Mali Empire in 1235 (Topic Mali Empire 1235). In Cordoba, Ibn Rushd (1126-1198) reintroduced Aristotle to European thought; Maimonides (1138-1204) wrote the *Guide for the Perplexed*. In the Andes, the pre-Inca cultures were consolidating before the Inca empire would form in 1438.

Zhu Xi (1130-1200), Ibn Rushd (1126-1198), and Maimonides (1138-1204) are three contemporary philosophical synthesizers from three civilizations. Each tried to fuse religious tradition with reasoned argument. The 12th century was a peak of Eurasian intellectual synthesis — China was one node, not a separate world.

## §9 历史观四派 / Four Schools of Tang-Song Historiography

### CN

研究 Tang-Song 中国不是单一答案。21 世纪学界至少有四派,各派背后是不同政治 + 哲学立场。读历史书时知道作者在哪派,比记住"正确答案"重要。

**1. 传统 Confucian 史观——道统传承中心**

代表学者:司马光 (1019-1086)《资治通鉴》、朱熹 (1130-1200)《资治通鉴纲目》、20 世纪钱穆 (1895-1990)《国史大纲》。

主张:Tang-Song 是中华文明巅峰。唐诗 + 宋词 + 程朱理学 + 科举制度 = 中国"道统"在这 700 年成形 + 后世延续到清。文天祥 (1236-1283)、岳飞 (1103-1142)、苏轼 (1037-1101) 是道统人物。崖山 1279 后中华文明虽未消失,但巅峰已过。

优点:重视思想 + 文化连续性 + 个体道德选择。

偏见:民族主义滤镜 + 抗拒外来批判 + 倾向把历史写成"圣人传记"而非社会过程。钱穆《国史大纲》1940 年代抗战写就,有强烈民族情绪。

**2. Marxist 史观——封建社会 + 农民起义**

代表学者:翦伯赞 (1898-1968)、范文澜 (1893-1969)、郭沫若 (1892-1978)。50s-80s 中国大陆教科书主导。

主张:Tang-Song 是封建社会一个阶段。推动历史的不是皇帝 + 文人,而是农民起义:黄巢 875-884 (Tang 末)、方腊 1120 (北宋末)、红巾军 1351-1368 (元末)。地主阶级 + 农民阶级矛盾是基本动力。王安石变法 1069-1085 是地主阶级内部"改良派 vs 守旧派"斗争。

优点:首次重视底层社会 + 经济结构 + 起义记录。

偏见:阶级斗争还原论 + 忽视文化精英 + 把所有现象塞进"五种生产方式"框架 (原始 / 奴隶 / 封建 / 资本 / 社会主义)。文学价值常被贬为"统治阶级麻醉工具"。

**3. 西方 modernist 史观——Needham Question**

代表学者:Joseph Needham (1900-1995)《Science and Civilisation in China》多卷本、John K. Fairbank (1907-1991)、Mark Elvin《The Pattern of the Chinese Past》(1973)。

主张:Tang-Song 12-13 世纪在科技 + 经济 + 国家能力上领先世界。北宋 GDP 估占当时世界 30% 左右 (Angus Maddison 估算)。火药 + 印刷 + 罗盘 + 纸钞都在这期间发明。Joseph Needham Question:为什么中国领先 800 年后,科学革命没在中国发生而在 17 世纪欧洲?Mark Elvin 提出"高水平均衡陷阱"假说——中国传统农业太成功,反而锁住了进一步突破的动力。

优点:严谨比较 + 量化证据 + 把中国放进世界史。

偏见:现代化 framework 隐含"欧洲为标准"。Joseph Needham 后期被批太线性 + 太"工业革命中心论"。

**4. 21 世纪 globalist 史观——Eurasian network**

代表学者:Peter Bol《This Culture of Ours》(1992) + 《Neo-Confucianism in History》(2008)、余英时 (1930-2021)《士与中国文化》(2003)、Robert Hymes《Statesmen and Gentlemen》(1986)。

主张:Tang-Song 不是孤立"中国 example",而是 Eurasian 大网络一极。丝绸之路 + 海上贸易 + 元朝 1271-1368 把中国 + 中亚 + 中东 + 欧洲连成一张网。Peter Bol 重视宋代士大夫"localist turn"——精英从依附朝廷转向地方社会。余英时重视宋明理学是"内向超越"——中国哲学的内部突破,不是欧洲外来刺激。

优点:反 essentialism + 重视 cross-cultural mobility + 把中国当世界一部分。

偏见:有时过度解构"中国"概念 + 削弱内部连续性。

**怎么用四派**

读 Tang-Song 史的实用建议:钱穆 / 司马光看思想。翦伯赞 / 范文澜看农民起义。Needham / Elvin 看科技 + 经济。Peter Bol / 余英时看 globalist + 内部哲学。一派的盲点是另一派的视野。

### EN

Studying Tang-Song China has no single answer. There are at least four schools in 21st-century scholarship, each with its own political and philosophical stance. When reading a Tang-Song book, knowing which school the author belongs to matters more than memorizing a "correct answer."

**1. Traditional Confucian — Center of the Way**

Key scholars: Sima Guang (1019-1086) *Comprehensive Mirror in Aid of Governance*, Zhu Xi (1130-1200) *Outline and Details of the Comprehensive Mirror*, and 20th-century Qian Mu (1895-1990) *Outline of National History*.

Claim: Tang-Song is the peak of Chinese civilization. Tang poetry, Song lyrics, Cheng-Zhu Neo-Confucianism, and the imperial examination took shape across 700 years and the orthodox lineage continued through the Qing dynasty. Wen Tianxiang (1236-1283), Yue Fei (1103-1142), and Su Shi (1037-1101) are figures in this lineage. After Yashan in 1279, Chinese civilization did not vanish, but its peak had passed.

Strengths: emphasis on thought, cultural continuity, individual moral choice.

Bias: a nationalist filter, resistance to outside critique, tendency to write history as "biographies of sages" rather than as social process. Qian Mu wrote during the 1940s wartime period and the work carries strong nationalist emotion.

**2. Marxist — Feudal Society and Peasant Uprisings**

Key scholars: Jian Bozan (1898-1968), Fan Wenlan (1893-1969), Guo Moruo (1892-1978). Dominant in mainland Chinese textbooks from the 1950s to the 1980s.

Claim: Tang-Song is a stage of feudal society. The drivers of history are not emperors and literati but peasant uprisings: Huang Chao 875-884 (late Tang), Fang La 1120 (late Northern Song), Red Turban 1351-1368 (late Yuan). The landlord-peasant contradiction is the basic engine. Wang Anshi's reforms 1069-1085 were a struggle within the landlord class between "reformers and conservatives."

Strengths: first to take seriously bottom-of-society conditions, economic structure, and uprising records.

Bias: class-struggle reductionism, neglect of cultural elites, forcing every phenomenon into the "five modes of production" frame (primitive, slave, feudal, capitalist, socialist). Literary value is often dismissed as "anesthesia for the ruling class."

**3. Western modernist — the Needham Question**

Key scholars: Joseph Needham (1900-1995) multi-volume *Science and Civilisation in China*, John K. Fairbank (1907-1991), Mark Elvin *The Pattern of the Chinese Past* (1973).

Claim: in the 12th-13th century, Tang-Song China led the world in technology, economy, and state capacity. Northern Song GDP is estimated at roughly 30 percent of world GDP at the time (Angus Maddison's estimate). Gunpowder, printing, the compass, and paper money were all invented in this period. The Needham Question: after leading for 800 years, why did the scientific revolution not happen in China but in 17th-century Europe? Mark Elvin proposed a "high-level equilibrium trap" hypothesis: Chinese traditional agriculture was so successful that it locked away the incentive for further breakthroughs.

Strengths: rigorous comparison, quantitative evidence, places China inside world history.

Bias: the modernization framework implicitly sets Europe as the standard. Late Needham has been criticized as too linear and too centered on the Industrial Revolution.

**4. 21st-century globalist — Eurasian network**

Key scholars: Peter Bol *This Culture of Ours* (1992) and *Neo-Confucianism in History* (2008), Yu Ying-shih (1930-2021) *Scholar-Officials and Chinese Culture* (2003), Robert Hymes *Statesmen and Gentlemen* (1986).

Claim: Tang-Song is not an isolated "China example" but one node in a Eurasian network. The Silk Road, maritime trade, and the Yuan dynasty 1271-1368 wove China, Central Asia, the Middle East, and Europe into one web. Peter Bol emphasizes a Song-era "localist turn" by literati, who shifted from dependence on the court toward local society. Yu Ying-shih treats Song-Ming Neo-Confucianism as an "inward transcendence," an internal breakthrough in Chinese philosophy rather than a response to a European stimulus.

Strengths: anti-essentialism, attention to cross-cultural mobility, places China as part of the world.

Bias: sometimes over-deconstructs the concept of "China" and weakens internal continuity.

**How to use the four schools**

Practical advice for reading Tang-Song history: read Qian Mu and Sima Guang for thought. Read Jian Bozan and Fan Wenlan for peasant uprisings. Read Needham and Elvin for technology and economy. Read Peter Bol and Yu Ying-shih for the global frame and internal philosophy. One school's blind spot is another school's field of view.

## §10 八条反 Whig 钢筋 / Eight Counter-Whig Reinforcements

### CN

Whig 史观 = "历史是单向进步,过去是为现在铺路"。下面 8 条挑战常见的简化叙事。

**1.「唐朝最强」**

通俗叙事说唐朝 (618-907) 是中国"最强"。证据:唐疆域峰值约 5,000,000 km²、长安百万人口、丝绸之路繁盛。

复杂化:5,000,000 km² 是约 660 年高宗时期峰值,755 安史之乱后唐快速衰退,藩镇林立到 907。如果用 GDP 估算,北宋 (960-1127) 占当时世界 30% (Angus Maddison) 反而是真"最强"——只是版图更小 + 军事不向北扩张。

**2.「宋朝弱」**

通俗叙事说宋朝重文轻武 = 弱。证据:1127 靖康之变 + 1279 崖山。

复杂化:重文轻武 = 内部稳定。两宋 320 年没有大规模内乱,常备军 100 万左右 + 武器技术领先 (火药 + 神臂弓 + 投石机)。宋的战略选择是"买和平"——岁币换边境稳定 + 集中资源发展经济。这不是"弱",是不同的国家战略。把它叫"弱"是用现代民族国家眼光评判 medieval 帝国。

**3.「崖山之后无中华」**

钱穆 1942 抗战时期提出的命题。意思:崖山 1279 宋元交替后,中华文明本质消失,清末民初要"重建"。

复杂化:21 世纪学界主流 (Peter Bol、余英时) 认为这是 essentialism + 民族情绪。中华文明在元 (1271-1368)、明 (1368-1644)、清 (1644-1912) 继续演变 + 改造。元朝杂剧 + 明清小说 + 王阳明心学 + 顾炎武实学,都是"崖山之后"的中华文明。1942 命题是 wartime emotion,不是历史判断。

**4.「四大发明炫耀」**

中国教科书强调火药 + 印刷 + 罗盘 + 造纸 (有时加纸钞) 是中国对世界文明的贡献。

复杂化:四大发明确实在唐宋发明 + 完善。但传到欧洲 + 改变历史 (军事革命 + 大航海 + 科学革命) 主要是 1300-1500 年。中国本身在唐宋发明这些,却没有用它们改变中国 (火药主要做烟花 + 防御武器、印刷主要印佛经 + 科举书,没有引爆类似欧洲宗教改革的传播革命)。这就是 Joseph Needham Question——"为什么发明在中国,革命在欧洲"。

**5.「武则天篡夺」**

传统史观骂武则天 (624-705,在位 690-705) 篡唐 + 杀子 + 用酷吏。

复杂化:21 世纪学界重新评价。武则天是中国历史唯一称帝女性。她推行科举改革 (扩大殿试 + 702 长安二年创武举——跟科举 605 同期分轨,后中断,1102 宋徽宗朝再制度化)、打击门阀士族 + 提拔寒门、农业政策稳定。"篡夺"是从男性继承制角度看的。21 世纪从制度史 + 性别史角度看,武则天是 Tang 制度建设关键人物。

**6.「王安石变法失败」**

通俗叙事说王安石 (1021-1086) 变法 1069-1085 被反复废除立 + 北宋变法党与守旧党斗争消耗了国力 + 1127 靖康崩盘是变法失败的结果。

复杂化:21 世纪学界 (Peter Bol、漆侠) 认为变法是宋代国家能力建设关键。青苗法 + 募役法 + 保甲法 + 市易法等是宋代向"现代国家"方向的尝试。1127 崩盘的直接原因是徽宗 1100-1126 末期联金抗辽政策错误,不能简单归因于变法。"变法失败论"是司马光《资治通鉴》视角,不是唯一视角。

**7.「岳飞被秦桧害死」**

通俗叙事:岳飞 (1103-1142) 抗金英雄,被秦桧 (1090-1155) 在 1142 风波亭以"莫须有"罪名害死。

复杂化:21 世纪学界 (王曾瑜、邓广铭) 把这复杂化。真决策者是高宗赵构 (1107-1187,在位 1127-1162),秦桧是执行者,不是首谋。高宗的政治判断:岳飞 1140 北伐打到朱仙镇,如果继续打回北宋故都汴京,高宗自己的合法性会被质疑 (因为父亲徽宗 + 哥哥钦宗还在金朝活着,迎回后高宗就要让位)。岳飞坚持北伐 + 拒绝接受"议和"军令,从政治判断角度有自己的问题。这不是为秦桧洗地,但避免把它简化成"忠 vs 奸"的脸谱戏。

**8.「中国一直是中央集权」**

通俗印象:中国从秦 (前 221) 到清 (1912) 一直是中央集权大一统。

复杂化:Tang-Song 期间不是。安史之乱 755 后,Tang 731-907 进入藩镇时代,节度使割据 + 中央失控。907-960 五代十国是分裂期。北宋 960-1127 重建中央集权但仅一段时间。1127 靖康后南北分裂,1127-1279 南宋只控制淮河以南。Tang-Song 700 年里"统一"+ "分裂"交替,不是单一 monolith。

### EN

The Whig view says history is one-directional progress and the past was paving the way for the present. Eight reinforcements below challenge common simplifications.

**1. "Tang was the strongest"**

The popular narrative says Tang (618-907) was China's "strongest" dynasty, citing territory peaking near 5 million km², Chang'an's million-person population, and the busy Silk Road.

Complication: 5 million km² was the peak around 660 under Emperor Gaozong. After the An Lushan Rebellion in 755, Tang declined quickly, with regional military commands fragmenting authority until 907. By GDP estimates, Northern Song (960-1127) at roughly 30 percent of world GDP (Angus Maddison) is the real "strongest" period, with smaller territory but a different strategy.

**2. "Song was weak"**

The popular narrative says Song privileged civil over military and was therefore weak. Evidence cited: the 1127 Jingkang collapse and 1279 Yashan.

Complication: privileging civil over military meant internal stability. Song's 320 years saw no large-scale civil wars. The standing army was around one million with leading weapons technology (gunpowder, *shenbi* crossbow, trebuchets). Song's strategic choice was to buy peace, paying annual tribute for stable borders and concentrating resources on the economy. This is not weakness; it is a different state strategy. Calling it weak applies a modern nation-state lens to a medieval empire.

**3. "After Yashan, no more China"**

A thesis Qian Mu put forward in 1942 during the war: after the 1279 Yashan transition from Song to Yuan, the essence of Chinese civilization vanished, and the late Qing and early Republic period had to "rebuild" it.

Complication: the 21st-century mainstream (Peter Bol, Yu Ying-shih) sees this as essentialism plus nationalist feeling. Chinese civilization continued and transformed through Yuan (1271-1368), Ming (1368-1644), and Qing (1644-1912). Yuan zaju drama, Ming-Qing novels, Wang Yangming's School of Mind, and Gu Yanwu's substantive learning are all post-Yashan Chinese civilization. The 1942 thesis is wartime emotion, not historical judgment.

**4. "The Four Great Inventions" boast**

Chinese textbooks emphasize gunpowder, printing, the compass, and papermaking (sometimes plus paper money) as China's contribution to world civilization.

Complication: the four were indeed invented and refined in Tang-Song. But their transmission to Europe and the historical changes they caused (military revolution, age of exploration, scientific revolution) mostly fell between 1300 and 1500. China itself invented these but did not use them to transform China. Gunpowder was used mainly for fireworks and defensive weapons; printing was used mainly for Buddhist scriptures and exam books, without triggering anything like the European Reformation's print revolution. This is the Needham Question: "Why was the invention in China and the revolution in Europe?"

**5. "Wu Zetian usurped"**

The traditional view condemns Wu Zetian (624-705, ruled 690-705) as a usurper of Tang who killed sons and used cruel officials.

Complication: 21st-century scholarship reassesses her. Wu is the only woman to have declared herself emperor of China. She extended the imperial examination (expanding the palace exam; she also created the military examination in 702 — a parallel track to the civil exam founded by Sui in 605 — that lapsed and was re-formalized under Huizong's Northern Song court in 1102), curbed aristocratic clans and promoted men of humble origin, and stabilized agricultural policy. The "usurpation" frame comes from the male-succession lens. From institutional and gender history perspectives, Wu is a key figure in Tang state-building.

**6. "Wang Anshi's reforms failed"**

The popular narrative says Wang Anshi's (1021-1086) reforms 1069-1085 were repeatedly abolished and reinstated, and that the reformer-conservative struggle drained Northern Song so the 1127 Jingkang collapse was the result.

Complication: 21st-century scholarship (Peter Bol, Qi Xia) treats the reforms as central to Song state-capacity building. The Green Sprouts policy, hired-service system, *baojia* mutual-responsibility system, and state trade policy were attempts to move toward a "more modern state." The 1127 collapse was directly caused by Emperor Huizong's late-period (1100-1126) failed alliance-with-Jin policy against Liao, not simply by reform. The "failure" reading is Sima Guang's *Comprehensive Mirror* perspective; it is one perspective, not the only one.

**7. "Yue Fei was killed by Qin Hui"**

The popular narrative: Yue Fei (1103-1142), an anti-Jin hero, was killed in 1142 at Wind Wave Pavilion by Qin Hui (1090-1155) under the trumped-up charge of "perhaps so."

Complication: 21st-century scholarship (Wang Zengyu, Deng Guangming) complicates this. The real decision-maker was Emperor Gaozong, Zhao Gou (1107-1187, ruled 1127-1162). Qin Hui was the executor, not the prime mover. Gaozong's political calculation: Yue Fei's 1140 northern campaign reached Zhuxianzhen. If he kept advancing back to the old Northern Song capital Kaifeng, Gaozong's own legitimacy would be questioned, since his father Emperor Huizong and brother Emperor Qinzong were still alive in Jin captivity, and on their return Gaozong would have to abdicate. Yue Fei's insistence on northward war and refusal to accept the peace orders also reflected his own political miscalculation. This is not a defense of Qin Hui, but it avoids reducing the story to a costume drama of "loyal hero versus treacherous minister."

**8. "China was always centralized"**

Popular impression: from Qin (221 BCE) to Qing (1912), China was always a centralized unified empire.

Complication: not during Tang-Song. After the An Lushan Rebellion in 755, Tang from 731 to 907 entered the era of military commissions, with regional military governors in effective autonomy and the center losing control. The 907-960 Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms were a fragmentation period. Northern Song 960-1127 rebuilt central control, but only for one stretch. After 1127 Jingkang, north and south split: Southern Song 1127-1279 controlled only south of the Huai River. Across the 700 years of Tang-Song, "unity" and "fragmentation" alternated. There is no single monolith.

## §11 不下结论思考问题 / Open Discussion Questions

### CN

下面 6 个问题没有标准答案。和 {{userParentWord|爸妈}} 聊一次,记录两个人观点不同的地方,不必达成一致。

这个 1942 命题说中华文明在 1279 后本质消失。

**Q4. 苏轼三度流放——你的 21 世纪 pattern**

苏轼 (1037-1101) 一生三次流放:1080 黄州 + 1094 惠州 + 1097 海南。每次流放,他写出更好的诗词 + 食物发明 (东坡肉) + 更深的人生哲学。

问题:21 世纪你身上有没有这种 pattern?例子——内卷 + 跳槽 + 创业失败 + 移民 + 独立思考者被排挤。被"流放" (失业 / 失恋 / 离家 / 不被理解) 反而成就了你某部分?

**Q5. 李清照的婚姻——12 世纪 vs 21 世纪**

李清照 (1084-约 1155) 18 岁嫁赵明诚 + 28 年金石录共著。1129 赵明诚死。1132 李清照 49 岁再婚张汝舟——这场婚姻只持续 100 天 (推断为骗婚 + 想吞金石录)。她检举诉讼,按宋律"妻告夫"即使胜诉也要坐牢——靠翰林学士綦崇礼营救,她最终坐了 9 天牢出狱。此后终生整理金石录。
